# The three-reviewer review of 2026-09-03

Three independent agents were given the same evidence pack (`MANIFEST.md`,
the measurements beside it, and the repository at commit b9414c2e) with NO
framing of what anyone believed was wrong, and asked the same open question:
what is actually wrong with how the pipeline gets a song from a seed to exit
0, and what should be done. Each wrote a position paper from first principles
(`round1_*.md`), then read the other two and answered every disputed claim
with a citation (`round2_*.md`), then converged on a final statement marking
each item CONSENSUS or CONTESTED (`round3_*.md`). The coordinator took no
part in the debate and did not summarise between rounds.

Banked verbatim, because a record of how a decision was reached is part of
the decision (doctrine 14). The consensus items became M-215, M-216 and
M-217 and the M-205 ledger entry the same day; the contested items are listed
in M-216 as open, with the measurement each reviewer named as settling it.

`timing.txt` carries two CORRECTION notes: two of its rows measured the wrong
file and are struck in place rather than deleted (doctrine 17).
