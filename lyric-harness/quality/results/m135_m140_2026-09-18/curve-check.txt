THIS RECEIPT SUPERSEDES THE FIRST ONE BANKED THE SAME DAY, and the reason is
worth more than the numbers. The first rebuild was paid against fingerprint
31a1ab473ff4, and the pin was moved onto it. CI then refused this branch under
`test_verbs.py` §12, which holds `lyric_harness.py` to EXACTLY ONE broad
exception handler — the declared span-lookup fallback. The M-140 work had added
two more. Both guarded conditions that cannot occur, so removing them was the
correct repair and it changed no behaviour; but `lyric_harness.py` is input 1
of seven to `comparator_fingerprint()`, WHOLE FILE, and the removal moved the
comparator a second time, to 7687581e91a2, discarding the memo the first
rebuild had just finished producing.

THE COST IS THE FINDING. A correction to a comparator-batch PR pays the whole
memo again — this is the batching rule's own logic applied to a branch rather
than across branches, and nothing in the tree said so before this sitting. The
first receipt is not deleted for being wrong about its arithmetic; it is
superseded because the tree moved underneath it.
