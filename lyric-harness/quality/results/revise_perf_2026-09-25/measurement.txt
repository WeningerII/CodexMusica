WHY THE ANSWER CANNOT MOVE. `_end_pair_schemas` returns
`found & set(wvp[(1, 2)])`: it reads the judge's verdict only for names in
`found`. `whole_vocabulary_pairs` judges each schema by its own
`line_pairs_for(REGISTRY[name], stream, ...)` call over one stream built from
its arguments; nothing one call writes is read by another's verdict (the
per-pair memo is keyed per schema, and it records a pure verdict). Asking a
subset therefore gives each asked schema the answer the full call gives it.
The judge's memo key carries the subset, so a restricted answer is never
served to a full caller.
