I counted every door along the block
I counted every window left unlit
I traced the wire from the pole to the roof
and found the same knot tied at either end
I kept the ledger open on the table
I kept the numbers honest as I could
the tally climbed and nothing balanced out
a column of small debts with no due date
I walked the perimeter before the rain
I walked it twice to make the count agree
the fence line held, the gate had learned to stick
a hinge that only answers when it's pushed
I read the meter standing in the cold
I read it wrong the first two times I tried
the needle sat between two honest numbers
and neither one of them would call it settled
no line, no bar, no fixed refrain to lean on
a shape that will not hold a single name
I filed the difference under things postponed
I filed it twice, in case the first one drifted
the drawer stuck shut on everything we owed
a debt so small it never earned a letter
I set the ledger down before the light changed
I set it down and let the number stand
the count goes on without a final sum
the count goes on, and no one asked it to
