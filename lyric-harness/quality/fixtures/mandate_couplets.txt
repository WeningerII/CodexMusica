the wire runs long past every door
the wire runs on and finds one more
we kept the count as we would know
so say the highway, say it slow
the ledger opened on the floor
and every debt was kept in store
the numbers climbed against the wall
and no one living heard them fall
a hinge that only answers pushed
a silence better left untouched
the gate stood open to the yard
the count was easy, counting hard
we walked the fence line twice a year
to see the same repairs appear
the meter read a steady hum
a number nobody would sum
the drawer stuck shut on what we owed
a debt so small and yet it showed
the light along the wire held true
the count went on the whole night through
a shape that would not hold a name
was counted anyway, the same
the tally climbed and would not rest
and every column failed the test
we set the ledger down at last
and let the number join the past
the count goes on and lets it go
so say the highway, say it slow
