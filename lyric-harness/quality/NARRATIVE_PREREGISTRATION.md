# The narrative-layer harm check — preregistration

**REGISTERED 2026-08-25, BEFORE ANY LAYER CODE EXISTS.** No table has been
adopted, no brief line built, no draft written under the layer this
document tests. That ordering is the point: the definition of *worse*
below is fixed while nobody knows the answer, so no result can be
reframed after the fact. Status: **REGISTERED — no results**. Results, when
they come, are appended below the marked line and nothing above it moves.

## 1 · The question

Does adding declared narrative obligations to the writer's brief make the
delivered writing WORSE, as read by the blind panel — and does it reach
the defect it exists for? One instrument, two pre-declared readings:

- **H1 (the layer reaches the defect).** The M-112 signature — a
  majority-or-unanimous panel rejection of the song's maximally BOUND
  section, with quotes naming sound-over-sense — has reproduced on
  **4 of 4** measured songs (`quality/RESULTS_PANEL.md` runs 2–5, the
  last unanimous). If the layer works, that signature stops reproducing
  on narrative-era drafts. If run N on a narrative-era song shows it
  again, H1 is falsified for that song and the count is kept.
- **H2 (the layer harms).** The likeliest failure mode is the layer's own
  M-112: obligations breed compliance-noise, and the narrative version of
  "the etch" is a line that services a dependency instead of a scene —
  signposting prose. WORSE is declared as either: (a) the narrative twin
  collects MORE total section-REJECT ballots than its bare twin in a
  majority of pairs, or (b) any unanimous section rejection of a
  narrative twin whose quotes name explaining/signposting register.

**No BETTER condition is declared, deliberately.** Doctrine 7: this
harness enforces floors and does not certify improvement. The experiment
exists to detect harm with a named rollback, and to give H1 a chance to
fail in public.

## 2 · The design — paired blind drafts

- **Unit: the pair.** One seed, two drafts. The BARE twin is written
  against the plan exactly as the planner emits it today (drawn
  relations, all current gates). The NARRATIVE twin is written against
  the identical plan plus the narrative brief. Both twins must reach
  `song` exit 0 and `revise` SUCCESS through the identical gate set
  before the panel sees either; a twin that cannot is recorded, not
  replaced in silence.
- **Ordering rule: the bare twin is written FIRST, every time.** The
  writer cannot unknow a story once the layer has stated one, so the
  contamination is put where it biases AGAINST the hypothesis of harm
  least: the bare twin is drafted before the narrative brief for that
  seed is ever generated or read.
- **Seeds: a declared procession, no shopping.** The first three integers
  ≥ 32 whose `make_plan` emits without refusal, taken in order. If a
  seed's shape supports NO consistent story line-up (the silhouette set
  is empty), the pair is recorded REFUSED-BY-LAYER and the procession
  continues to the next integer — the refusal count is itself a result
  and is reported, never hidden (doctrine 79). Three counts, never
  summed: pairs completed, pairs refused-by-layer, pairs refused-by-gate.
- **At least 3 completed pairs before any verdict** (doctrine 73: one
  song is a coin flip reported as a verdict). More pairs may be added;
  the verdict may not be read early.
- **Panel per the standing protocol** (`quality/RESULTS_PANEL.md` §1):
  independent ballots, zero cross-talk, section as the unit, every ballot
  quoting its section, quoteless verdicts discarded, shuffled blind
  labels with the key committed before launch, twins never adjacent and
  never first or last, majority per unit, disagreement kept as data. The
  judges are NOT told which drafts are twins, that twins exist, or that
  any layer is under test.

## 3 · Instruments, and what may gate

- **The panel is the semantic instrument and nothing gates on it** — a
  panel verdict is model output at nonzero temperature (the standing rule
  of `RESULTS_PANEL.md`). The verdict of THIS experiment is read from
  panel output under the pre-declared definitions above, by a person.
- **The feature register is the deterministic control.**
  `songs/RESULTS.tsv` rows for every twin: rhyme predictability,
  concreteness, MATTR, function-word ratio watched for regression on the
  axes the layer does not target. Directions disclosed; no new threshold
  is invented here (doctrine 58 — a number nobody calibrated is a number
  nobody may enforce).
- **The rollback commitment.** If WORSE per §1·H2, the layer's brief
  wiring is withdrawn in the same sitting the verdict is read; the
  vocabulary tables may remain as disclosure. The rollback is part of the
  registration so that keeping the layer never becomes the default
  outcome of inertia.

## 4 · Confounders, declared now

1. **One writer writes both twins.** Unavoidable here; the ordering rule
   above is the mitigation, and it is partial.
2. **The bare twin is not a pre-era draft.** It post-dates the layer's
   design and the writer has read these documents. The comparison is
   "with and without the brief," not "before and after the idea."
3. **Panel non-reproducibility.** Verdicts do not re-derive
   byte-identical; the mitigation is the pre-declared reading, the pair
   count floor, and keeping disagreement as data.
4. **Shape is controlled exactly (same seed), words are not** — a twin
   difference is the brief plus writer variance, and nothing here can
   split those two. Stated so the result is never quoted as more than it
   is.

## 5 · What this experiment does not touch

Nothing samples corpus stories (move 37). Nothing scores meaning
(doctrine 6). Nothing reaches the recipe engine (standing rule 1). The
banked songs are the failure log, not the exemplar set — no instrument in
this experiment reads them as calibration (doctrine 13/14).
