## A · The atom vocabulary — eight, closed

The test for membership is M-54's, transposed: *violate it — does the
section become a NOVEL song move, or a MISLABELLED one?* An atom earns a
row only if a section carrying the wrong one is mislabelled.

| atom | one sentence | the violate-it reading |
|---|---|---|
| ESTABLISH | a world, cast, or situation is put in place | a song may open anywhere — but a section CLAIMING establish that only decorates is mislabelled |
| COMPLICATE | declared pressure enters; the situation is disturbed | tension the words never carry is an arrangement fact, not this atom |
| TURN | the reading flips; what was true is re-lit | a turn nothing precedes has nothing to flip — needs an inbound BUT by definition |
| DWELL | the moment is held and intensified without advancing | dwelling is not failure to advance; it is advancing REFUSED on purpose |
| ANCHOR | the invariant claim — the fixed point the song returns to | an anchor that varies its words is a verse wearing a chorus mark |
| JUDGE | a compressed verdict on what has happened | judgment before anything has happened is an epigraph, not a section |
| RESOLVE | the standing pressure is cashed or settled | resolution with no prior COMPLICATE resolves nothing — vacuous, refused |
| DEPART | leave-taking; aftermath; the walk home | departure is not resolution — a song may leave with everything unsettled |

## C · The junction grammar — six edges between adjacent sections

Lineage: the PDTB top-level sense classes, which the discourse-relations
literature has already ground fine; the set is DECLARED here, not
imported, and it is closed.

| junction | reading | may enter | conventions (notes, never gates) |
|---|---|---|---|
| THEREFORE | this section because of the last | any atom except TURN | |
| BUT | this section against the last | TURN, ANCHOR, RESOLVE, DEPART | ~~a TURN may ONLY be entered by BUT — the one hard edge rule proposed~~ SOFTENED by ruling (§F·4): TURN enters by BUT or JUXTAPOSE — the *ten* witness |
| AND-THEN | mere succession | any | legal and conventionally limited — the writers-room ban is a CONVENTION (doctrine 6); a chain of them is what the panel calls an inventory |
| MEANWHILE | elsewhere, same time | ESTABLISH, DWELL | |
| ELABORATE | deeper into the same moment | DWELL (natural home), JUDGE | |
| JUXTAPOSE | set beside, connection unstated | any | a REAL junction with a master tradition (renga's linked verse); parataxis stays declarable, never banned |

## D · The returning-section rule — one card, many edges

A returning function's instances share ONE atom (the card's face is
fixed, exactly as the return machinery fixes its words) and each instance
takes its OWN inbound junction. That per-instance edge list is the
**trajectory**, and it is where every known chorus device lives as a
parsable coordinate: reframe (THEREFORE, THEREFORE, BUT), mantra
(AND-THEN held flat while the verses move), anchor-as-home (the verses'
distance from it), the earned last chorus (the return ladder's one-word
variation plus a BUT inbound). Decidable corollary, already stated in
conversation and kept: a trajectory containing BUT requires intervening
material between those instances — back-to-back returns cannot reframe,
and that is checkable from the shape alone.

## E · What a story line-up IS, and what falls out

A line-up = one atom per sung section (each drawn from its row in §B)
plus one junction per adjacent seam (legal under §C), with returning
functions bound by §D. The set of line-ups a SHAPE admits is a pure
function of the emitted plan — no words — which yields, for free once
these tables are adopted: the plan-time silhouette match, the refusal
when the set is empty, the `--sweep` narrative predicate (the seed
filter), and the collapse (drawn uniform over the admissible set, or
declared by the writer, declared-silences-drawn — M-117's precedence
whole). Enforcement is NOT in this document's scope: the split between
gate, disclosure, and panel is step 5's, after calibration.

## F · Questions for the red pen — RULED 2026-08-25 under the owner's delegation

1. The eight atoms — right list? Is JUDGE real or a species of DWELL?
   **RULED: the eight stand, JUDGE is real.** The external witness is
   Labov's natural-narrative schema, where EVALUATION is the component
   that separates a story from a report — and it maps onto this table
   nearly one-to-one (orientation→ESTABLISH, complicating
   action→COMPLICATE, evaluation→JUDGE, result→RESOLVE, coda→DEPART).
   A section can dwell without judging and judge without dwelling; the
   banked one-line verse of `crooked_waltz.txt` judges and dwells on
   nothing.
2. chorus = ANCHOR alone, or admit DWELL?
   **RULED: ANCHOR alone.** The atoms name STRUCTURAL roles, not
   content registers — a purely sensory chorus is still the fixed
   point the song returns to — and two faces indistinguishable without
   reading meaning must be one face (the same parsimony that keeps
   doctrine 6 out of this layer).
3. The drop / breakdown / reprise hesitations.
   **RULED: drop = ANCHOR alone** (the "arrival" quality lives in the
   inbound edge, not a second face; no new edge rule — the build→drop
   THEREFORE stays prose); **breakdown = DWELL stands** (the gloss is
   an arrangement fact; a writer wanting the quiet reveal has the
   bridge); **reprise = ANCHOR with no junction mandate** — the
   "changed" half is already graded by grid's reprise machinery, and a
   second spelling of it here would be doctrine 1's own defect.
4. TURN-only-by-BUT — keep or soften?
   **RULED: SOFTENED to {BUT, JUXTAPOSE}, and the draft was wrong.**
   Kishōtenketsu's *ten* is a turn entered by juxtaposition, not
   opposition — a master tradition, and the hard rule would have
   structurally banned that family, which is doctrine 24's forbidden
   move (a rule that deletes a category must relabel instead).
   THEREFORE and AND-THEN stay excluded from TURN: a turn caused by
   the previous section is consequence, and "and then everything
   changed" is the unearned reversal.
5. Default collapse: drawn or writer-declared?
   **RULED: drawn, declared-silences-drawn** — M-117's precedence
   pattern, the owner's own standing ruling one coordinate over.
   Entropy consumed after every existing draw, so seed shapes and
   relation draws stay byte-identical.
