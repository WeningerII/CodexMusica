--- TITLE: Great Shepherd of thy people, hear [item: PG20476-27]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0027
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
Great Shepherd of thy people, hear;
Thy presence now display;
We kneel within thy house of prayer;
O, give us hearts to pray.

[VERSE 2]
The clouds which veil thee from our sight,
In pity, Lord, remove;
Dispose our minds to hear aright
The message of thy love.

[VERSE 3]
Help us, with holy fear and joy,
To kneel before thy face;
O, make us, creatures of thy power,
The children of thy grace.

--- TITLE: How welcome to the soul, when pressed [item: PG20476-49]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0049
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
How welcome to the soul, when pressed
With six days' noise, and care, and toil,
Is the returning day of rest,
Which hides us from the world awhile!

[VERSE 2]
How happy they, whose lot is cast
Where Christ invites the "weary" yet;
They find their sorrows quickly past,
And all their burdens soon forget.

[VERSE 3]
Though pinched with poverty at home,
With sharp afflictions daily fed,
It makes amends, if they can come
To God's own house for heavenly bread.

[VERSE 4]
We thank thee for thy day, O Lord!
And here thy promised presence seek;
Open thy hand with blessings stored,
And give us manna for the week.

--- TITLE: Zeal is that pure and heavenly flame [item: PG20476-401]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0401
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
Zeal is that pure and heavenly flame
The fire of love supplies;
Whilst that which often bears the name,
Is self but in disguise.

[VERSE 2]
True zeal is merciful and mild,
Can pity and forbear;
The false is headstrong, fierce and wild,
And breathes revenge and war.

[VERSE 3]
While zeal for truth the Christian warms,
He knows the worth of peace;
But self contends for names and forms,
Its party to increase.

[VERSE 4]
Zeal has attained its highest aim,
Its end is satisfied,
If sinners love the Saviour's name,--
Nor seeks it aught beside.

[VERSE 5]
This idol self, O Lord, dethrone,
And from our hearts remove;
And let no zeal by us be shown
But that which springs from love.

--- TITLE: How sweet the name of Jesus sounds [item: PG20476-436]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0436
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
How sweet the name of Jesus sounds
In a believer's ear!
It soothes his sorrows, heals his wounds,
And drives away his fear.

[VERSE 2]
It makes the wounded spirit whole,
It calms the troubled breast;
'T is manna to the hungry soul,
And, to the weary, rest.

[VERSE 3]
Weak is the effort of my heart,
And cold my warmest thought,
But when I see thee as thou art,
I'll praise thee as I ought.

[VERSE 4]
Till then I would thy love proclaim,
With every fleeting breath;
And may the music of thy name
Refresh my soul in death.

--- TITLE: 'T  is a point I long to know,-- [item: PG20476-454]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0454
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
'T  is a point I long to know,--
Oft it causes anxious thought,--
Do I love the Lord, or no?
Am I his, or am I not?

[VERSE 2]
If I love, why am I thus?
Why this dull and lifeless frame?
Hardly, sure, can they be worse,
Who have never heard his name.

[VERSE 3]
If I pray, or hear, or read,
Sin is mixed with all I do;
You that love the Lord indeed,
Tell me, is it thus with you?

[VERSE 4]
Yet I mourn my stubborn will,
Find my sin a grief and thrall;
Should I grieve for what I feel,
If I did not love at all?

[VERSE 5]
Father, let me love thee more,
If I love at all, I pray;
If I have not loved before,
Help me to begin to-day.

--- TITLE: A glance from heaven, with sweet effect [item: PG20476-526]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0526
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
A glance from heaven, with sweet effect,
Sometimes my pensive spirit cheers:
But ere I can my thoughts collect,
As suddenly it disappears.

[VERSE 2]
So lightning in the gloom of night
Affords a momentary day;
Disclosing objects full in sight,
Which, soon as seen, are snatched away.

[VERSE 3]
The lightning's flash did not create
The opening prospect it revealed;
But only showed the real state
Of what the darkness had concealed.

[VERSE 4]
Just so, we by a glimpse discern
The glorious things within the veil;
That, when in darkness, we may learn
To live by faith, till light prevail.

--- TITLE: Now, gracious Lord, thine arm reveal [item: PG20476-892]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0892
--- AUTHOR: Newton

[VERSE 1]
Now, gracious Lord, thine arm reveal,
And make thy glory known;
Now let us all thy presence feel,
And soften hearts of stone.

[VERSE 2]
From all the guilt of former sin
May mercy set us free;
And let the year we now begin,
Begin and end with thee.

[VERSE 3]
Send down thy spirit from above,
That saints may love thee more,
And sinners now may learn to love,
Who never loved before.

[VERSE 4]
And when before thee we appear,
In our eternal home,
May growing numbers worship here,
And praise thee in our room.
