--- TITLE: For thee, O God, our constant praise [item: PG20476-33]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0033
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
For thee, O God, our constant praise
In Zion waits, thy chosen seat;
Our promised altars there we'll raise,
And all our zealous vows complete.

[VERSE 2]
O thou, who to my humble prayer
Didst always bend thy listening ear,
To thee shall all mankind repair,
And at thy gracious throne appear.

[VERSE 3]
Our sins, though numberless, in vain
To stop thy flowing mercy try;
For thou wilt cleanse the guilty stain,
And wash away the crimson dye.

[VERSE 4]
Blest is the man, who, near thee placed,
Within thy sacred dwelling lives;
Whilst we at humbler distance taste
The vast delight thy worship gives.

--- TITLE: Through endless years thou art the same [item: PG20476-127]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0127
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
Through endless years thou art the same,
O thou eternal God;
Each future age shall know thy name,
And tell thy works abroad.

[VERSE 2]
The strong foundations of the earth
Of old by thee were laid;
By thee the beauteous arch of heaven
With matchless skill was made.

[VERSE 3]
Soon may this goodly frame of things
Created by thy hand,
Be, like a vesture, laid aside,
And changed at thy command.

[VERSE 4]
But thy perfections, all divine,
Eternal as thy days,
Through everlasting ages shine,
With undiminished rays.

--- TITLE: O Thou, to whom all creatures bow [item: PG20476-134]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0134
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
O Thou, to whom all creatures bow
Within this earthly frame,
Through all the world how great art thou!
How glorious is thy name!

[VERSE 2]
When heaven, thy glorious work on high,
Employs my wondering sight,--
The moon, that nightly rules the sky,
With stars of feebler light,--

[VERSE 3]
Lord, what is man, that he is blessed
With thy peculiar care!
Why on his offspring is conferred
Of love so large a share?

[VERSE 4]
O Thou, to whom all creatures bow
Within this earthly frame,
Through all the world how great art thou!
How glorious is thy name!

--- TITLE: Thou, Lord, by strictest search hast known [item: PG20476-140]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0140
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
Thou, Lord, by strictest search hast known
My rising up and lying down;
My secret thoughts are known to thee,
Known long before conceived by me.

[VERSE 2]
O could I so perfidious be,
To think of once deserting thee!
Where, Lord, could I thy influence shun?
Or whither from thy presence run?

[VERSE 3]
If I the morning's wings could gain,
And fly beyond the western main,
Thy swifter hand would first arrive,
And there arrest thy fugitive.

[VERSE 4]
Or should I try to shun thy sight
Beneath the sable wings of night,
One glance from thee, one piercing ray,
Would kindle darkness into day.

[VERSE 5]
Search, try, O God, my thoughts and heart,
If mischief lurks in any part;
Correct me where I go astray,
And guide me in thy perfect way.

--- TITLE: Be thou, O God, exalted high [item: PG20476-149]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0149
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
Be thou, O God, exalted high;
And as thy glory fills the sky,
So let it be on earth displayed,
Till thou art here, as there, obeyed.

[VERSE 2]
O God, our hearts are fixed and bent
Their thankful tribute to present;
And, with the heart, the voice, we'll raise
To thee, our God, in songs of praise.

[VERSE 3]
Thy praises, Lord, we will resound
To all the listening nations round;
Thy mercy highest heaven transcends;
Thy truth beyond the clouds extends.

[VERSE 4]
Be thou, O God, exalted high;
And as thy glory fills the sky,
So let it be on earth displayed,
Till thou art here, as there, obeyed.

--- TITLE: Ye boundless realms of joy [item: PG20476-160]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0160
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
Ye boundless realms of joy,
Exalt your Maker's name;
His praise your songs employ
Above the starry frame:
Your voices raise,
Ye cherubim
And seraphim,
To sing his praise.

[VERSE 2]
Let all adore the Lord,
And praise his holy name,
By whose almighty word
They all from nothing came;
And all shall last,
From changes free;
His firm decree
Stands ever fast.

--- TITLE: God's perfect law converts the soul [item: PG20476-184]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0184
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
God's perfect law converts the soul,
Reclaims from false desires;
With sacred wisdom his sure word
The ignorant inspires.

[VERSE 2]
The statutes of the Lord are just,
And bring sincere delight;
His pure commands, in search of truth,
Assist the feeblest sight.

[VERSE 3]
His perfect worship here is fixed,
On sure foundations laid;
His equal laws are in the scales
Of truth and justice weighed.

[VERSE 4]
Of more esteem than golden mines,
Or gold refined with skill;
More sweet than honey, or the drops
That from the comb distil.

[VERSE 5]
My trusty counsellors they are,
And friendly warnings give;
Divine rewards attend on those,
Who by thy precepts live.

--- TITLE: Lord, who's the happy man, that may [item: PG20476-347]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0347
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
Lord, who's the happy man, that may
To thy blest courts repair,
Not, stranger-like, to visit them,
But to inhabit there?

[VERSE 2]
'Tis he whose every thought and deed
By rules of virtue moves;
Whose generous tongue disdains to speak
The thing his heart disproves;

[VERSE 3]
Who never did a slander forge,
His neighbor's fame to wound;
Nor hearken to a false report,
By malice whispered round;

[VERSE 4]
Who to his plighted vows and trust
Has ever firmly stood;
And though he promise to his loss,
He makes his promise good.

[VERSE 5]
The man who by this steady course
Has happiness ensured,
When earth's foundations shake, shall stand
By Providence secured.

--- TITLE: How blest is he, who ne'er consents [item: PG20476-409]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0409
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
How blest is he, who ne'er consents
By ill advice to walk;
Nor stands in sinners' ways, nor sits
Where men profanely talk:

[VERSE 2]
But makes the perfect law of God
His business and delight;
Devoutly reads therein by day,
And meditates by night.

[VERSE 3]
Like some fair tree, which, fed by streams,
With timely fruit does bend,
He still shall flourish, and success
All his designs attend.

[VERSE 4]
Ungodly men, and their attempts,
No lasting root shall find;
Untimely blasted, and dispersed
Like chaff before the wind.

--- TITLE: No change of times shall ever shock [item: PG20476-624]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0624
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady

[VERSE 1]
No change of times shall ever shock
My firm affection, Lord, to thee;
For thou hast always been a rock,
A fortress and defence to me.

[VERSE 2]
Thou my Deliverer art, my God;
My trust is in thy mighty power;
Thou art my shield from foes abroad,
At home my safeguard and my tower.

[VERSE 3]
To heaven I made my mournful prayer,
To God addressed my humble moan,
Who graciously inclined his ear,
And heard me from his lofty throne.

[VERSE 4]
Who, then, deserves to be adored,
But God, on whom my hopes depend?
Or who, except the mighty Lord,
Can with resistless power defend?
