--- TITLE: Behold! the Prince of Peace [item: PG20476-216]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0216
--- AUTHOR: Needham

[VERSE 1]
Behold! the Prince of Peace,
The chosen of the Lord,
God's well-beloved Son, fulfils
The sure prophetic word.

[VERSE 2]
No royal pomp adorns
This King of righteousness:
Meekness and patience, truth and love,
Compose his princely dress.

[VERSE 3]
The spirit of the Lord,
In rich abundance shed,
On this great Prophet gently lights,
And rests upon his head.

[VERSE 4]
Jesus, the light of men,
His doctrine life imparts;
O, may we feel its quickening power
To warm and glad our hearts.

[VERSE 5]
Cheered by its beams, our souls
Shall run the heavenly way;
The path which Christ has marked and trod,
Will lead to endless day.

--- TITLE: Happy the man whose cautious steps [item: PG20476-402]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0402
--- AUTHOR: Needham

[VERSE 1]
Happy the man whose cautious steps
Still keep the golden mean;
Whose life by wisdom's rules well formed,
Declares a conscience clean.

[VERSE 2]
To sect or party his large soul
Disdains to be confined;
The good he loves of every name,
And prays for all mankind.

[VERSE 3]
His business is to keep his heart;
Each passion to control;
Nobly ambitious well to rule
The empire of his soul.

[VERSE 4]
Not on the world his heart is set,
His treasure is above;
Nothing beneath the sovereign good
Can claim his highest love.
