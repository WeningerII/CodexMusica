--- TITLE: Great God! and wilt thou condescend [item: PG20476-763]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0763
--- AUTHOR: S. S. H. Book

[VERSE 1]
Great God! and wilt thou condescend
To be my Father and my Friend?
I but a child, and thou so high,
The Lord of earth and air and sky!

[VERSE 2]
Art thou my Father?--Let me be
A meek, obedient child to thee;
And try, in every deed and thought,
To serve and please thee as I ought.

[VERSE 3]
Art thou my Father?--I'll depend
Upon the care of such a friend;
And only wish to do and be
Whatever seemeth good to thee.

[VERSE 4]
Art thou my Father?--Then, at last,
When all my days on earth are past,
Send down and take me, in thy love,
To be thy better child above.
