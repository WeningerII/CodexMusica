--- TITLE: O, for the death of those [item: PG20476-604]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0604
--- AUTHOR: Ch. Psalmody

[VERSE 1]
O, for the death of those
Who slumber in the Lord!
O, be like theirs my last repose,
Like theirs my last reward!

[VERSE 2]
Their ransomed spirits soar,
On wings of faith and love,
To meet the Saviour they adore,
And reign with him above.

[VERSE 3]
With us their names shall live
Through long-succeeding years,
Embalmed with all our hearts can give,--
Our praises and our tears.

--- TITLE: Thou God of hope, to thee we bow [item: PG20476-782]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0782
--- AUTHOR: Ch. Psalmody

[VERSE 1]
Thou God of hope, to thee we bow;
Thou art our refuge in distress;
The husband of the widow thou,
The father of the fatherless.

[VERSE 2]
The poor are thy peculiar care;
To them thy promises are sure:
Thy gifts the poor in spirit share;
O, may we always thus be poor.

[VERSE 3]
May we thy law of love fulfil,
To bear each other's burdens here,
Endure and do thy righteous will,
And walk in all thy faith and fear.

[VERSE 4]
Thou God of hope, to thee we bow;
Thou art our refuge in distress;
The husband of the widow thou,
The father of the fatherless.
