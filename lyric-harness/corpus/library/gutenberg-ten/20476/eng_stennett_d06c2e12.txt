--- TITLE: "'T is finished!"--so the Saviour cried [item: PG20476-233]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0233
--- AUTHOR: Stennett

[VERSE 1]
"'T is finished!"--so the Saviour cried,
And meekly bowed his head and died:
"'T is finished!"--yes, the race is run,
The battle fought, the victory won.

[VERSE 2]
"'T is finished!"--all that heaven foretold
By prophets in the days of old;
And truths are opened to our view,
That kings and prophets never knew.

[VERSE 3]
"'T is finished!"--Son of God, thy power
Hath triumphed in this awful hour;
And yet our eyes with sorrow see
That life to us was death to thee.

[VERSE 4]
"'T is finished!"--let the joyful sound
Be heard through all the nations round;
"'Tis finished!"--let the triumph rise,
And swell the chorus of the skies.

--- TITLE: Thy life I read, my dearest Lord [item: PG20476-666]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0666
--- AUTHOR: Stennett

[VERSE 1]
Thy life I read, my dearest Lord,
With transport all-divine;
Thine image trace in every word,
Thy love in every line.

[VERSE 2]
With joy, I see a thousand charms
Spread o'er thy lovely face;
While infants in thy tender arms
Receive the smiling grace.

[VERSE 3]
"I take these little lambs," said he,
"And lay them on my breast;
Protection they shall find in me,--
In me be ever blest.

[VERSE 4]
"Death may the bands of life unloose,
But can't dissolve my love;
Millions of infant souls compose
The family above."

[VERSE 5]
His words, ye happy parents, hear,
And shout, with joys divine,
"Dear Saviour! all we have and are
Shall be forever thine."
