--- TITLE: What if the little rain should say [item: PG20476-750]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0750
--- AUTHOR: William Cutter

[VERSE 1]
What if the little rain should say,
So small a drop as I
Can ne'er refresh these thirsty fields,
I'll tarry in the sky?

[VERSE 2]
What if a shining beam of noon
Should in its fountain stay,
Because its feeble light alone
Cannot create a day?

[VERSE 3]
Doth not each rain-drop help to form
The cool, refreshing shower,
And every ray of light to warm
And beautify the flower?

[VERSE 4]
Go thou, and strive to do thy share--
One talent,--less than thine,--
Improved with steady zeal and care,
Would gain rewards divine.
