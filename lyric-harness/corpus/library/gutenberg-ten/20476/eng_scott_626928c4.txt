--- TITLE: Happy the meek, whose gentle breast [item: PG20476-349]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0349
--- AUTHOR: Scott

[VERSE 1]
Happy the meek, whose gentle breast,
Clear as the summer's evening ray,
Calm as the regions of the blest,
Enjoys on earth celestial day.

[VERSE 2]
His heart no broken friendships sting,
No storms his peaceful tent invade;
He rests beneath th' Almighty wing,
Hostile to none, of none afraid.

[VERSE 3]
Spirit of grace, all meek and mild,
Inspire our breasts, our souls possess;
Repel each passion rude and wild,
And bless us as we aim to bless.

--- TITLE: Imposture shrinks from light [item: PG20476-400]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0400
--- AUTHOR: Scott

[VERSE 1]
Imposture shrinks from light,
And dreads the curious eye;
But sacred truths the test invite,
They bid us search and try.

[VERSE 2]
With understanding blest,
Created to be free,
Our faith on man we dare not rest,
Subject to none but thee.

[VERSE 3]
Lord, give the light we need;
With soundest knowledge fill;
From noxious error guard our creed,
From prejudice our will.

[VERSE 4]
The truth thou shalt impart,
May we with firmness own;
Abhorring each evasive art,
And fearing thee alone.

--- TITLE: Where'er the Lord shall build my house [item: PG20476-926]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0926
--- AUTHOR: Scott

[VERSE 1]
Where'er the Lord shall build my house,
An altar to his name I'll raise;
There, morn and evening, shall ascend
The sacrifice of prayer and praise.

[VERSE 2]
With duteous mind, the social band
Shall search the records of thy law;
There learn thy will, and humbly bow
With filial reverence and awe.

[VERSE 3]
Here may he fix his sacred seat,
And spread the banner of his love;
Till, ripened for a happier state,
We meet the family above.
