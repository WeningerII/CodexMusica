--- TITLE: Ye that obey the immortal King [item: PG20476-18]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0018
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Ye that obey the immortal King,
Attend his holy place;
Bow to the glories of his name,
And sing his wondrous grace.

[VERSE 2]
Lift up your hands by morning light,
And raise your thanks on high;
Send your admiring thoughts, by night,
Above the starry sky.

[VERSE 3]
The God of Zion cheer your hearts
With rays of quickening grace:
'Tis he that spreads the heavens abroad,
Whose presence fills the place.

--- TITLE: Let the whole race of creatures lie [item: PG20476-145]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0145
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Let the whole race of creatures lie
Abased before the Lord:
Whate'er his mighty hand has formed
He governs with a word.

[VERSE 2]
Ten thousand ages ere the skies
Were into motion brought,
All the long years and worlds to come
Stood present to his thought.

[VERSE 3]
Trusting thy wisdom, God of love,
We would not wish to know
What, in the book of thy decrees,
Awaits us here below

[VERSE 4]
Be this alone our fervent prayer,--
Whate'er our lot shall be,
Or joys, or sorrows, may they form
Our souls for heaven and thee.

--- TITLE: Great God! the heavens' well ordered frame [item: PG20476-168]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0168
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Great God! the heavens' well ordered frame
Declares the glory of thy name,
There thy rich works of wonder shine:
A thousand starry beauties there,
A thousand radiant marks appear,
Of boundless skill and power divine.

[VERSE 2]
From night to day, from day to night,
The dawning and the dying light
Lectures of heavenly wisdom read;
With silent eloquence they raise
Our thoughts to our Creator's praise,
And neither sound nor language need.

[VERSE 3]
Yet thy divine instructions run
Far as the journeys of the sun:
Thy light and truth are known abroad;
We see thy smile in Nature's face,
And in the pages of thy grace
We read the glories of our God.

--- TITLE: 'T is by the faith of joys to come [item: PG20476-374]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0374
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
'T is by the faith of joys to come
We walk through deserts dark as night;
Till we arrive at heaven, our home,
Faith is our guide, and faith our light.

[VERSE 2]
The want of sight she well supplies;
She makes the pearly gates appear;
Far into distant worlds she flies,
And brings eternal glories near.

[VERSE 3]
Cheerful we tread the desert through,
While faith inspires a heavenly ray;
Though lions roar and tempests blow,
And rocks and dangers fill the way.

[VERSE 4]
So Abraham, by divine command,
Left his own house to walk with God;
His faith beheld the promised land,
And fired his zeal along the road.

--- TITLE: God of our mercy and our praise [item: PG20476-386]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0386
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
God of our mercy and our praise,
Thy glory is our song;
We'll speak the honors of thy grace
With a rejoicing tongue.

[VERSE 2]
When Christ among the sons of men
In humble form was found,
With cruel slanders, false and vain,
They compassed him around.

[VERSE 3]
Their miseries his compassion moved,
Their peace he still pursued;
They rendered hatred for his love,
And evil for his good.

[VERSE 4]
Their malice raged without a cause;
Yet, with his dying breath,
He prayed for murderers on his cross,
And blest his foes in death.

[VERSE 5]
O, may his conduct, all divine,
To us a model prove:
Like his, O God, our hearts incline
Our enemies to love.

--- TITLE: Jesus invites his friends [item: PG20476-692]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0692
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Jesus invites his friends
To meet around his board,
And join in blest communion here
With him their gracious Lord.

[VERSE 2]
For us he gave his life;
For us he gave his blood;
To save from sin our thankless race,
And bring them back to God.

[VERSE 3]
Our heavenly Father calls
Christ and his members one;
We the young children of his grace,
And he the elder Son.

[VERSE 4]
Let all our souls unite
A grateful song to raise;
Pleasure and love fill every mind,
And every voice be praise.

--- TITLE: When children give their hearts to God [item: PG20476-741]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0741
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
When children give their hearts to God,
'Tis pleasing in his eyes;
A flower, when offered in the bud,
Is no vain sacrifice.

[VERSE 2]
It saves us from a thousand snares
To mind religion young;
Grace will preserve our following years,
And make our virtues strong.

[VERSE 3]
To thee, Almighty God, to thee
May we our hearts resign;
'Twill please us to look back and see,
That our whole lives were thine.

--- TITLE: Would you behold the works of God [item: PG20476-826]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0826
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Would you behold the works of God,
His wonders in the world abroad?
With hardy mariners survey
The unknown regions of the sea.

[VERSE 2]
They leave their native shores behind,
And seize the favor of the wind;
Till God command, and tempests rise,
That heave the ocean to the skies.

[VERSE 3]
When land is far, and death is nigh,
Bereaved of hope, to God they cry:
His mercy hears their loud address,
And sends salvation in distress.

[VERSE 4]
He bids the winds their wrath assuage,
And stormy tempests cease to rage;
The grateful band their fears give o'er
And hail with joy their native shore.

[VERSE 5]
O, may the sons of men record
The wondrous goodness of the Lord;
Let them their purest offerings bring,
And in the church his glory sing.

--- TITLE: With songs and honors sounding loud [item: PG20476-853]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0853
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
With songs and honors sounding loud,
Address the Lord on high;
O'er all the heavens he spreads his cloud,
And waters veil the sky.

[VERSE 2]
He sends his showers of blessings down,
To cheer the plains below;
He makes the grass the mountains crown,
And corn in valleys grow.

[VERSE 3]
His steady councils change the face
Of each declining year;
He bids the sun cut short his race,
And wintry days appear.

[VERSE 4]
On us his providence has shone,
With gentle, smiling rays;
O, may our lips and lives make known
His goodness and his praise.

--- TITLE: Good is the heavenly King [item: PG20476-859]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0859
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
Good is the heavenly King,
Who makes the earth his care,
Visits the pastures every spring,
And bids the grass appear.

[VERSE 2]
Like rivers raised on high,
The clouds, at thy command,
Pour out their blessings from the sky,
To cheer the thirsty land.

[VERSE 3]
The hills, on every side,
Rejoice at falling showers:
The meadows, dressed in all their pride,
Perfume the air with flowers.

[VERSE 4]
The ridges drink their fill,
And ranks of corn appear;
Thy ways abound with blessings still,
Thy goodness crowns the year.

--- TITLE: How pleasing, Lord! to see [item: PG20476-928]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0928
--- AUTHOR: Watts

[VERSE 1]
How pleasing, Lord! to see,
How pure is the delight,
When mutual love, and love to thee,
A family unite!

[VERSE 2]
From these celestial springs
Such streams of comfort flow,
As no increase of riches brings,
Nor honors can bestow.

[VERSE 3]
No bliss can equal theirs,
Where such affections meet;
While mingled praise and mingled prayers
Make their communion sweet.

[VERSE 4]
'Tis the same pleasure fills
The breast in worlds above;
Where joy like morning dew distils,
And all the air is love.
