--- TITLE: The Lord our God is clothed with might [item: PG20476-105]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0105
--- AUTHOR: H. K. White

[VERSE 1]
The Lord our God is clothed with might;
The winds obey his will;
He speaks, and in the heavenly height
The rolling sun stands still.

[VERSE 2]
Rebel, ye waves, and o'er the land
With threatening aspect roar;
The Lord uplifts his awful hand,
And chains you to the shore.

[VERSE 3]
Ye winds of night, your force combine
Without his high behest,
Ye shall not, in the mountain pine,
Disturb the sparrow's nest.

[VERSE 4]
His voice sublime is heard afar;
In distant peals it dies;
He binds the whirlwinds to his car,
And sweeps the howling skies.

[VERSE 5]
Ye nations, bend; in reverence bend;
Ye monarchs, wait his nod,
And bid the choral song ascend
To celebrate our God.

--- TITLE: Through sorrow's night and danger's way [item: PG20476-360]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0360
--- AUTHOR: H. K. White

[VERSE 1]
Through sorrow's night and danger's way
Amid the deepening gloom,
The soldiers of an injured King
Are marching to the tomb.

[VERSE 2]
Their service done, securely laid
In this their last retreat,
Unheeded o'er their silent dust
The storms of life shall beat.

[VERSE 3]
Yet not thus lifeless in the grave
The vital spark shall lie;
O'er nature's ruins it shall rise,
To reach its kindred sky.

[VERSE 4]
Then heaven's soft dew o'er every eye
Shall shed its mildest rays;
And the long silent dust shall wake
In strains of endless praise.

--- TITLE: Christians! brethren! ere we part [item: PG20476-730]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0730
--- AUTHOR: H. K. White

[VERSE 1]
Christians! brethren! ere we part,
Every voice and every heart
Join, and to our Father raise
One last hymn of grateful praise.

[VERSE 2]
Though we here should meet no more,
Yet there is a brighter shore;
There, released from toil and pain,
There we all may meet again.

[VERSE 3]
Now to him who reigns in heaven
Be eternal glory given;
Grateful for thy love divine,
O may all our hearts be thine!

--- TITLE: O Lord, another day is flown [item: PG20476-933]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0933
--- AUTHOR: H. K. White

[VERSE 1]
O Lord, another day is flown,
And we, a lonely band,
Are met once more before thy throne,
To bless thy fostering hand.

[VERSE 2]
And wilt thou lend a listening ear
To praises low as ours!
Thou wilt; for thou dost love to hear
The song which meekness pours.

[VERSE 3]
O, let thy grace perform its part,
And let contention cease;
And shed abroad in every heart
Thine everlasting peace.

[VERSE 4]
Thus chastened, cleansed, entirely thine,
A flock by Jesus led,
The Sun of holiness shall shine
In glory on our head.

[VERSE 5]
And thou wilt turn our wandering feet,
And thou wilt bless our way,
Till worlds shall fade, and faith shall greet
The dawn of lasting day.
