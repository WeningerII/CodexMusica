--- TITLE: Come, shout aloud the Father's grace [item: PG20476-644]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0644
--- AUTHOR: Heginbotham

[VERSE 1]
Come, shout aloud the Father's grace,
And sing the Saviour's love;
Soon shall you join the glorious theme,
In loftier strains above.

[VERSE 2]
God, the eternal, mighty God,
To dearer names descends;
Calls us his treasure and his joy,
His children and his friends.

[VERSE 3]
My Father, God! and may these lips
Pronounce a name so dear?
Not thus could heaven's sweet harmony
Delight my listening ear.

[VERSE 4]
Thanks to my God for every gift
His bounteous hands bestow;
And thanks eternal for that love
Whence all these comforts flow.

--- TITLE: Great God! let all our tuneful powers [item: PG20476-851]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0851
--- AUTHOR: Heginbotham

[VERSE 1]
Great God! let all our tuneful powers
Awake and sing thy mighty name;
Thy hand rolls on our circling hours,
The hand from which our being came.

[VERSE 2]
Seasons and moons revolving round
In beauteous order speak thy praise;
And years with smiling mercy crowned,
To thee successive honors raise.

[VERSE 3]
Each changing season on our souls
Its sweetest, kindest influence sheds;
And every period, as it rolls,
Showers countless blessings on our heads.

[VERSE 4]
Our lives, our health, our friends, we owe
All to thy vast unbounded love;
Ten thousand precious gifts below,
And hope of nobler joys above.

--- TITLE: God of our lives, thy various praise [item: PG20476-890]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0890
--- AUTHOR: Heginbotham

[VERSE 1]
God of our lives, thy various praise
Our voices shall resound:
Thy hand directs our fleeting days,
And brings the seasons round.

[VERSE 2]
To thee shall grateful songs arise,
Our Father and our Friend,
Whose constant mercies from the skies
In genial streams descend.

[VERSE 3]
In every scene of life, thy care,
In every age, we see;
And constant as thy favors are,
So let our praises be.

[VERSE 4]
Still may thy love, in every scene,
In every age, appear;
And let the same compassion deign
To bless the opening year.

[VERSE 5]
If mercy smile, let mercy bring
Our wandering souls to God:
In our affliction we shall sing,
If thou wilt bless the rod.
