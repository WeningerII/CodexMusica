--- TITLE: All men are equal in their birth [item: PG20476-766]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0766
--- AUTHOR: H. Martineau

[VERSE 1]
All men are equal in their birth,
Heirs of the earth and skies;
All men are equal when that earth
Fades from their dying eyes.

[VERSE 2]
God meets the throngs who pay their vows
In courts that hands have made,
And hears the worshipper who bows
Beneath the plantain shade.

[VERSE 3]
'Tis man alone who difference sees,
And speaks of high and low,
And worships those, and tramples these,
While the same path they go.

[VERSE 4]
O, let man hasten to restore
To all their rights of love;
In power and wealth exult no more;
In wisdom lowly move.

[VERSE 5]
Ye great, renounce your earth-born pride,
Ye low, your shame and fear:
Live, as ye worship, side by side;
Your brotherhood revere.
