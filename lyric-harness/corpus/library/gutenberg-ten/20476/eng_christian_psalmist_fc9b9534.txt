--- TITLE: Behold my servant; see him rise [item: PG20476-198]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0198
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
Behold my servant; see him rise
Exalted in my might!
Him have I chosen, and in him
I place supreme delight.

[VERSE 2]
On him in rich effusion poured,
My spirit shall descend;
My truth and judgment he shall show
To earth's remotest end.

[VERSE 3]
Gentle and still shall be his voice;
No threats from him proceed;
The smoking flax shall he not quench,
Nor break the bruised reed.

[VERSE 4]
The feeble spark to flames he'll raise;
The weak will not despise;
Judgment he shall bring forth to truth,
And make the fallen rise.

[VERSE 5]
The progress of his zeal and power
Shall never know decline,
Till foreign lands and distant isles
Receive the law divine.

--- TITLE: Behold the Saviour on the cross [item: PG20476-231]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0231
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
Behold the Saviour on the cross,
A spectacle of woe!
See from his agonizing wounds
The blood incessant flow;

[VERSE 2]
Till death's pale ensigns o'er his cheek
And trembling lips were spread;
Till light forsook his closing eyes,
And life his drooping head.

[VERSE 3]
'Tis finished--the Messiah dies
For sins, but not his own;
The great redemption is complete,
And death is overthrown.

[VERSE 4]
'Tis finished--ritual worship ends,
And Gospel ages run;
All old things now are past away,
A new world is begun.

--- TITLE: Faith, hope, and love, now dwell on earth [item: PG20476-387]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0387
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
Faith, hope, and love, now dwell on earth,
And earth by them is blest;
But faith and hope must yield to love,
Of all the graces best.

[VERSE 2]
Hope shall to full fruition rise,
And faith be sight above;
These are the means, but this the end,
For saints forever love.

--- TITLE: Be with me, Lord, where'er I go [item: PG20476-488]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0488
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
Be with me, Lord, where'er I go;
Teach me what thou wouldst have me do;
Show me my weakness,--let me see
I have my power, my all from thee.

[VERSE 2]
Enrich me always with thy love;
My kind protection ever prove;
Thy signet put upon my breast,
And let thy spirit on me rest.

[VERSE 3]
Assist and teach me how to pray;
Incline my nature to obey;
What thou abhorr'st that let me flee,
And only love what pleases thee.

[VERSE 4]
O may I never do my will,
But thine, and only thine, fulfil;
Let all my time and all my ways
Be spent and ended to thy praise.

--- TITLE: 'Tis God the spirit leads [item: PG20476-510]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0510
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
'Tis God the spirit leads
In paths before unknown:
The work to be performed is ours;
The strength is all his own.

[VERSE 2]
Assisted by his grace,
We still pursue our way;
And hope at last to reach the prize,
Secure in endless day.

[VERSE 3]
'Tis he that works to will;
'Tis he that works to do;
His is the power by which we act,
His be the glory too.

--- TITLE: What must it be to dwell above [item: PG20476-511]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0511
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
What must it be to dwell above,
At God's right hand, where Jesus reigns,
Since the sweet earnest of his love
O'erwhelms us on these earthly plains!
No heart can think, no tongue explain,
What bliss it is with Christ to reign.

[VERSE 2]
When sin no more obstructs our sight,
When sorrow pains our hearts no more,
How shall we view the Prince of Light
And all his works of grace explore!
What heights and depths of love divine
Will there through endless ages shine!

[VERSE 3]
This is the heaven I long to know;
For this, with patience, I would wait,
Till, weaned from earth, and all below,
I mount to my celestial seat,
And wave my palm, and wear my crown,
And, with the elders, cast them down.

--- TITLE: Fountain of mercy, God of love [item: PG20476-877]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0877
--- AUTHOR: Christian Psalmist

[VERSE 1]
Fountain of mercy, God of love,
How rich thy bounties are!
The rolling seasons, as they move,
Proclaim thy constant care.

[VERSE 2]
When in the bosom of the earth
The sower hid the grain,
Thy goodness marked its secret birth,
And sent the early rain.

[VERSE 3]
The spring's sweet influence, Lord, was thine
The plants in beauty grew;
Thou gav'st refulgent suns to shine,
And mild, refreshing dew.

[VERSE 4]
These various mercies from above
Matured the swelling grain;
A kindly harvest crowns thy love,
And plenty fills the plain.

[VERSE 5]
We own and bless thy gracious sway;
Thy hand all nature hails;
Seed-time nor harvest, night nor day,
Summer nor winter, fails.
