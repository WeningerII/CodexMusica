--- TITLE: What shall we render, bounteous Lord [item: PG20476-785]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0785
--- AUTHOR: Boden

[VERSE 1]
What shall we render, bounteous Lord,
For all the grace we see?
The goodness feeble man can yield
Extendeth not to thee.

[VERSE 2]
To scenes of woe, to beds of pain,
We'll cheerfully repair,
And, with the gifts thy hand bestows,
Relieve the sufferers there.

[VERSE 3]
The widow's heart shall sing for joy;
The orphan shall be glad;
And hungering souls we'll gladly point
To Christ, the living bread.

[VERSE 4]
Thus what our heavenly Father gave
Shall we as freely give;
Thus copy him who lived to save,
And died that we might live.
