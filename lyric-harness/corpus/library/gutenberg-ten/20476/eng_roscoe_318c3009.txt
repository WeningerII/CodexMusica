--- TITLE: This is the first and great command-- [item: PG20476-385]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0385
--- AUTHOR: Roscoe

[VERSE 1]
This is the first and great command--
To love thy God above;
And this the second--as thyself
Thy neighbor thou shalt love.

[VERSE 2]
Who is my neighbor? He who wants
The help which thou canst give;
And both the law and prophets say,
This do, and thou shalt live.

--- TITLE: Great God! beneath whose piercing eye [item: PG20476-842]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0842
--- AUTHOR: Roscoe

[VERSE 1]
Great God! beneath whose piercing eye
The world's extended kingdoms lie;
Whose favoring smile upholds them all,
Whose anger smites them, and they fall;

[VERSE 2]
We bow before thy heavenly throne;
Thy power we see, thy goodness own;
But, cherished by thy milder voice,
Our bosoms tremble and rejoice.

[VERSE 3]
Thy kindness to our fathers shown,
Their children's children long shall own;
To thee with grateful hearts shall raise
Their tribute of exulting praise.

[VERSE 4]
Our God, our Guardian, and our Friend!
Oh still thy sheltering arm extend;
Preserved by thee for ages past,
For ages may thy kindness last.
