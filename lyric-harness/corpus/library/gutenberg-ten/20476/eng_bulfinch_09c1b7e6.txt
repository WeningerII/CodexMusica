--- TITLE: Hail to the Sabbath day [item: PG20476-42]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0042
--- AUTHOR: Bulfinch

[VERSE 1]
Hail to the Sabbath day!
The day divinely given,
When men to God their homage pay,
And earth draws near to heaven.

[VERSE 2]
Lord, in this sacred hour,
Within thy courts we bend,
And bless thy love, and own thy power,
Our Father and our Friend.

[VERSE 3]
But thou art not alone
In courts by mortals trod;
Nor only is the day thine own
When man draws near to God.

[VERSE 4]
Thy temple is the arch
Of yon unmeasured sky;
Thy Sabbath, the stupendous march
Of grand eternity.

[VERSE 5]
Lord, may that holier day
Dawn on thy servants' sight;
And purer worship may we pay
In heaven's unclouded light.

--- TITLE: Children of light, awake [item: PG20476-359]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0359
--- AUTHOR: Bulfinch

[VERSE 1]
Children of light, awake,
At Jesus' call arise,
Forth with your leader to partake
His toils, his victories.

[VERSE 2]
Ye must not idly stand,
His sacred voice who hear;
Arm for the strife the feeble hand,
The holy standard rear.

[VERSE 3]
Nought doth the world afford,
But toil must be the price;
Wilt thou not, servant of the Lord,
Then toil for paradise?

[VERSE 4]
Awake, ye sons of light,
Strive till the prize be won;
Far spent already is the night;
The day comes brightening on.
