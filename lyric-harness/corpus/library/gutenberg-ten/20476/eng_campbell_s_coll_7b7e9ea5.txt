--- TITLE: Mere human power shall fast decay [item: PG20476-405]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0405
--- AUTHOR: Campbell's Coll

[VERSE 1]
Mere human power shall fast decay,
And youthful vigor cease;
But they who wait upon the Lord
In strength shall still increase.

[VERSE 2]
They with unwearied feet shall tread
The path of life divine,
With growing ardor onward move,
With growing brightness shine.

[VERSE 3]
On eagles' wings they mount, they soar;
Their wings are faith and love;
Till, past the cloudy regions here,
They rise to heaven above.

--- TITLE: God of mercy, hear our prayer [item: PG20476-738]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0738
--- AUTHOR: Campbell's Coll

[VERSE 1]
God of mercy, hear our prayer
For the children thou hast given;
Let them all thy blessings share--
Grace on earth and bliss in heaven.

[VERSE 2]
In the morning of their days
May their hearts be drawn to thee;
Let them learn to lisp thy praise
In their earliest infancy.

[VERSE 3]
When we see their passions rise,
Sinful habits unsubdued,
Then to thee we lift our eyes,
That their hearts may be renewed.

[VERSE 4]
For this mercy, Lord, we cry;
Bend thine ever-gracious ear;
While on thee our souls rely,
Hear our prayer--in mercy hear.
