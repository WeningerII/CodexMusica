--- TITLE: The heaven of heavens cannot contain [item: PG20476-118]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0118
--- AUTHOR: Drennan

[VERSE 1]
The heaven of heavens cannot contain
The universal Lord;
Yet he in humble hearts will deign
To dwell and be adored.

[VERSE 2]
Where'er ascends the sacrifice
Of fervent praise and prayer,
Or on the earth, or in the skies,
The God of heaven is there.

[VERSE 3]
His presence is diffused abroad
Through realms, through worlds unknown;
Who seek the mercies of our God
Are ever near his throne.

--- TITLE: All nature feels attractive power [item: PG20476-381]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0381
--- AUTHOR: Drennan

[VERSE 1]
All nature feels attractive power,
A strong, embracing force;
The drops that sparkle in the shower,
The planets in their course.

[VERSE 2]
Thus, in the universe of mind,
Is felt the law of love;
The charity both strong and kind,
For all that live and move.

[VERSE 3]
In this fine sympathetic chain
All creatures bear a part;
Their every pleasure, every pain,
Linked to the feeling heart.

[VERSE 4]
More perfect bond, the Christian plan
Attaches soul to soul;
Our neighbor is the suffering man,
Though at the farthest pole.

[VERSE 5]
To earth below, from heaven above,
The faith in Christ professed,
More clearly shows that God is love,
And whom he loves is blessed.
