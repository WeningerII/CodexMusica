--- TITLE: Rise, crowned with light, imperial Salem, rise [item: PG20476-298]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0298
--- AUTHOR: Pope

[VERSE 1]
Rise, crowned with light, imperial Salem, rise!
Exalt thy towering head, and lift thine eyes!
See heaven its sparkling portals wide display,
And break upon thee in a flood of day!

[VERSE 2]
See a long race thy spacious courts adorn,
See future sons and daughters yet unborn,
In crowding ranks on every side arise,
Demanding life, impatient for the skies!

[VERSE 3]
See barbarous nations at thy gates attend,
Walk in thy light, and in thy temples bend!
See thy bright altars thronged with prostrate kings,
While every land its joyous tribute brings.

[VERSE 4]
The seas shall waste, the skies to smoke decay,
Rocks fall to dust, and mountains melt away
But fixed his word, his saving power remains,
Thy realm shall last, thy own Messiah reigns.

--- TITLE: Father of all! in every age [item: PG20476-482]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0482
--- AUTHOR: Pope

[VERSE 1]
Father of all! in every age,
In every clime, adored,
By saint, by savage, and by sage,
Jehovah, Jove, or Lord!--

[VERSE 2]
Save me alike from foolish pride,
Or impious discontent
At aught thy wisdom has denied,
Or aught thy goodness lent.

[VERSE 3]
This day be bread and peace my lot:
All else beneath the sun
Thou knowest if best bestowed or not
And let thy will be done.

[VERSE 4]
Yet not to earth's contracted span
Thy goodness let me bound,
Or think thee Lord alone of man,
When thousand worlds are round.

[VERSE 5]
To thee whose temple is all space,
Whose altar, earth, sea, skies,
One chorus let all beings raise,
All nature's incense rise.

--- TITLE: Father of all, whose cares extend [item: PG20476-483]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0483
--- AUTHOR: Pope

[VERSE 1]
Father of all, whose cares extend
To earth's remotest shore,
Through every age let praise ascend,
And every clime adore.

[VERSE 2]
Mean though I am, not wholly so,
Since quickened by thy breath;
Lord, lead me wheresoe'er I go,
Through this day's life or death.

[VERSE 3]
Teach me to feel another's woe,
To hide the fault I see;
That mercy I to others show,
That mercy show to me.

[VERSE 4]
If I am right, thy grace impart
Still in the right to stay;
If I am wrong, O teach my heart
To find that better way.

[VERSE 5]
What conscience dictates to be done,
Or warns me not to do,
This teach me more than hell to shun,
That more than heaven pursue.
