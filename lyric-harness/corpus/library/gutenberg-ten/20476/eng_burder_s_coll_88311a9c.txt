--- TITLE: What though no flowers the fig-tree clothe [item: PG20476-629]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0629
--- AUTHOR: Burder's Coll

[VERSE 1]
What though no flowers the fig-tree clothe
Though vines their fruit deny,
The labor of the olive fail,
And fields no meat supply;--

[VERSE 2]
Though from the fold, with sad surprise,
My flock cut off I see;
Though famine reign in empty stalls,
Where herds were wont to be;--

[VERSE 3]
Yet in the Lord will I be glad,
And glory in his love;
In him I'll joy, who will the God
Of my salvation prove.

[VERSE 4]
God is the treasure of my soul,
The source of lasting joy--
A joy which want shall not impair,
Nor death itself destroy.
