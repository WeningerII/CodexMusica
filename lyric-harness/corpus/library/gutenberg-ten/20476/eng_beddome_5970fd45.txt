--- TITLE: Almighty God! thy wondrous works [item: PG20476-139]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0139
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
Almighty God! thy wondrous works
Of providence and grace,
An angel's perfect mind exceed,
And all our pride abase.

[VERSE 2]
Stupendous heights! amazing depths!
Creatures in vain explore:
Or, if a transient glimpse we gain,
'Tis faint and quickly o'er.

[VERSE 3]
Though all the mysteries lie concealed
Beyond what we can see,
Grant us the knowledge of ourselves,
The knowledge, Lord, of thee.

--- TITLE: Jesus! delightful, charming name [item: PG20476-245]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0245
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
Jesus! delightful, charming name!
It spreads a fragrance round;
Justice and mercy, truth and peace,
In union here are found.

[VERSE 2]
He is our life, our joy, our strength;
In him all glories meet;
He is a shade above our heads,
A light to guide our feet.

[VERSE 3]
When storms arise and tempests blow,
He speaks the stilling word;
The threatening billows cease to flow,
The winds obey their Lord.

[VERSE 4]
The thickest clouds are soon dispersed,
If Jesus shows his face;
To weary, heavy-laden souls
He is the resting-place.

--- TITLE: In duties and in sufferings too [item: PG20476-249]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0249
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
In duties and in sufferings too,
My Lord I feign would trace,
As he hath done, so would I do,
Sustained by heavenly grace.

[VERSE 2]
Inflamed with zeal, 'twas his delight
To do his Father's will;
May the same zeal my soul excite
His precepts to fulfil.

[VERSE 3]
Meekness, humility and love
Through all his conduct shine;
O, may my whole deportment prove
A copy, Lord, of thine.

--- TITLE: The wandering star and fleeting wind [item: PG20476-326]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0326
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
The wandering star and fleeting wind
Are emblems of the fickle mind;
The morning cloud and early dew
Bring our inconstancy to view.

[VERSE 2]
But cloud and wind and dew and star,
Only a faint resemblance bear;
Nor can there aught in nature be
So changeable and frail as we.

[VERSE 3]
Our outward walk and inward frame
Are scarcely through an hour the same:
We vow, and straight our vows forget,
And then those very vows repeat.

[VERSE 4]
With contrite hearts, Lord, we confess
Our folly and unsteadfastness:
When shall these hearts more stable be,
Fixed by thy grace alone on thee?

--- TITLE: Am I an Israelite indeed [item: PG20476-502]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0502
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
Am I an Israelite indeed.
Without a false disguise?
Have I renounced my sins, and left
My refuges of lies?

[VERSE 2]
Say, does my heart unchanged remain,
Or is it formed anew?
What is the rule by which I walk,
The object I pursue?

[VERSE 3]
Cause me, O God of truth and grace,
My real state to know;
If I am wrong, O set me right!
If right, preserve me so!

--- TITLE: Ye trembling souls, dismiss your fears [item: PG20476-643]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0643
--- AUTHOR: Beddome

[VERSE 1]
Ye trembling souls, dismiss your fears;
Be mercy all your theme;
For mercy like a river flows,
In one perpetual stream.

[VERSE 2]
Fear not the powers of earth and hell;
God will those powers restrain;
His arm will all their rage repel,
And make their efforts vain.

[VERSE 3]
Fear not the want of outward good;
For his he will provide,
Grant them supplies of daily food,
And give them heaven beside.

[VERSE 4]
Fear not that he will e'er forsake,
Or leave his work undone;
He's faithful to his promises,
And faithful to his Son.
