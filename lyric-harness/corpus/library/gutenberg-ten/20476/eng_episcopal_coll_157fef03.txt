--- TITLE: Sinner, rouse thee from thy sleep [item: PG20476-268]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0268
--- AUTHOR: Episcopal Coll

[VERSE 1]
Sinner, rouse thee from thy sleep;
Wake, and o'er thy folly weep;
Raise thy spirit, dark and dead;
Jesus waits his light to shed.

[VERSE 2]
Wake from sleep; arise from death;
See the bright and living path;
Watchful, tread that path; be wise;
Leave thy folly; seek the skies.

[VERSE 3]
Leave thy folly; cease from crime;
From this hour redeem thy time;
Life secure without delay;
Brief is this thy mortal day.

[VERSE 4]
O, then, rouse thee from thy sleep;
Wake, and o'er thy folly weep;
Jesus calls from death and night;
Jesus waits to shed his light.

--- TITLE: The Spirit, in our hearts [item: PG20476-275]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0275
--- AUTHOR: Episcopal Coll

[VERSE 1]
The Spirit, in our hearts,
Is whispering, "Sinner, come!"
The Bride, the Church of Christ, proclaims
To all his children, "Come!"

[VERSE 2]
Let him that heareth say
To all about him, "Come!"
Let him that thirsts for righteousness,
To Christ, the Fountain, come!

[VERSE 3]
Yes, whosoever will,
O, let him freely come,
And freely drink the stream of life;
'Tis Jesus bids him come.

[VERSE 4]
Lo, Jesus, who invites,
Declares, "I quickly come:"
Lord, even so! I wait thine hour:
Jesus, my Saviour, come!

--- TITLE: When, Lord, to this our western land [item: PG20476-736]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0736
--- AUTHOR: Episcopal Coll

[VERSE 1]
When, Lord, to this our western land,
Led by thy providential hand,
Our wandering fathers came,
Their ancient homes, their friends in youth
Sent forth the heralds of thy truth,
To keep them in thy name.

[VERSE 2]
Then, through our solitary coast,
The desert features soon were lost;
Thy temples there arose;
Our shores, as culture made them fair,
Were hallowed by thy rites, by prayer,
And blossomed as the rose.

[VERSE 3]
And O, may we repay this debt
To regions solitary yet
Within our spreading land!
There brethren, from our common home,
Still westward, like our fathers, roam,
Still guided by thy hand.

[VERSE 4]
Father, we own this debt of love;
O, shed thy Spirit from above,
To move each Christian breast,
Till heralds shall thy truth proclaim,
And temples rise, to fix thy name,
Through all our boundless West!

--- TITLE: In the glad morn of life, when youth [item: PG20476-746]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0746
--- AUTHOR: Episcopal Coll

[VERSE 1]
In the glad morn of life, when youth
With generous ardor glows,
And shines in all the fairest charms
That beauty can disclose;

[VERSE 2]
Deep on thy soul,--before its powers
Are yet by vice enslaved,--
Be thy Creator's lofty name
And character engraved.

[VERSE 3]
For soon the shades of grief may cloud
The sunshine of thy days;
And cares and toils, an endless round,
Encompass all thy ways.

[VERSE 4]
True wisdom, early sought and gained,
In age will give thee rest;
O then, improve the morn of life,
To make its evening blest!

--- TITLE: Now, the shades of night are gone [item: PG20476-938]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0938
--- AUTHOR: Episcopal Coll

[VERSE 1]
Now, the shades of night are gone;
Now the morning light comes on;
Lord, may we be thine to-day,
Drive the shades of sin away.

[VERSE 2]
Fill our souls with heavenly light,
Banish doubt, and clear our sight;
In thy service, Lord, to-day,
May we stand, and watch and pray.

[VERSE 3]
Keep our haughty passions bound;
Save us from our foes around;
Going out and coming in,
Keep us safe from every sin.

[VERSE 4]
When our work of life is past,
O, receive us then at last;
Night and sin will be no more,
When we reach the heavenly shore.
