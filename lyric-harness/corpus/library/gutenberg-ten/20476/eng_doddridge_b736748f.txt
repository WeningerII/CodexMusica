--- TITLE: Eternal Source of light and thought [item: PG20476-74]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0074
--- AUTHOR: Doddridge

[VERSE 1]
Eternal Source of light and thought!
Be all beneath thyself forgot,
Whilst thee, great parent-mind, we own,
In prostrate homage round thy throne.

[VERSE 2]
Whilst in themselves our souls survey
Of thee some faint reflected ray,
They wondering to their Father rise:
His power how vast! his thoughts how wise!

[VERSE 3]
O may we live before thy face,
The willing subjects of thy grace;
And through each path of duty move,
With filial awe, and filial love.

--- AUTHOR: Doddridge

[VERSE 1]
Behold th' amazing sight,
The Saviour lifted high!
Behold the Father's chief delight
Expire in agony!

[VERSE 2]
For whom, for whom, my heart,
Were all these sorrows borne?
Why did he feel that piercing smart,
And meet that cruel scorn?

[VERSE 3]
For love of us he bled,
And all in torture died;
'T was love that bowed his fainting head,
And oped his gushing side.

[VERSE 4]
In him our hearts unite,
Nor share his grief alone,
But from his cross pursue their flight
To his triumphant throne.

--- TITLE: Amazing, beauteous change [item: PG20476-312]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0312
--- AUTHOR: Doddridge

[VERSE 1]
Amazing, beauteous change!
A world created new!
My thoughts with transport range,
The lovely scene to view:
In all I trace,
The work is thine;
Saviour divine,
Be thine the praise!

[VERSE 2]
See crystal fountains play
Amidst the burning sands;
The river's winding way
Shines through the thirsty lands;
New grass is seen,
Its carpet spreads
And o'er the meads
Of living green.

[VERSE 3]
Where pointed brambles grew,
Entwined with horrid thorn,
Gay flowers, forever new,
The painted fields adorn;
The blushing rose,
In union fair,
And lily there,
Their sweets disclose.

[VERSE 4]
The tyrants of the plain
Their savage chase give o'er;
No more they rend the slain.
And thirst for blood no more;
But infant hands
And lions yoke
Fierce tigers stroke,
In flowery bands.

[VERSE 5]
O, when, Almighty Lord,
Shall these glad scenes arise,
To verify thy word,
And bless our wondering eyes?
That earth may raise,
United songs
With all its tongues,
Of ardent praise.

How swift they pass away!
The dying flower reclines its head,
The beauty of a day.

Soon are those earthly treasures lost,
We fondly call our own;
Scarce the possession can we boast,
When straight we find them gone.

[VERSE 3]

With God laid up in store;
Treasures beyond the changing sky,
More bright than golden ore.

Have scattered here below,
In the fair, fertile fields above
To ample harvests grow.
