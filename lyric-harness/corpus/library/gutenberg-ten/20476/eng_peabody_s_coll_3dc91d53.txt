--- TITLE: Father! bless thy word to all [item: PG20476-85]
--- SOURCE: PG20476 https://www.gutenberg.org/files/20476/20476-h/20476-h.htm#h0085
--- AUTHOR: Peabody's Coll

[VERSE 1]
Father! bless thy word to all;
Quick and powerful may it prove;
O, may sinners hear thy call,
May thy people grow in love.

[VERSE 2]
Father, bid the world rejoice;
Send thy heavenly truth abroad;
May the nations hear thy voice,
Hear it, and return to God.
