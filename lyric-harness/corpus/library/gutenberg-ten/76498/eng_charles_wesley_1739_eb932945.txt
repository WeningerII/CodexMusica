--- TITLE: O for a thousand tongues to sing [item: PG76498-108]
--- SOURCE: PG76498 https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/76498.txt.utf-8 (numbered entry 108; browser source line 5668)
--- AUTHOR: Charles Wesley, 1739.

[VERSE 1]
O for a thousand tongues to sing
My great Redeemer’s praise,
The glories of my God and King,
The triumphs of His grace!

[VERSE 2]
My gracious Master and my God,
Assist me to proclaim,
To spread through all the earth abroad
The honors of Thy name.

[VERSE 3]
Jesus! the name that charms our fears,
That bids our sorrows cease;
’Tis music in the sinner’s ears,
’Tis life and health and peace.

[VERSE 4]
He breaks the power of canceled sin,
He sets the prisoner free;
His blood can make the foulest clean;
His blood avails for me.

[VERSE 5]
Look unto Him, ye nations; own
Your God, ye fallen race;
Look and be saved through faith alone,
Be justified by grace.

[VERSE 6]
See all your sins on Jesus laid;
The Lamb of God was slain;
His soul was once an offering made
For every soul of man.

[VERSE 7]
Glory to God and praise and love
Be ever, ever giv’n
By saints below and saints above,
The Church in earth and heav’n.

--- TITLE: Hail the day that sees Him rise [item: PG76498-243]
--- SOURCE: PG76498 https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/76498.txt.utf-8 (numbered entry 243; browser source line 11663)
--- AUTHOR: Charles Wesley, 1739.

[VERSE 1]
Hail the day that sees Him rise,
To His throne above the skies!
Christ, the Lamb for sinners given,
Reascends His native heaven.

[VERSE 2]
There the glorious triumph waits:
Lift your heads, eternal gates;
He hath conquered death and sin;
Take the King of Glory in!

[VERSE 3]
Him though highest heaven receives,
Still He loves the earth He leaves;
Though returning to His throne,
Still He calls mankind His own.

[VERSE 4]
See, He lifts His hands above;
See, He shows the prints of love.
Hark! His gracious lips bestow
Blessings on His Church below.

[VERSE 5]
Still for us His death He pleads;
Prevalent, He intercedes;
Near Himself prepares our place,
Harbinger of human race.

[VERSE 6]
There we shall with Thee remain
Partners of Thy endless reign;
There Thy face unclouded see,
Find our heaven of heavens in Thee.
