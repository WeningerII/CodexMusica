--- TITLE: When the day of toil is done [item: PG76498-546]
--- SOURCE: PG76498 https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/76498.txt.utf-8 (numbered entry 546; browser source line 24235)
--- AUTHOR: J. Ellerton, 1871.

[VERSE 1]
When the day of toil is done,
When the race of life is run,
Father, grant Thy wearied one
Rest forevermore.

[VERSE 2]
When the strife of sin is stilled,
When the foe within is killed,
Be Thy gracious word fulfilled,
“Peace forevermore.”

[VERSE 3]
When the darkness melts away
At the breaking of the day,
Bid us hail the cheering ray,
Light forevermore.

[VERSE 4]
When the heart, by sorrow tried,
Feels at length its throbs subside,
Bring us where all tears are dried,
Joy forevermore.

[VERSE 5]
When for vanished days we yearn,
Days that never can return,
Teach us in Thy love to learn
Love forevermore.

[VERSE 6]
When the breath of life is flown,
When the grave must claim its own,
Lord of life, be ours Thy crown,—
Life forevermore.
