--- TITLE: Troll The Bowl [item: PG22223-pg22223-027]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 1279-1308
--- AUTHOR: Thomas Dekker

[VERSE 1]
COLD's the wind, and wet's the rain,
Saint Hugh be our good speed!
Ill is the weather that bringeth no gain,
Nor helps good hearts in need.

[VERSE 2]
Troll the bowl, the jolly nut-brown bowl,
And here, kind mate, to thee!
Let's sing a dirge for Saint Hugh's soul,
And down it merrily.

[VERSE 3]
Down-a-down, hey, down-a-down,
Hey derry derry down-a-down.
Ho! well done, to let me come,
Ring compass, gentle joy!

[VERSE 4]
