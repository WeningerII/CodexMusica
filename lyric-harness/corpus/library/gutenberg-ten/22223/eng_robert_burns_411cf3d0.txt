--- TITLE: Whistle, And I 'Ll Come To Ye, My Lad [item: PG22223-pg22223-136]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 7892-7920
--- AUTHOR: Robert Burns

[VERSE 1]
O WHISTLE, and I'll come to ye, my lad;
O whistle, and I'll come to ye, my lad:
Tho' father and mither and a' should gae mad,
O whistle, and I'll come to ye, my lad.

[VERSE 2]
But warily tent, when ye come to court me,
And come na unless the back-yett be a-jee;
Syne up the back-stile, and let naebody see,
And come as ye were na comin' to me.

[VERSE 3]
At kirk, or at market, whene'er ye meet me,
Gang by me as tho' that ye car'd na a flie
But steal me a blink o' your bonie black ee,
Yet look as ye were na lookin' at me.

[VERSE 4]
Aye vow and protest that ye care na for me,
And whiles ye may lightly my beauty a wee;
But court na anither, tho jokin' ye be,
For fear that she wyle your fancy frae me.

[VERSE 5]
O whistle, and I'll come to ye, my lad;
O whistle, and I 'll come to ye, my lad:
Tho' father and mither and a' should gae mad,
O whistle, and I'll come to ye, my lad.

--- TITLE: The De'Il'S Awa' Wi' The Exciseman [item: PG22223-pg22223-137]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 7920-7943
--- AUTHOR: Robert Burns

[VERSE 1]
THE De'il cam fiddling thro' the town,
And danc'd awa wi' the Exciseman;
And ilka wife cry'd 'Auld Mahoun,
We wish you luck o' your prize, man.

[VERSE 2]
'We'll mak our maut, and brew our drink,
We'll dance, and sing, and rejoice, man;
And monie thanks to the muckle black De'il
That danc'd awa wi' the Exciseman.

[VERSE 3]
'There's threesome reels, and foursome reels,
There's hornpipes and strathspeys, man;
But the ae best dance that cam to our lan',
Was--the De'il 's awa wi' the Exciseman.

[VERSE 4]
We'll mak our maut, and brew our drink,
We'll dance, and sing, and rejoice, man;
And monie thanks to the muckle black De'il
That danc'd awa wi' the Exciseman.'
