--- TITLE: The' Royal George' [item: PG22223-pg22223-114]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 6445-6493
--- AUTHOR: William Cowper

[VERSE 1]
TOLL for the Brave!
The brave that are no more!
All sunk beneath the wave
Fast by their native shore!

[VERSE 2]
Eight hundred of the brave,
Whose courage well was tried,
Had made the vessel heel
And laid her on her side.

[VERSE 3]
A land-breeze shook the shrouds,
And she was overset;
Down went the _Royal George_
With all her crew complete.

[VERSE 4]
Toll for the brave!
Brave Kempenfelt is gone;
His last sea-fight is fought,
His work of glory done.

[VERSE 5]
It was not in the battle;
No tempest gave the shock,
She sprang no fatal leak,
She ran upon no rock.

[VERSE 6]
His sword was in its sheath,
His fingers held the pen,
When Kempenfelt went down
With twice four hundred men.

[VERSE 7]
Weigh the vessel up,
Once dreaded by our foes!
And mingle with our cup
The tear that England owes.

[VERSE 8]
Her timbers yet are sound,
And she may float again
Full charged with England's thunders
And plough the distant main:

[VERSE 9]
But Kempenfelt is gone,
His victories are o'er;
And he and his eight hundred
Shall plough the wave no more.

--- TITLE: Boadicea [item: PG22223-pg22223-115]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 6493-6552
--- AUTHOR: William Cowper

[VERSE 1]
WHEN the British warrior queen,
Bleeding from the Roman rods,
Sought, with an indignant mien,
Counsel of her country's gods,

[VERSE 2]
Sage beneath the spreading oak
Sat the Druid, hoary chief;
Every burning word he spoke
Full of rage, and full of grief.

[VERSE 3]
'Princess, if our aged eyes
Weep upon thy matchless wrongs,
'Tis because resentment ties
All the terrors of our tongues.

[VERSE 4]
'Rome shall perish--write that word
In the blood that she has spilt;
Perish, hopeless and abhorr'd,
Deep in ruin as in guilt.

[VERSE 5]
'Rome, for empire far renown'd,
Tramples on a thousand states;
Soon her pride shall kiss the ground--
Hark! the Gaul is at her gates!

[VERSE 6]
'Other Romans shall arise,
Heedless of a soldier's name;
Sounds, not arms, shall win the prize,
Harmony the path to fame.

[VERSE 7]
'Then the progeny that springs
From the forests of our land,
Arm'd with thunder, clad with wings,
Shall a wider world command.

[VERSE 8]
'Regions Caesar never knew
Thy posterity shall sway;
Where his eagles never flew,
None invincible as they.'

[VERSE 9]
Such the bard's prophetic words,
Pregnant with celestial fire,
Bending as he swept the chords
Of his sweet but awful lyre.

[VERSE 10]
She, with all a monarch's pride,
Felt them in her bosom glow;
Rush'd to battle, fought, and died;
Dying hurl'd them at the foe.

[VERSE 11]
'Ruffians, pitiless as proud,
Heaven awards the vengeance due
Empire is on us bestow'd,
Shame and ruin wait for you.'
