--- TITLE: Little Billee [item: PG22223-pg22223-259]
--- SOURCE: PG22223 plain-text lines 14577-14635
--- AUTHOR: William Makepeace Thackeray

[VERSE 1]
THERE were three sailors of Bristol city
Who took a boat and went to sea,

[VERSE 2]
But first with beef and captain's biscuits
And pickled pork they loaded she.

[VERSE 3]
There was gorging Jack and guzzling Jimmy,
And the youngest he was little Billee.

[VERSE 4]
Now when they got as far as the Equator
They'd nothing left but one split pea.

[VERSE 5]
Says gorging Jack to guzzling Jimmy,
'I am extremely hungaree.'

[VERSE 6]
To gorging Jack says guzzling Jimmy,
'We've nothing left; us must eat we.'

[VERSE 7]
Says gorging Jack to guzzling Jimmy,
'With one another we shouldn't agree!

[VERSE 8]
'There's little Bill, he's young and tender,
We're old and tough, so let's eat he.'

[VERSE 9]
'Oh, Bill, we're going to kill and eat you,
So undo the button of your chemie.'

[VERSE 10]
When Bill received this information
He used his pocket handkerchie.

[VERSE 11]
'First let me say my catechism,
Which my poor mammy taught to me.'

[VERSE 12]
'Make haste, make haste,' says guzzling Jimmy,
While Jack pulled out his snickersnee.

[VERSE 13]
So Billy went up to the main-top gallant mast,
And down he fell on his bended knee.

[VERSE 14]
He scarce had come to the twelfth commandment
When up he jumps. 'There's land I see:

[VERSE 15]
'There's Jerusalem and Madagascar,
And North and South Amerikee:

[VERSE 16]
'There's the British flag a-riding at anchor,
With Admiral Napier, K.C.B.'

[VERSE 17]
So when they got aboard of the Admiral's,
He hanged fat Jack and flogged Jimmee:

[VERSE 18]
But as for little Bill, he made him
The Captain of a Seventy-three.
