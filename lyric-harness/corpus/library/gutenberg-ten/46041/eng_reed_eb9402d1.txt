--- TITLE: Hear, O sinner! mercy hails you [item: PG46041-316]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c316
--- AUTHOR: Reed.

[VERSE 1]
Hear, O sinner! mercy hails you,
Now with sweetest voice she calls;
Bids you haste to seek the Saviour,
Ere the hand of Justice falls:
Trust in Jesus;
’Tis the voice of mercy calls.

[VERSE 2]
Haste, O sinner! to the Saviour—
Seek his mercy while you may;
Soon the day of grace is over;
Soon your life will pass away!
Haste to Jesus;
You must perish if you stay.

--- TITLE: Ye saints, your music bring [item: PG46041-545]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c545
--- AUTHOR: Reed.

[VERSE 1]
Ye saints, your music bring,
Attuned to sweetest sound,
Strike every trembling string,
Till earth and heaven resound;
The triumphs of the cross we sing;
Awake, ye saints, each joyful string.

[VERSE 2]
The cross, the cross alone,
Subdued the powers of hell;
Like lightning from his throne
The prince of darkness fell,
The triumphs of the cross we sing,
Awake, ye saints, each joyful string.

[VERSE 3]
The cross hath power to save
From all the foes that rise;
The cross hath made the grave
A passage to the skies;
The triumphs of the cross we sing;
Awake, ye saints, each joyful string.

--- TITLE: O that I could for ever dwell [item: PG46041-981]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c981
--- AUTHOR: Reed.

[VERSE 1]
O that I could for ever dwell,
Delighted, at the Saviour’s feet;
Behold the form I love so well,
And all his tender words repeat!

[VERSE 2]
The world shut out from all my soul,
And heaven brought in with all its bliss,
O! is there aught from pole to pole,
One moment to compare with this?

[VERSE 3]
This is the hidden life I prize—
A life of penitential love;
When I my follies most despise,
And raise my highest thoughts above?

[VERSE 4]
When all I am I clearly see,
And freely own with deepest shame;
When the Redeemer’s love to me
Kindles within a deathless flame.

[VERSE 5]
Thus would I live till nature fail,
And all my former sins forsake;
Then rise to God within the vail,
And of eternal joys partake.
