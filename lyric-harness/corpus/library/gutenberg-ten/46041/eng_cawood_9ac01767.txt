--- TITLE: Hark! what mean those holy voices [item: PG46041-135]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c135
--- AUTHOR: Cawood.

[VERSE 1]
Hark! what mean those holy voices,
Sweetly sounding through the skies?
Lo! th’ angelic host rejoices!
Heavenly hallelujahs rise.

[VERSE 2]
Hear them tell the wondrous story,
Hear them chant in hymns of joy—
“Glory to the highest, glory!
Glory be to God most high!

[VERSE 3]
“Peace on earth, good-will from heaven,
Reaching far as man is found;
Souls redeemed and sins forgiven!”
Loud our golden harps shall sound.

[VERSE 4]
“Christ is born, the great anointed;
Heaven and earth his praises sing;
O receive whom God appointed,
For your Prophet, Priest, and King!

[VERSE 5]
“Haste, ye mortals, to adore him;
Learn his name, and taste his joy;
Till in heaven ye sing before him—
“‘Glory be to God most high!’”
