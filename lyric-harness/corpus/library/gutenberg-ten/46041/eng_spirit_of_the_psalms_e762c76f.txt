--- TITLE: Bright was the guiding star that led [item: PG46041-229]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c229
--- AUTHOR: Spirit of the Psalms.

[VERSE 1]
Bright was the guiding star that led,
With mild, benignant ray,
The Gentiles to the lowly shed
Where the Redeemer lay.

[VERSE 2]
But, lo! a brighter, clearer light
Now points to his abode;
It shines through sin and sorrow’s night
To guide us to our God.

[VERSE 3]
O haste to follow where it leads;
The gracious call obey,
Be rugged wilds or flowery meads
The Christian’s destined way.

[VERSE 4]
O gladly tread the narrow path
While light and grace are given:
Who meekly follow Christ on earth,
Shall reign with him in heaven.
