--- TITLE: Almighty Father of mankind [item: PG46041-87]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c87
--- AUTHOR: Logan.

[VERSE 1]
Almighty Father of mankind!
On thee my hopes remain;
And when the day of trouble comes,
I shall not trust in vain.

[VERSE 2]
In early years, thou wast my guide,
And of my youth the friend;
And, as my days began with thee,
With thee my days shall end.

[VERSE 3]
I know the Power in whom I trust,
The arm on which I lean;
He will my Saviour ever be,
Who has my Saviour been.

[VERSE 4]
Thou wilt not cast me off, when age
And evil days descend;
Thou wilt not leave me in despair,
To mourn my latter end.

[VERSE 5]
Therefore, in life I’ll trust in thee;
In death I will adore;
And after death will sing thy praise,
When time shall be no more.

--- TITLE: The hour of my departure’s come [item: PG46041-1051]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1051
--- AUTHOR: Logan.

[VERSE 1]
The hour of my departure’s come;
I hear the voice that calls me home;
At last, O Lord! let troubles cease,
And let thy servant die in peace.

[VERSE 2]
The race appointed I have run,
The combat’s o’er, the prize is won;
And now my witness is on high,
And now my record’s in the sky.

[VERSE 3]
Not in mine innocence I trust;
I bow before thee in the dust;
And through my Saviour’s blood alone
I look for mercy at thy throne.

[VERSE 4]
I come, I come at thy command;
I give my spirit to thy hand;
Stretch forth thine everlasting arms,
And shield me in the last alarms.
