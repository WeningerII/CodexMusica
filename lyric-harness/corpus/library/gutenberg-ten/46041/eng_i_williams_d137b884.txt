--- TITLE: The child leans on its parent’s breast [item: PG46041-794]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c794
--- AUTHOR: I. Williams.

[VERSE 1]
The child leans on its parent’s breast,
Leaves there its cares, and is at rest;
The bird sits singing by his nest,
And tells aloud
His trust in God, and so is blest
’Neath every cloud.

[VERSE 2]
He has no store, he sows no seed;
Yet sings aloud, and doth not heed;
By flowing stream or grassy mead,
He sings to shame
Men, who forget, in fear of need,
A Father’s name.

[VERSE 3]
The heart that trusts for ever sings,
And feels as light as it had wings;
A well of peace within it springs:
Come good or ill,
Whate’er to-day, to-morrow brings,
It is his will!

--- TITLE: The chariot! the chariot! its wheels roll in fire [item: PG46041-1111]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1111
--- AUTHOR: I. Williams.

[VERSE 1]
The chariot! the chariot! its wheels roll in fire,
As the Lord cometh down in the pomp of his ire;
Lo! self-moving, it drives on its pathway of cloud;
And the heavens with the burden of Godhead are bowed.

[VERSE 2]
The glory! the glory! around him are poured
Mighty hosts of the angels that wait on the Lord;
And the glorified saints, and the martyrs are there,
And there, all who the palm-wreaths of victory wear!

[VERSE 3]
The trumpet! the trumpet! the dead have all heard;
Lo! the depths of the stone-covered charnel are stirred!
From the sea, from the earth, from the south, from the north,
All the vast generations of men are come forth.

[VERSE 4]
The judgment! the judgment! the thrones are all set,
Where the lamb and the bright-crownéd elders are met!
There all flesh is at once in the sight of the Lord,
And the doom of eternity hangs on his word.
