--- TITLE: Let us with a joyful mind [item: PG46041-97]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c97
--- AUTHOR: Milton.

[VERSE 1]
Let us with a joyful mind
Praise the Lord, for he is kind;
For his mercies shall endure,
Ever faithful, ever sure.
Let us sound his name abroad,
For of gods he is the God
Who by wisdom did create
Heaven’s expanse and all its state;

[VERSE 2]
Did the solid earth ordain
How to rise above the main;
Who, by his commanding might,
Filled the new-made world with light;
Caused the golden-tresséd sun
All the day his course to run;
And the moon to shine by night,
’Mid her spangled sisters bright.

[VERSE 3]
All his creatures God doth feed,
His full hand supplies their need;
Let us therefore warble forth
His high majesty and worth.
He his mansion hath on high,
’Bove the reach of mortal eye;
And his mercies shall endure,
Ever faithful, ever sure.

--- TITLE: Lord, Lord, defend the desolate [item: PG46041-1262]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1262
--- AUTHOR: Milton.

[VERSE 1]
Lord, Lord, defend the desolate,
And rescue from the hands
Of wicked men the low estate,
Of him that help demands.

[VERSE 2]
Visit the weak and fatherless,
Defend the poor man’s cause,
And raise the man in deep distress
By just and equal laws.

[VERSE 3]
Yea, Lord, judge thou the world in might,
The wrongs of earth redress;
For thou art he who shall by right,
The nations all possess.
