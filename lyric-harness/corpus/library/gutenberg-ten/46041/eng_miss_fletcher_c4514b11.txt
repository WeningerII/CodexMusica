--- TITLE: Think gently of the erring one [item: PG46041-490]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c490
--- AUTHOR: Miss Fletcher.

[VERSE 1]
Think gently of the erring one!
O, let us not forget,
However darkly stained by sin,
He is our brother yet.

[VERSE 2]
Heir of the same inheritance,
Child of the self-same God,
He hath but stumbled in the path
We have in weakness trod.

[VERSE 3]
Speak gently to the erring ones!
We yet may lead them back,
With holy words and tones of love,
From misery’s thorny track.

[VERSE 4]
Forget not, brother, thou hast sinned,
And sinful yet may be;
Deal gently with the erring heart,
As God hath dealt with thee.
