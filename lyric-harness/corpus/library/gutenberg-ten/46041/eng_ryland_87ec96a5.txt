--- TITLE: In all my Lord’s appointed ways [item: PG46041-380]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c380
--- AUTHOR: Ryland.

[VERSE 1]
In all my Lord’s appointed ways,
My journey I’ll pursue;
Hinder me not, you much-loved saints,
For I must go with you.

[VERSE 2]
Through floods and flames, if Jesus lead,
I’ll follow where he goes;
Hinder me not, shall be my cry,
Though earth and hell oppose.

[VERSE 3]
Through trials and through sufferings too,
I’ll go at his command;
Hinder me not, for I am bound
To my Immanuel’s land.

[VERSE 4]
And when my Saviour calls me home,
Still this my cry shall be—
Hinder me not—come, welcome death—
I’ll gladly go with thee.

--- TITLE: Sovereign Ruler of the skies [item: PG46041-919]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c919
--- AUTHOR: Ryland.

[VERSE 1]
Sovereign Ruler of the skies,
Ever gracious, ever wise!
All my times are in thy hand;
All events at thy command.

[VERSE 2]
Times of sickness, times of health,
Times of penury and wealth—
All must come, and last, and end,
As shall please my heavenly Friend.

[VERSE 3]
O thou gracious, wise and just!
In thy hands my life I trust;
Have I somewhat dearer still?—
I resign it to thy will.

[VERSE 4]
Thee at all times will I bless;
Having thee, I all possess:
Ne’er can I bereavéd be,
While I do not part with thee.
