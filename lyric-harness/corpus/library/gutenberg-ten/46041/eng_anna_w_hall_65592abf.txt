--- TITLE: Father, O hear me now [item: PG46041-925]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c925
--- AUTHOR: Anna W. Hall.

[VERSE 1]
Father, O hear me now!
Father divine!
Thou, only thou, canst see
The heart’s deep agony:
Help me to say to thee
“Thy will, not mine!”

[VERSE 2]
O God! be thou my stay
In this dark hour;
Kindly each sorrow hear,
Hush every troubled fear,
Thee let me still revere,
Still own thy power.

[VERSE 3]
In thee alone I trust,
Thou Holy One!
Humbly to thee I pray
That through each troubled day
Of life, I still may say,
“Thy will be done!”
