--- TITLE: Roll on, thou mighty ocean [item: PG46041-1287]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1287
--- AUTHOR: Noel’s Coll.

[VERSE 1]
Roll on, thou mighty ocean;
And, as thy billows flow,
Bear messengers of mercy
To every land below.

[VERSE 2]
Arise, ye gales, and waft them
Safe to the destined shore,
That man may sit in darkness
And death’s deep shade no more.

[VERSE 3]
O thou eternal Ruler,
Who holdest in thine arm
The tempests of the ocean,
Protect them from all harm.

[VERSE 4]
O be thy presence with them,
Wherever they may be;
Though far from us who love them,
O be they still with thee!
