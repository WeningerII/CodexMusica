--- TITLE: Thou Grace divine, encircling all [item: PG46041-1311]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1311
--- AUTHOR: Eliza Scudder.

[VERSE 1]
Thou Grace divine, encircling all,
A soundless, shoreless sea!
Wherein at last, our souls shall fall,
O Love of God most free!

[VERSE 2]
When over dizzy steeps we go,
One soft hand blinds our eyes,
The other leads us safe and slow,
O Love of God most wise!

[VERSE 3]
And though we turn us from thy face,
And wander wide and long,
Thou holdest us still in thine embrace,
O Love of God most strong!

[VERSE 4]
The saddened heart, the restless soul,
The toilworn frame and mind,
Alike confess thy sweet control,
O Love of God most kind!

[VERSE 5]
But not alone thy care we claim,
Our wayward steps to win:
We know thee by a dearer name,
O Love of God within!

[VERSE 6]
And filled and quickened by thy breath,
Our souls are strong and free
To rise o’er sin, and fear, and death,
O Love of God, to thee!
