--- TITLE: Bright Source of everlasting love [item: PG46041-954]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c954
--- AUTHOR: Boden.

[VERSE 1]
Bright Source of everlasting love,
To thee our souls we raise;
And to thy sovereign bounty rear
A monument of praise.

[VERSE 2]
Thy mercy gilds the path of life
With every cheering ray,
Kindly restrains the rising tear,
Or wipes that tear away.

[VERSE 3]
To tents of woe, to beds of pain,
Our cheerful feet repair,
And with the gifts thy hand bestows,
Relieve the mourners there.

[VERSE 4]
The widow’s heart shall sing for joy;
The orphan shall be fed;
The hungering soul we’ll gladly point
To Christ, the living Bread.
