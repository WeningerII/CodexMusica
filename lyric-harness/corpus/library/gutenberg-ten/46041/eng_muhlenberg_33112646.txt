--- TITLE: Since o’er thy footstool here below [item: PG46041-55]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c55
--- AUTHOR: Muhlenberg.

[VERSE 1]
Since o’er thy footstool here below
Such radiant gems are strewn,
O, what magnificence must glow,
Great God, about thy throne!
So brilliant here these drops of light—
There the full ocean rolls, how bright!

[VERSE 2]
If night’s blue curtain of the sky—
With thousand stars inwrought,
Hung like a royal canopy
With glittering diamonds fraught—
Be, Lord, thy temple’s outer vail,
What splendor at the shrine must dwell!

[VERSE 3]
The dazzling sun at noonday hour—
Forth from his flaming vase
Flinging o’er earth the golden shower
Till vale and mountain blaze—
But shows, Lord, one beam of thine;
What, then, the day where thou dost shine!

[VERSE 4]
O, how shall these dim eyes endure
That noon of living rays!
Or how our spirits, so impure,
Upon thy glory gaze!
Anoint, Lord, anoint our sight,
And fit us for that world of light.

--- TITLE: Like Noah’s weary dove [item: PG46041-456]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c456
--- AUTHOR: Muhlenberg.

[VERSE 1]
Like Noah’s weary dove,
That soared the earth around,
But not a resting-place above
The cheerless waters found;

[VERSE 2]
O cease, my wandering soul,
On restless wing to roam;
All the wide world, to either pole,
Has not for thee a home.

[VERSE 3]
Behold the ark of God,
Behold the open door;
Hasten to gain that dear abode,
And rove, my soul, no more.

[VERSE 4]
There safe thou shalt abide,
There sweet shall be thy rest,
And every longing satisfied,
With full salvation blest.

[VERSE 5]
And when the waves of ire,
Again the earth shall fill,
The ark shall ride the sea of fire;
Then rest on Zion’s hill.

--- TITLE: I would not live alway: I ask not to stay [item: PG46041-836]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c836
--- AUTHOR: Muhlenberg.

[VERSE 1]
I would not live alway: I ask not to stay
Where storm after storm rises dark o’er the way;
The few cloudy mornings that dawn on us here
Are enough for life’s woes, full enough for its cheer.

[VERSE 2]
I would not live alway: no, welcome the tomb;
Since Jesus has lain there, I dread not its gloom;
There sweet be my rest, till he bid me arise
To hail him in triumph descending the skies.

[VERSE 3]
Who, who would live alway, away from his God,
Away from yon heaven, that blissful abode,
Where the rivers of pleasure flow o’er the bright plains,
And the noontide of glory eternally reigns;

[VERSE 4]
Where the saints of all ages in harmony meet,
Their Saviour and brethren transported to greet,
While the anthems of rapture unceasingly roll,
And the smile of the Lord is the feast of the soul!
