--- TITLE: O sweetly breathe the lyres above [item: PG46041-399]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c399
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
O sweetly breathe the lyres above,
When angels touch the quivering string,
And wake, to chant Immanuel’s love,
Such strains as angel-lips can sing!

[VERSE 2]
And sweet, on earth, the choral swell,
From mortal tongues, of gladsome lays;
When pardoned souls their raptures tell,
And, grateful, hymn Immanuel’s praise.

[VERSE 3]
Jesus, thy name our souls adore;
We own the bond that makes us thine;
And carnal joys, that charmed before,
For thy dear sake we now resign.

[VERSE 4]
Our hearts, by dying love subdued,
Accept thine offered grace to-day;
Beneath the cross, with blood bedewed,
We bow, and give ourselves away.

[VERSE 5]
In thee we trust—on thee rely;
Though we are feeble, thou art strong;
O, keep us till our spirits fly
To join the bright, immortal throng!

--- TITLE: Away from earth my spirit turns [item: PG46041-518]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c518
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
Away from earth my spirit turns—
Away from every transient good:
With strong desire my bosom burns
To feast on heaven’s diviner food.

[VERSE 2]
Thou, Saviour, art the living bread;
Thou wilt my every want supply;
By thee sustained, and cheered, and led,
I’ll press through dangers to the sky.

[VERSE 3]
What though temptations oft distress,
And sin assails, and breaks my peace;
Thou wilt uphold, and save, and bless,
And bid the storms of passion cease.

[VERSE 4]
Then let me take thy gracious hand,
And walk beside thee onward still;
Till my glad feet shall safely stand
Forever firm on Zion’s hill.

--- TITLE: Come, O thou mighty Saviour [item: PG46041-598]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c598
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
Come, O thou mighty Saviour,
We look for thine appearing;
Descend, we pray,
Thy love display,
Our waiting spirits cheering.

[VERSE 2]
Come, clothed with glorious power;
Let all thy saints adore thee,
And let thy word,
The Spirit’s sword,
Subdue thy foes before thee.

[VERSE 3]
May every heart with gladness,
Thine offered grace receiving,
Now cease from sin,
And pure within,
Have peace, in thee believing.

[VERSE 4]
Then, when thou comest to judgment,
On flying clouds descending,
May we rejoice
When, at thy voice,
The solid earth is rending.

--- TITLE: Before thy throne with tearful eyes [item: PG46041-941]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c941
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
Before thy throne with tearful eyes,
My gracious Lord, I humbly fall;
To thee my weary spirit flies,
For thy forgiving love I call.

[VERSE 2]
How free thy mercy overflows,
When sinners on thy grace rely!
Thy tender love no limit knows;
O, save me—justly doomed to die!

[VERSE 3]
Yes! thou wilt save; my soul is free!
The gloom of sin is fled away;
My tongue breaks forth in praise to thee,
And all my powers thy word obey.

[VERSE 4]
Hence while I wrestle with my foes—
The world, the flesh, the hosts of hell—
Sustain thou me till conflicts close,
Then endless songs my thanks shall tell.

--- TITLE: And is there, Lord, a rest [item: PG46041-1133]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1133
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
And is there, Lord, a rest
For weary souls designed,
Where not a care shall stir the breast,
Or sorrow entrance find?

[VERSE 2]
Is there a blissful home,
Where kindred minds shall meet,
And live, and love, nor ever roam
From that serene retreat?

[VERSE 3]
Are their bright, happy fields,
Where nought that blooms shall die;
Where each new scene fresh pleasure yields,
And healthful breezes sigh?

[VERSE 4]
Are there celestial streams,
Where living waters glide,
With murmurs sweet as angel dreams,
And flowery banks beside?

[VERSE 5]
For ever blesséd they
Whose joyful feet shall stand,
While endless ages waste away,
Amid that glorious land!

[VERSE 6]
My soul would thither tend
While toilsome years are given;
Then let me, gracious Lord, ascend
To sweet repose in heaven!

--- TITLE: Wake thee, O Zion! thy mourning is ended [item: PG46041-1284]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1284
--- AUTHOR: Palmer.

[VERSE 1]
Wake thee, O Zion! thy mourning is ended;
God—thine own God—hath regarded thy prayer;
Wake thee, and hail him in glory descended,
Thy darkness to scatter—thy wastes to repair.

[VERSE 2]
Wake thee, O Zion! his spirit of power
To newness of life is awaking the dead;
Array thee in beauty, and greet the glad hour
That brings thee salvation, through Jesus who bled.

[VERSE 3]
Saviour, we gladly, with voices resounding
Loud as the thunder, our chorus would swell:
Till from rock, wood and mountain, its echoes rebounding,
To all the wide world of salvation shall tell.
