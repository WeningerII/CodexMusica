--- TITLE: How beauteous were the marks divine [item: PG46041-144]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c144
--- AUTHOR: A. C. Coxe.

[VERSE 1]
How beauteous were the marks divine,
That in thy meekness used to shine;
That lit thy lonely pathway, trod
In wondrous love, O Son of God!

[VERSE 2]
O, who like thee—so calm, so bright,
So pure, so made to live in light?
O, who like thee did ever go
So patient through a world of woe?

[VERSE 3]
O, who like thee so humbly bore
The scorn, the scoffs of men, before?
So meek, forgiving, godlike, high,
So glorious in humility?

[VERSE 4]
The bending angels stooped to see,
The lisping infant clasp thy knee,
And smile, as in a father’s eye,
Upon thy mild divinity.

[VERSE 5]
And death, which sets the prisoner free,
Was pang, and scoff, and scorn to thee;
Yet love through all thy torture glowed,
And mercy with thy life-blood flowed.

[VERSE 6]
O, in thy light be mine to go,
Illuming all my way of woe;
And give me ever on the road
To trace thy footsteps, Son of God!

--- TITLE: O where are kings and empires now [item: PG46041-451]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c451
--- AUTHOR: A. C. Coxe.

[VERSE 1]
O where are kings and empires now,
Of old that went and came?
But holy Church is praying yet,
A thousand years the same.

[VERSE 2]
Mark ye her holy battlements,
And her foundations strong:
And hear within, the solemn voice,
And her unending song.

[VERSE 3]
For not like kingdoms of the world,
The Holy Church of God!
Though earthquake shocks are rocking her,
And tempests are abroad;

[VERSE 4]
Unshaken as eternal hills,
Unmovable she stands—
A mountain that shall fill the earth,
A fane unbuilt by hands.

--- TITLE: We are living, we are dwelling [item: PG46041-1277]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1277
--- AUTHOR: A. C. Coxe.

[VERSE 1]
We are living, we are dwelling
In a grand and awful time,
In an age on ages telling;
To be living is sublime.

[VERSE 2]
Hark! the onset! will ye fold your
Faith-clad arms in lazy lock?
Up! O, up! thou drowsy soldier;
Worlds are charging to the shock.

[VERSE 3]
Worlds are charging, heaven beholding;
Thou hast but an hour to fight;
Now, the blazoned cross unfolding,
On! right onward for the right.

[VERSE 4]
On! let all the soul within you
For the truth’s sake go abroad:
Strike! let every nerve and sinew
Tell on ages—tell for God.
