--- TITLE: Great God! thy penetrating eye [item: PG46041-40]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c40
--- AUTHOR: E. Scott.

[VERSE 1]
Great God! thy penetrating eye
Pervades my inmost powers;
With awe profound my wondering soul
Falls prostrate and adores.

[VERSE 2]
To be encompassed round with God,
The Holy and the Just,
Armed with omnipotence to save,
Or crush me to the dust—

[VERSE 3]
O how tremendous is the thought!
Deep may it be impressed,
And may thy Spirit firmly ’grave
This truth within my breast.

[VERSE 4]
Begirt with thee, my fearless soul
The gloomy vale shall tread;
And thou wilt bind th’ immortal crown
Of glory on my head.

--- TITLE: See how the rising sun [item: PG46041-1184]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1184
--- AUTHOR: E. Scott.

[VERSE 1]
See how the rising sun
Pursues his shining way;
And wide proclaims his Maker’s praise,
With every brightening ray.

[VERSE 2]
Thus would my rising soul
Its heavenly parent sing;
And to its great Original
An humble tribute bring.

[VERSE 3]
O may I grateful use
The blessings I receive;
And ne’er in thought, in word, or deed,
His holy Spirit grieve.

[VERSE 4]
May all my days and powers
Be sacred, Lord, to thee:
And in thy presence may I spend
A blest eternity!
