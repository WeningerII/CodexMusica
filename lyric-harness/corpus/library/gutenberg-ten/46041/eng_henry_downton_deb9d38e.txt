--- TITLE: For thy mercy and thy grace [item: PG46041-1244]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1244
--- AUTHOR: Henry Downton.

[VERSE 1]
For thy mercy and thy grace,
Faithful through another year,
Hear our song of thankfulness,
Father, and Redeemer, hear!

[VERSE 2]
In our weakness and distress,
Rock of strength! be thou our stay!
In the pathless wilderness
Be our true and living way!

[VERSE 3]
Who of us death’s awful road
In the coming year shall tread?
With thy rod and staff, O God!
Comfort thou his dying head!

[VERSE 4]
Keep us faithful, keep us pure,
Keep us evermore thine own!
Help, O help us to endure!
Fit us for the promised crown!

[VERSE 5]
So, within thy palace gate,
We shall praise, on golden strings,
Thee, the only Potentate,
Lord of lords, and King of kings!
