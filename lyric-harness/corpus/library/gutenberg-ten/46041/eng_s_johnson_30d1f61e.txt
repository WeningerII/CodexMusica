--- TITLE: Father, in thy mysterious presence kneeling [item: PG46041-584]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c584
--- AUTHOR: S. Johnson.

[VERSE 1]
Father, in thy mysterious presence kneeling,
Fain would our souls feel all thy kindling love,
For we are weak, and need some deep revealing
Of trust, and strength, and calmness, from above.

[VERSE 2]
Lord, we have wandered forth thro’ doubt and sorrow,
And thou hast made each step an onward one;
And we will ever trust each unknown morrow—
Thou wilt sustain us till its work is done.

[VERSE 3]
In the heart’s depths, a peace serene and holy
Abides, and when pain seems to have her will,
Or we despair—O may that peace rise slowly,
Stronger than agony, and we be still.

[VERSE 4]
Now, Father, now, in thy dear presence kneeling,
Our spirits yearn to feel thy kindling love:
Now make us strong, we need thy deep revealing
Of trust, and strength, and calmness, from above.

--- TITLE: Onward, Christian, though the region [item: PG46041-885]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c885
--- AUTHOR: S. Johnson.

[VERSE 1]
Onward, Christian, though the region
Where thou art be drear and lone,
God hath set a guardian legion
Very near thee—press thou on!

[VERSE 2]
Listen, Christian, their hosanna
Rolleth o’er thee—“God is love,”
Write upon thy red-cross banner,
“Upward ever—heaven’s above.”

[VERSE 3]
By the thorn-road, and none other,
Is the mount of vision won;
Tread it without shrinking, brother!
Jesus trod it—press thou on!

[VERSE 4]
By thy trustful, calm endeavor,
Guiding, cheering, like the sun,
Earth-bound hearts thou shalt deliver;
O, for their sake, press thou on!

[VERSE 5]
Be this world the wiser, stronger,
For thy life of pain and peace;
While it needs thee, O no longer
Pray thou for thy quick release:

[VERSE 6]
Pray thou, Christian, daily, rather,
That thou be a faithful son;
By the prayer of Jesus—“Father,
Not my will, but thine, be done!”
