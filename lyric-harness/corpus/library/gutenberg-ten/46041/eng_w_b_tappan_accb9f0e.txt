--- TITLE: ’Tis midnight; and on Olive’s brow [item: PG46041-159]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c159
--- AUTHOR: W. B. Tappan.

[VERSE 1]
’Tis midnight; and on Olive’s brow
The star is dimmed that lately shone;
’Tis midnight; in the garden now,
The suffering Saviour prays alone.

[VERSE 2]
’Tis midnight; and, from all removed,
The Saviour wrestles lone, with fears;
E’en that disciple whom he loved
Heeds not his Master’s grief and tears.

[VERSE 3]
’Tis midnight; and for others’ guilt
The man of sorrows weeps in blood;
Yet he that hath in anguish knelt
Is not forsaken by his God.

[VERSE 4]
’Tis midnight; from the heavenly plains
Is borne the song that angels know;
Unheard by mortals are the strains
That sweetly soothe the Saviour’s woe.

--- TITLE: There is an hour of hallowed peace [item: PG46041-1126]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1126
--- AUTHOR: W. B. Tappan.

[VERSE 1]
There is an hour of hallowed peace
For those with care oppressed,
When sighs and sorrowing tears shall cease,
And all be hushed to rest.

[VERSE 2]
’Tis then the soul is freed from fears
And doubts which here annoy;
Then they that oft had sown in tears,
Shall reap again in joy.

[VERSE 3]
There is a home of sweet repose,
Where storms assail no more;
The stream of endless pleasure flows,
On that celestial shore.

[VERSE 4]
There purity with love appears,
And bliss without alloy;
There they that oft had sown in tears
Shall reap again in joy.

--- TITLE: There is an hour of peaceful rest [item: PG46041-1130]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1130
--- AUTHOR: W. B. Tappan.

[VERSE 1]
There is an hour of peaceful rest,
To mourning wanderers given;
There is a tear for souls distressed,
A balm for every wounded breast—
’Tis found above—in heaven.

[VERSE 2]
There is a home for weary souls,
By sins and sorrows driven;
When tossed on life’s tempestuous shoals,
Where storms arise and ocean rolls,
And all is drear—but heaven.

[VERSE 3]
There faith lifts up the tearless eye,
The heart with anguish riven;
It views the tempest passing by,
Sees evening shadows quickly fly,
And all serene—in heaven.

[VERSE 4]
There fragrant flowers immortal bloom,
And joys supreme are given;
There rays divine disperse the gloom;
Beyond the dark and narrow tomb
Appears the dawn—of heaven.
