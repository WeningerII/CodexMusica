--- TITLE: Ye Christian heralds! go, proclaim [item: PG46041-465]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c465
--- AUTHOR: Winchell’s Sel.

[VERSE 1]
Ye Christian heralds! go, proclaim
Salvation through Immanuel’s name;
To distant climes the tidings bear,
And plant the rose of Sharon there.

[VERSE 2]
He’ll shield you with a wall of a fire,
With holy zeal your hearts inspire,
Bid raging winds their fury cease,
And hush the tempest into peace.

[VERSE 3]
And when our labors all are o’er,
Then we shall meet to part no more—
Meet with the blood-bought throng, to fall,
And crown our Jesus—Lord of all!
