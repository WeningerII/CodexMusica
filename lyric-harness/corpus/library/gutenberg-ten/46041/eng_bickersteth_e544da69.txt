--- TITLE: O Jesus, Saviour of the lost [item: PG46041-559]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c559
--- AUTHOR: Bickersteth.

[VERSE 1]
O Jesus, Saviour of the lost,
My Rock and Hiding-place,
By storms of sin and sorrow tost,
I seek thy sheltering grace.

[VERSE 2]
Guilty, forgive me, Lord! I cry;
Pursued by foes, I come;
A sinner, save me, or I die;
An outcast, take me home.

[VERSE 3]
Once safe in thine almighty arms,
Let storms come on amain;
There danger never, never harms;
There death itself is gain.

[VERSE 4]
And when I stand before thy throne
And all thy glory see,
Still be my righteousness alone
To hide myself in thee.
