--- TITLE: Lamp of our feet! whereby we trace [item: PG46041-16]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c16
--- AUTHOR: Barton.

[VERSE 1]
Lamp of our feet! whereby we trace
Our path when wont to stray;
Stream from the fount of heavenly grace!
Brook by the traveler’s way!

[VERSE 2]
Bread of our souls, whereon we feed!
True manna from on high!
Our guide and chart! wherein we read
Of realms beyond the sky.

[VERSE 3]
Pillar of fire through watches dark,
And radiant cloud by day!
When waves would whelm our tossing bark,
Our anchor and our stay!

[VERSE 4]
Word of the everlasting God!
Will of his glorious Son!
Without thee how could earth be trod,
Or heaven itself be won?

--- TITLE: Around Bethesda’s healing wave [item: PG46041-349]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c349
--- AUTHOR: Barton.

[VERSE 1]
Around Bethesda’s healing wave,
Waiting to hear the rustling wind
Which spoke the angel nigh, who gave
Its virtue to that holy spring,
With patience and with hope endued,
Were seen the gathered multitude.

[VERSE 2]
Bethesda’s pool has lost its power!
No angel, by his glad descent
Dispenses that diviner dower
Which with its healing waters went;
But he, whose word surpassed its wave,
Is still omnipotent to save.

[VERSE 3]
Saviour! thy love is still the same
As when that healing word was spoke;
Still in thine all-redeeming name
Dwells power to burst the strongest yoke!
O, be that power, that love, displayed,
Help those whom thou alone canst aid.

--- TITLE: Our pathway oft is wet with tears [item: PG46041-1222]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1222
--- AUTHOR: Barton.

[VERSE 1]
Our pathway oft is wet with tears,
Our sky with clouds o’ercast,
And worldly cares and worldly fears
Go with us to the last;—
Not to the last! God’s word hath said,
Could we but read aright:
O pilgrim! lift in hope thy head—
At eve it shall be light!

[VERSE 2]
Though earth-born shadows now may shroud
Our toilsome path awhile,
God’s blesséd word can part each cloud,
And bid the sunshine smile.
If we but trust in living faith,
His love and power divine,
Then, though our sun may set in death,
His light shall round us shine.

[VERSE 3]
When tempest-clouds are dark on high,
His bow of love and peace
Shines beauteous in the vaulted sky—
A pledge that storms shall cease.
Then keep we on with hope unchilled,
By faith and not by sight,
And we shall own his word fulfilled—
At eve it shall be light.
