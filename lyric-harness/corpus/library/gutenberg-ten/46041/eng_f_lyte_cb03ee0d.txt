--- TITLE: Praise the Lord, his glories show [item: PG46041-58]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c58
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Praise the Lord, his glories show,
Saints within his courts below,
Angels round his throne above,
All that see and share his love!

[VERSE 2]
Earth to heaven, and heaven to earth,
Tell his wonders, sing his worth;
Age to age, and shore to shore,
Praise him, praise him, evermore!

[VERSE 3]
Praise the Lord, his mercies trace;
Praise his providence and grace—
All that he for man hath done,
All he sends us through his Son.

[VERSE 4]
Strings and voices, hands and hearts,
In the concert bear your parts:
All that breathe, your Lord adore;
Praise him, praise him, evermore!

--- TITLE: Praise, my soul, the King of heaven [item: PG46041-101]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c101
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Praise, my soul, the King of heaven;
To his feet thy tribute bring;
Ransomed, healed, restored, forgiven,
Who like me his praise should sing?
Praise him! praise him!
Praise the everlasting King!

[VERSE 2]
Praise him for his grace and favor
To our fathers in distress;
Praise him, still the same for ever:
Slow to chide, and swift to bless;
Praise him! praise him!
Glorious in his faithfulness!

[VERSE 3]
Father-like he tends and spares us;
Well our feeble frame he knows;
In his hands he gently bears us—
Rescues us from all our foes;
Praise him! praise him!
Widely as his mercy flows!

[VERSE 4]
Angels, help us to adore him:
Ye behold him face to face;
Sun and moon, bow down before him;
Dwellers all in time and space,
Praise him! praise him!
Praise with us the God of grace!

--- TITLE: Arise, ye people, and adore [item: PG46041-199]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c199
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Arise, ye people, and adore,
Exulting strike the chord;
Let all the earth, from shore to shore,
Confess th’ almighty Lord.

[VERSE 2]
Glad shouts aloud—wide echoing round,
Th’ ascending Lord proclaim;
The angelic choir respond the sound,
And shake creation’s frame.

[VERSE 3]
They sing of death and hell o’erthrown
In that triumphant hour;
And God exalts his conquering Son
To his right hand of power.

[VERSE 4]
O shout, ye people, and adore,
Exulting strike the chord;
Let all the earth, from shore to shore,
Confess th’ almighty Lord.

--- TITLE: Redeemed from guilt, redeemed from fears [item: PG46041-401]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c401
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Redeemed from guilt, redeemed from fears,
My soul enlarged, and dried my tears,
What can I do, O Love Divine,
What to repay such gifts as thine?

[VERSE 2]
What can I do, so poor, so weak,
But from thy hands new blessings seek,
A heart to feel thy mercies more,
A soul to know thee, and adore?

[VERSE 3]
O teach me at thy feet to fall,
And yield thee up myself, my all!
Before thy saints my debts to own,
And live and die to thee alone!

[VERSE 4]
Thy Spirit, Lord, at large impart,
Expand, and raise, and fill my heart!
So may I hope my life shall be
Some faint return, O Lord, to thee.

--- TITLE: Hasten, Lord! the glorious time [item: PG46041-599]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c599
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Hasten, Lord! the glorious time,
When, beneath Messiah’s sway,
Every nation, every clime,
Shall the gospel call obey.

[VERSE 2]
Mightiest kings his power shall own,
Heathen tribes his name adore;
Satan and his host, o’erthrown,
Bound in chains shall hurt no more.

[VERSE 3]
Then shall wars and tumults cease,
Then be banished grief and pain;
Righteousness, and joy, and peace,
Undisturbed shall ever reign.

[VERSE 4]
Bless we, then, our gracious Lord!
Ever praise his glorious name;
All his mighty acts record,
All his wondrous love proclaim.

--- TITLE: Sweet is the task, O Lord [item: PG46041-625]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c625
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Sweet is the task, O Lord,
Thy glorious acts to sing,
To praise thy name, and hear thy word,
And grateful offerings bring.

[VERSE 2]
Sweet, at the dawning hour,
Thy boundless love to tell;
And when the night-wind shuts the flower,
Still on the theme to dwell.

[VERSE 3]
Sweet, on this day of rest,
To join in heart and voice
With those who love and serve thee best,
And in thy name rejoice.

[VERSE 4]
To songs of praise and joy,
May all our days be given,
That such may be our best employ
Eternally in heaven.

--- TITLE: My spirit on thy care [item: PG46041-779]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c779
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
My spirit on thy care,
Blest Saviour, I recline;
Thou wilt not leave me to despair,
For thou art love divine.

[VERSE 2]
In thee I place my trust;
On thee I calmly rest:
I know thee good, I know thee just,
And count thy choice the best.

[VERSE 3]
Whate’er events betide,
Thy will they all perform;
Safe in thy breast my head I hide,
Nor fear the coming storm.

[VERSE 4]
Let good or ill befall,
It must be good for me—
Secure of having thee in all
Of having all in thee.

--- TITLE: Vainly through night’s weary hours [item: PG46041-783]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c783
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Vainly through night’s weary hours,
Keep we watch lest foes alarm;
Vain our bulwarks, and our towers,
But for God’s protecting arm.

[VERSE 2]
Vain were all our toil and labor,
Did not God that labor bless;
Vain, without his grace and favor,
Every talent we possess.

[VERSE 3]
Vainer still the hope of heaven
That on human strength relies;
But to him shall help be given
Who in humble faith applies.

[VERSE 4]
Seek we, then, the Lord’s Anointed;
He shall grant us peace and rest:
Ne’er was suppliant disappointed
Who through Christ his prayer addressed.

--- TITLE: My rest is in heaven—my home is not here [item: PG46041-838]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c838
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
My rest is in heaven—my home is not here;
Then why should I murmur when trials appear?
Be hushed, my sad spirit, the worst that may come
But shortens thy journey and hastens thee home.

[VERSE 2]
A pilgrim and stranger, I seek not my bliss,
Nor lay up my treasures in regions like this;
I look for a city which hands have not piled;
I pant for a country by sin undefiled.

[VERSE 3]
Afflictions may try me, but can not destroy;
One vision of home turns them all into joy;
And the bitterest tear that flows from my eyes,
But sweetens my hope of that home in the skies.

[VERSE 4]
Though foes and temptations my progress oppose,
They only make heaven more sweet at the close;
Come joy or come sorrow—the worst may befall,
One moment in heaven will make up for all.

[VERSE 5]
The thorn and the thistle around me may grow,
I would not repose upon roses below;
I ask not my portion, I seek not my rest,
Till, seated with Jesus, I lean on his breast.

[VERSE 6]
A scrip for the way and a staff in my hand,
I march on in haste through the enemy’s land:
The road may be rough, but it can not be long:
So I’ll smooth it with hope, and I’ll cheer it with song.

--- TITLE: Gently, gently lay thy rod [item: PG46041-1022]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1022
--- AUTHOR: F. Lyte.

[VERSE 1]
Gently, gently lay thy rod
On my sinful head, O God!
Stay thy wrath, in mercy stay,
Lest I sink beneath its sway.

[VERSE 2]
Heal me, for my flesh is weak;
Heal me, for thy grace I seek;
This my only plea I make—
Heal me for thy mercy’s sake.

[VERSE 3]
Who, within the silent grave,
Shall proclaim thy power to save?
Lord! my sinking soul reprieve;
Speak, and I shall rise and live.

[VERSE 4]
Lo! he comes—he heeds my plea!
Lo! he comes—the shadows flee;
Glory round me dawns once more!
Rise, my spirit, and adore!
