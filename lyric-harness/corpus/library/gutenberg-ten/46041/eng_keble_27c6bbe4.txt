--- TITLE: Blest are the pure in heart [item: PG46041-741]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c741
--- AUTHOR: Keble.

[VERSE 1]
Blest are the pure in heart
For they shall see our God;
The secret of the Lord is theirs;
Their soul is his abode.

[VERSE 2]
Still to the lowly soul
He doth himself impart,
And for his temple and his throne
Selects the pure in heart.

--- TITLE: Sun of my soul! thou Saviour dear [item: PG46041-978]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c978
--- AUTHOR: Keble.

[VERSE 1]
Sun of my soul! thou Saviour dear,
It is not night if thou be near:
O, may no earth-born cloud arise
To hide thee from thy servant’s eyes!

[VERSE 2]
When soft the dews of kindly sleep
My wearied eyelids gently steep,
Be my last thought—how sweet to rest
For ever on my Saviour’s breast!

[VERSE 3]
Abide with me from morn till eve,
For without thee I can not live;
Abide with me when night is nigh,
For without thee I dare not die.

[VERSE 4]
Be near to bless me when I wake,
Ere through the world my way I take;
Abide with me till in thy love
I lose myself in heaven above.

--- TITLE: New every morning is the love [item: PG46041-1176]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1176
--- AUTHOR: Keble.

[VERSE 1]
New every morning is the love
Our wakening and uprising prove;
Through sleep and darkness safely brought,
Restored to life, and power, and thought.

[VERSE 2]
New mercies, each returning day,
Hover around us while we pray:
New perils past, new sins forgiven,
New thoughts of God, new hopes of heaven.

[VERSE 3]
Old friends, old scenes will lovelier be
As more of heaven in each we see;
Some softening gleam of love and prayer
Shall dawn on every cross and care.

[VERSE 4]
Only, O Lord, in thy dear love,
Fit us for perfect rest above,
And keep us this, and every day,
To live more nearly as we pray.
