--- TITLE: Shall we grow weary in our watch [item: PG46041-896]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c896
--- AUTHOR: Whittier.

[VERSE 1]
Shall we grow weary in our watch,
And murmur at the long delay,
Impatient of our Father’s time
And his appointéd way?

[VERSE 2]
O, oft a deeper test of faith
Than prison-cell, or martyr’s stake,
The self-renouncing watchfulness
Of silent prayer may make.

[VERSE 3]
We gird us bravely to rebuke
Our erring brother in the wrong;
And in the ear of pride and power
Our warning voice is strong.

[VERSE 4]
Easier to smite with Peter’s sword,
Than watch one hour in humbling prayer;
Life’s great things, like the Syrian lord,
Our hearts can do and dare.

[VERSE 5]
But, O, we shrink from Jordan’s side,
From waters which alone can save;
And murmur for Abana’s banks
And Pharpar’s brighter wave.

[VERSE 6]
O thou, who in the garden’s shade
Didst wake thy weary ones again,
Who slumbered at that fearful hour,
Forgetful of thy pain—

[VERSE 7]
Bend o’er us now, as over them,
And set our sleep-bound spirits free,
Nor leave us slumbering in the watch
Our souls should keep with thee!

--- TITLE: All as God wills! who wisely heeds [item: PG46041-904]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c904
--- AUTHOR: Whittier.

[VERSE 1]
All as God wills! who wisely heeds
To give or to withhold,
And knoweth more of all my needs
Than all my prayers have told.

[VERSE 2]
Enough that blessings undeserved
Have marked my erring track—
That wheresoe’er my feet have swerved,
His chastening turned me back—

[VERSE 3]
That more and more a Providence,
Of love is understood,
Making the springs of time and sense
Sweet with eternal good—

[VERSE 4]
That death seems but a covered way
Which opens into light,
Wherein no blinded child can stray
Beyond the Father’s sight—

[VERSE 5]
That care and trial seem at last,
Through memory’s sunset air,
Like mountain ranges overpast,
In purple distance fair—

[VERSE 6]
That all the jarring notes of life,
Seem blending in a psalm,
And all the angles of its strife
Slow rounding into calm.

[VERSE 7]
And so the shadows fall apart
And so the west winds play;
And all the windows of my heart
I open to the day.

--- TITLE: One whom Jesus loved has truly spoken [item: PG46041-972]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c972
--- AUTHOR: Whittier.

[VERSE 1]
One whom Jesus loved has truly spoken!
The holier worship which God deigns to bless,
Restores the lost, and heals the spirit broken,
And feeds the widow and the fatherless.

[VERSE 2]
Then, brother man, fold to thy heart thy brother!
For where love dwells, the peace of God is there;
To worship rightly is to love each other;
Each smile a hymn, each kindly deed a prayer.

[VERSE 3]
Follow, with reverent steps, the great example
Of him whose holy work was doing good;
So shall the wide earth seem our Father’s temple,
Each loving life a psalm of gratitude.

[VERSE 4]
Thus shall all shackles fall; the stormy clangor
Of wild war music o’er the earth shall cease;
Love shall tread out the baleful fires of anger,
And in its ashes plant the tree of peace.
