--- TITLE: Ye trembling captives! hear [item: PG46041-301]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c301
--- AUTHOR: Pratt’s Coll.

[VERSE 1]
Ye trembling captives! hear;
The gospel-trumpet sounds;
No music more can charm the ear,
Or heal your heart-felt wounds.

[VERSE 2]
’Tis not the trump of war,
Nor Sinai’s awful roar;
Salvation’s news it spreads afar,
And vengeance is no more.

[VERSE 3]
Forgiveness, love, and peace,
Glad heaven aloud proclaims;
And earth, the jubilee’s release,
With eager rapture claims.

[VERSE 4]
Far, far to distant lands
The saving news shall spread;
And Jesus all his willing bands
In glorious triumph lead.

--- TITLE: Rise, gracious God, and shine [item: PG46041-1273]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1273
--- AUTHOR: Pratt’s Coll.

[VERSE 1]
Rise, gracious God, and shine
In all thy saving might;
Now prosper every good design,
To spread thy glorious light.

[VERSE 2]
O bring the nations near
That they may sing thy praise;
Thy word let all the heathen hear,
And learn thy holy ways.

[VERSE 3]
Send forth thy glorious power;
All nations then shall see,
And earth present her grateful store,
In converts born to thee.
