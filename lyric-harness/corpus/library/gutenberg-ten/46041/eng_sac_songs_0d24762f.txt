--- TITLE: The mellow eve is gliding [item: PG46041-1200]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1200
--- AUTHOR: Sac. Songs.

[VERSE 1]
The mellow eve is gliding
Serenely down the west;
So, every care subsiding,
My soul would sink to rest.

[VERSE 2]
The woodland hum is ringing
The daylight’s gentle close;
May angels round me, singing,
Thus hymn my last repose.

[VERSE 3]
The evening star has lighted
Her crystal lamp on high;
So, when in death benighted,
May hope illume the sky.

[VERSE 4]
In golden splendor dawning,
The morrow’s light shall break;
O, on the last bright morning
May I in glory wake!
