--- TITLE: Within thy house, O Lord, our God [item: PG46041-690]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c690
--- AUTHOR: Con. Ev. Mag.

[VERSE 1]
Within thy house, O Lord, our God,
In glory now appear;
Make this a place of thine abode,
And shed thy blessings here.

[VERSE 2]
When we thy mercy-seat surround,
Thy Spirit, Lord, impart;
And let thy gospel’s joyful sound,
With power, reach every heart.

[VERSE 3]
Here let the blind their sight obtain;
Here give the mourners rest;
Let Jesus here triumphant reign,
Enthroned in every breast.

[VERSE 4]
Here let the voice of sacred joy
And humble prayer arise,
Till higher strains our tongues employ
In realms beyond the skies.
