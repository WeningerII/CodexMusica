--- TITLE: Thy goodness, Lord, our souls confess [item: PG46041-83]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c83
--- AUTHOR: Gibbons.

[VERSE 1]
Thy goodness, Lord, our souls confess;
Thy goodness we adore:
A spring whose blessings never fail;
A sea without a shore.

[VERSE 2]
Sun, moon, and stars thy love attest
In every golden ray;
Love draws the curtains of the night,
And love brings back the day.

[VERSE 3]
Thy bounty every season crowns
With all the bliss it yields,
With joyful clusters loads the vines,
With strengthening grain the fields.

[VERSE 4]
But chiefly thy compassion, Lord,
Is in the gospel seen;
There, like a sun, thy mercy shines,
Without a cloud between.

[VERSE 5]
There, pardon, peace, and holy joy,
Through Jesus’ name are given;
He on the cross was lifted high,
That we might reign in heaven.

--- TITLE: Angels! roll the rock away [item: PG46041-189]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c189
--- AUTHOR: Gibbons.

[VERSE 1]
Angels! roll the rock away;
Death! yield up thy mighty prey;
See! the Saviour leaves the tomb,
Glowing with immortal bloom.

[VERSE 2]
Hark! the wondering angels raise
Louder notes of joyful praise:
Let the earth’s remotest bound
Echo with the blissful sound.

[VERSE 3]
Now, ye saints! lift up your eyes,
See him high in glory rise!
Ranks of angels, on the road,
Hail him—the incarnate God.

[VERSE 4]
Heaven unfolds its portals wide,
See the Conqueror through them ride!
King of glory! mount thy throne—
Boundless empire is thine own.

[VERSE 5]
Praise him, ye celestial choirs!
Tune, and sweep your golden lyres:
Raise, O earth! your noblest songs,
From ten thousand thousand tongues.

--- TITLE: Forgiveness! ’tis a joyful sound [item: PG46041-395]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c395
--- AUTHOR: Gibbons.

[VERSE 1]
Forgiveness! ’tis a joyful sound
To malefactors doomed to die;
Publish the bliss the world around;
You seraphs, shout it from the sky!

[VERSE 2]
’Tis the rich gift of love divine;
’Tis full, outmeasuring every crime;
Unclouded shall its glories shine,
And feel no change by changing time.

[VERSE 3]
For this stupendous love of heaven,
What grateful honors shall we show!
Where much transgression is forgiven,
Let love in equal ardors glow.

[VERSE 4]
By this inspired, let all our days
With gospel holiness be crowned;
Let truth and goodness, prayer and praise
In all abide, in all abound.

--- TITLE: Now let our souls, on wings sublime [item: PG46041-806]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c806
--- AUTHOR: Gibbons.

[VERSE 1]
Now let our souls, on wings sublime,
Rise from the vanities of time,
Draw back the parting vail, and see
The glories of eternity.

[VERSE 2]
Born by new, celestial birth,
Why should we grovel here on earth?
Why grasp at vain and fleeting toys,
So near to heaven’s eternal joys?

[VERSE 3]
Shall aught beguile us on the road,
While we are walking back to God?
For strangers into life we come,
And dying is but going home.

[VERSE 4]
Welcome, sweet hour of full discharge,
That sets our longing souls at large,
Unbinds our chains, breaks up our cell,
And gives us with our God to dwell.

[VERSE 5]
To dwell with God, to feel his love,
Is the full heaven enjoyed above;
And the sweet expectation now
Is the young dawn of heaven below.

--- TITLE: To thee, my God, whose presence fills [item: PG46041-1002]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1002
--- AUTHOR: Gibbons.

[VERSE 1]
To thee, my God, whose presence fills
The earth, and seas, and skies,
To thee, whose name, whose heart is Love,
With all my powers I rise.

[VERSE 2]
Troubles in long succession roll;
Wave rushes upon wave;
Pity, O pity my distress!
Thy child, thy suppliant, save!

[VERSE 3]
O bid the roaring tempest cease;
Or give me strength to bear
Whate’er thy holy will appoints,
And save me from despair!

[VERSE 4]
To thee, my God, alone I look,
On thee alone confide;
Thou never hast deceived the soul
That on thy grace relied.

[VERSE 5]
Though oft thy ways are wrapt in clouds
Mysterious and unknown,
Truth, righteousness, and mercy stand,
The pillars of thy throne.
