--- TITLE: Thou dear Redeemer, dying Lamb [item: PG46041-231]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c231
--- AUTHOR: Cennick.

[VERSE 1]
Thou dear Redeemer, dying Lamb,
I love to hear of thee;
No music’s like thy charming name,
Nor half so sweet can be.

[VERSE 2]
O, may I ever hear thy voice
In mercy to me speak;
In thee, my Priest, will I rejoice,
And thy salvation seek.

[VERSE 3]
My Jesus shall be still my theme,
While on this earth I stay;
I’ll sing my Jesus’ lovely name,
When all things else decay.

--- TITLE: Jesus my all to heaven has gone [item: PG46041-375]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c375
--- AUTHOR: Cennick.

[VERSE 1]
Jesus my all to heaven has gone,
He whom I fix my hopes upon;
His path I see, and I’ll pursue
The narrow way, till him I view.

[VERSE 2]
The way the holy prophets went,
The road that leads from banishment,
The King’s highway of holiness—
I’ll go, for all his paths are peace.

--- TITLE: Children of the heavenly King [item: PG46041-498]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c498
--- AUTHOR: Cennick.

[VERSE 1]
Children of the heavenly King,
As ye journey, sweetly sing;
Sing your Saviour’s worthy praise,
Glorious in his works and ways.

[VERSE 2]
Ye are traveling home to God,
In the way the fathers trod:
They are happy now—and ye
Soon there happiness shall see.

[VERSE 3]
Shout, ye little flock, and blest;
You on Jesus’ throne shall rest:
There your seat is now prepared—
There your kingdom and reward.

[VERSE 4]
Fear not, brethren, joyful stand
On the borders of your land;
Jesus Christ, your Father’s Son,
Bids you undismayed go on.

[VERSE 5]
Lord, submissive make us go,
Gladly leaving all below;
Only thou our leader be,
And we still will follow thee.
