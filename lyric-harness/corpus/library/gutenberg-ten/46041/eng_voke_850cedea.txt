--- TITLE: You messengers of Christ [item: PG46041-470]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c470
--- AUTHOR: Voke.

[VERSE 1]
You messengers of Christ,
His sovereign voice obey;
Arise and follow where he leads—
And peace attend your way.

[VERSE 2]
The master whom you serve
Will needful strength bestow;
Depending on his promised aid,
With sacred courage go.

[VERSE 3]
Mountains shall sink to plains,
And hell in vain oppose;
The cause is God’s, and must prevail
In spite of all his foes.

[VERSE 4]
Go, spread a Saviour’s fame,
And tell his matchless grace,
To the most guilty and depraved
Of Adam’s numerous race.

[VERSE 5]
We wish you, in his name,
The most divine success;
Assured that he who sends you forth
Will your endeavors bless.
