--- TITLE: Thy way is in the sea [item: PG46041-91]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c91
--- AUTHOR: Fawcett.

[VERSE 1]
Thy way is in the sea;
Thy paths we can not trace;
Nor solve, O Lord, the mystery
Of thy unbounded grace.

[VERSE 2]
Here the dark vails of sense
Our captive souls surround;
Mysterious deeps of providence
Our wandering thoughts confound.

[VERSE 3]
As through a glass we see
The wonders of thy love;
How little do we know of thee,
Or of the joys above.

[VERSE 4]
In part we know thy will,
And bless thee for the sight;
Soon will thy love the rest reveal
In glory’s clearer light.

[VERSE 5]
With joy shall we survey
Thy providence and grace;
And spend an everlasting day
In wonder, love and praise.

--- TITLE: Infinite excellence is thine [item: PG46041-237]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c237
--- AUTHOR: Fawcett.

[VERSE 1]
Infinite excellence is thine,
Thou lovely Prince of Grace!
Thy uncreated beauties shine
With never-fading rays.

[VERSE 2]
Sinners from earth’s remotest end
Come bending at thy feet;
To thee their prayers and praise ascend,
In thee their wishes meet.

[VERSE 3]
Thy name, as precious ointment shed,
Delights the church around;
Sweetly the sacred odors spread,
And purest joys abound.

[VERSE 4]
Millions of happy spirits live
On thy exhaustless store;
From thee they all their bliss receive,
And still thou givest more.

[VERSE 5]
Thou art their triumph and their joy;
They find their all in thee;
Thy glories will their tongues employ
Through all eternity.

--- TITLE: Humble souls, who seek salvation [item: PG46041-393]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c393
--- AUTHOR: Fawcett.

[VERSE 1]
Humble souls, who seek salvation
Through the Lamb’s redeeming blood,
Hear the voice of revelation;
Tread the path that Jesus trod.

[VERSE 2]
Hear the blest Redeemer call you;
Listen to his heavenly voice;
Dread no ills that can befall you,
While you make his way your choice.

[VERSE 3]
Plainly here his footsteps tracing,
Follow him without delay,
Gladly his command embracing;
Lo! your Captain leads the way.

--- TITLE: O my soul! what means this sadness? [item: PG46041-890]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c890
--- AUTHOR: Fawcett.

[VERSE 1]
O my soul! what means this sadness?
Wherefore art thou thus cast down?
Let thy griefs be turned to gladness;
Bid thy restless fears begone;
Look to Jesus,
And rejoice in his dear name.

[VERSE 2]
What though Satan’s strong temptations
Vex and grieve thee day by day
And thy sinful inclinations
Often fill thee with dismay;
Thou shalt conquer,
Through the Lamb’s redeeming blood.

[VERSE 3]
Though ten thousand ills beset thee,
From without and from within,
Jesus saith he’ll ne’er forget thee,
But will save from hell and sin.
He is faithful
To perform his gracious word.

[VERSE 4]
Though distresses now attend thee,
And thou treadest the thorny road;
His right hand shall still defend thee;
Soon he’ll bring thee home to God,
Therefore praise him,
Praise the great Redeemer’s name.

[VERSE 5]
O that I could now adore him
Like the heavenly host above,
Who for ever bow before him,
And unceasing sing his love,
Happy songsters!
When shall I your chorus join?

--- TITLE: The God of mercy will indulge [item: PG46041-1036]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1036
--- AUTHOR: Fawcett.

[VERSE 1]
The God of mercy will indulge
The flowing tear, the heaving sigh,
When honored parents fall around,
When friends beloved and kindred die.

[VERSE 2]
Yet not one anxious murmuring thought
Should with our mourning passion blend;
Nor should our bleeding hearts forget
Their mighty, ever-living Friend.

[VERSE 3]
Parent, Protector, Guardian, Guide,
Thou art each tender name in one;
On thee we cast our every care,
And comfort seek from thee alone.

[VERSE 4]
To thee, our Father, would we look,
Our Rock, our Portion, and our Friend,
And on thy covenant love and truth,
With humble, steadfast hope depend.
