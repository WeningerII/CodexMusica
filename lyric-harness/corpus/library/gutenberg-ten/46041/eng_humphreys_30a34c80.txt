--- TITLE: Blesséd are the sons of God [item: PG46041-420]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c420
--- AUTHOR: Humphreys.

[VERSE 1]
Blesséd are the sons of God;
They are bought with Jesus’ blood;
They are ransomed from the grave
Life eternal they shall have;
With them numbered may we be,
Here, and in eternity.

[VERSE 2]
They are justified by grace,
They enjoy the Saviour’s peace;
All their sins are washed away;
They shall stand in God’s great day:
With them numbered may we be,
Here, and in eternity.

[VERSE 3]
They are lights upon the earth—
Children of a heavenly birth—
One with God, with Jesus one;
Glory is in them begun;
With them numbered may we be,
Here, and in eternity.
