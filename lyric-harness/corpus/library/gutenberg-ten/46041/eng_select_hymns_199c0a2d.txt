--- TITLE: When shall we meet again? [item: PG46041-504]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c504
--- AUTHOR: Select Hymns.

[VERSE 1]
When shall we meet again?
Meet ne’er to sever?
When will Peace wreathe her chain
Round us for ever?
Our hearts will ne’er repose
Safe from each blast that blows
In this dark vail of woes,
Never—no, never!

[VERSE 2]
When shall love freely flow,
Pure as life’s river?
When shall sweet friendship glow,
Changeless for ever?
Where joys celestial thrill,
Where bliss each heart shall fill,
And fears of parting chill,
Never—no, never!

[VERSE 3]
Up to that world of light
Take us, dear Saviour;
May we all there unite,
Happy for ever:
Where kindred spirits dwell,
There may our music swell,
And time our joys dispel,
Never—no, never!

[VERSE 4]
Soon shall we meet again,
Meet ne’er to sever:
Soon shall Peace wreathe her chain
Round us for ever:
Our hearts will then repose
Secure from worldly woes;
Our songs of praise shall close,
Never—no, never!
