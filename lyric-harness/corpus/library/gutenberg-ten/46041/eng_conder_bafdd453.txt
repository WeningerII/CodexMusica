--- TITLE: Beyond, beyond that boundless sea [item: PG46041-84]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c84
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
Beyond, beyond that boundless sea,
Above that dome of sky,
Further than thought itself can flee,
Thy dwelling is on high:
Yet dear the awful thought to me,
That thou, my God, art nigh!

[VERSE 2]
Art nigh, and yet my laboring mind
Feels after thee in vain,
Thee in these works of power to find,
Or to thy seat attain.
Thy messenger the stormy wind;
Thy path, the trackless main:

[VERSE 3]
These speak of thee with loud acclaim;
They thunder forth thy praise,
The glorious honor of thy name,
The wonders of thy ways:
But thou art not in tempest flame
Nor in the noontide blaze.

[VERSE 4]
We hear thy voice when thunders roll
Through the wide fields of air;
The waves obey thy dread control;
But still, thou art not there:
Where shall I find him, O my soul!
Who yet is everywhere?

[VERSE 5]
O! not in circling depth or hight,
But in the conscious breast,
Present to faith, though vailed from sight;
There doth his Spirit rest:
O, come, thou Presence infinite!
And make thy creature blest.

--- TITLE: The Lord is King! lift up thy voice [item: PG46041-214]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c214
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
The Lord is King! lift up thy voice,
O earth, and all ye heavens, rejoice!
From world to world the joy shall ring—
“The Lord omnipotent is King!”

[VERSE 2]
The Lord is King! who then shall dare
Resist his will, distrust his care?
Holy and true are all his ways:
Let every creature speak his praise.

--- TITLE: Bread of heaven, on thee we feed [item: PG46041-534]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c534
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
Bread of heaven, on thee we feed,
For thy flesh is meat indeed;
Ever let our souls be fed
With this true and living bread.

[VERSE 2]
Vine of heaven, thy blood supplies
This blest cup of sacrifice;
Lord, thy wounds our healing give;
To thy cross we look and live.

[VERSE 3]
Day by day with strength supplied,
Through the life of him who died,
Lord of life, O let us be
Rooted, grafted, built on thee.

--- TITLE: Heavenly Father! to whose eye [item: PG46041-572]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c572
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
Heavenly Father! to whose eye
Future things unfolded lie;
Through the desert when I stray
Let thy counsels guide my way.

[VERSE 2]
Lord! uphold me day by day;
Shed a light upon my way;
Guide me through perplexing snares,
Care for me in all my cares.

[VERSE 3]
Should thy wisdom, Lord, decree
Trials long and sharp for me,
Pain, or sorrow, care or shame—
Father! glorify thy name.

[VERSE 4]
Let me neither faint nor fear,
Feeling still that thou art near;
In the course my Saviour trod,
Tending home to thee, my God.

--- TITLE: How honored, how dear, is that sacred abode [item: PG46041-585]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c585
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
How honored, how dear, is that sacred abode,
Where Christians draw near to their Father and God:
’Mid worldly commotion my wearied soul faints
For the house of devotion, the home of thy saints.

[VERSE 2]
Thou hearer of prayer, O still grant me a place
Where Christians repair to the courts of thy grace,
More blest beyond measure one day so employed,
Than years of vain pleasure by worldlings enjoyed.

[VERSE 3]
Me more would it please keeping post at thy gate,
Than lying at ease in the chambers of state;
The meanest condition outshines with thy smiles,
The pomp of ambition, the world with its wiles.

[VERSE 4]
The Lord is a Sun, and the Lord is a Shield:
What grace has begun, will with glory be sealed;
He hears the distresséd, he succors the just,
And they shall be blesséd who make him their trust.

--- TITLE: Praise on thee, in Zion’s gates [item: PG46041-655]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c655
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
Praise on thee, in Zion’s gates,
Daily, O Jehovah, waits;
Unto thee, who hearest prayer,
Shall the tribes of men repair.

[VERSE 2]
Though with conscious guilt oppressed,
On thy mercy still we rest;
Thy forgiving love display,
Take, O Lord, our sins away.

[VERSE 3]
O, how blesséd their reward,
Chosen servants of the Lord,
Who within thy courts abide,
With thy goodness satisfied.

--- TITLE: When I can trust my all with God [item: PG46041-777]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c777
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
When I can trust my all with God,
In trial’s fearful hour—
Bow all resigned beneath his rod,
And bless his sparing power;
A joy springs up amid distress,
A fountain in the wilderness.

[VERSE 2]
O! to be brought to Jesus’ feet,
Though trials fix me there,
Is still a privilege most sweet;
For he will hear my prayer;
Though sighs and tears its language be,
The Lord is nigh to answer me.

[VERSE 3]
Then, blesséd be the hand that gave,
Still blesséd when it takes;
Blesséd be he who smites to save,
Who heals the heart he breaks;
Perfect and true are all his ways,
Whom heaven adores and death obeys.

--- TITLE: I am thy workmanship, O Lord [item: PG46041-816]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c816
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
I am thy workmanship, O Lord!
And unto thee belong;
Thou art my shield, my Great Reward,
My Glory, and my song.

[VERSE 2]
Surround me with thy guardian might,
Uphold me with thy grace;
Unharmed, conduct me through the fight;
Unwearied, through the race.

[VERSE 3]
Make me a weapon of thy power,
An angel of thy will;
To thee devoted, let each hour
Its happy task fulfill.

[VERSE 4]
Yet dare not I, a child of dust
Thus plead my filial claim,
But as in him is all my trust,
Who bears a Saviour’s name.

--- TITLE: When, in the hour of lonely woe [item: PG46041-852]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c852
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
When, in the hour of lonely woe,
I give my sorrows leave to flow,
And anxious fear and dark distrust
Weigh down my spirit to the dust;

[VERSE 2]
When not e’en friendship’s gentle aid
Can heal the wounds the world has made,
O this shall check each rising sigh—
My Saviour is for ever nigh.

[VERSE 3]
His counsels and upholding care
My safety and my comfort are:
And he shall guide me all my days,
Till glory crown the work of grace.

--- TITLE: God is in the loneliest spot [item: PG46041-991]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c991
--- AUTHOR: Conder.

[VERSE 1]
God is in the loneliest spot
Present, though thou know it not;
Morning vows and evening prayer
Make a Bethel everywhere.

[VERSE 2]
Go where duty guides thy feet;
There good angels thou shalt meet;
Hosts of God thou canst not see,
Watch thy steps and wait on thee.
