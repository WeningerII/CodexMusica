--- TITLE: Sinners, come; no longer wander [item: PG46041-335]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c335
--- AUTHOR: B. Skene.

[VERSE 1]
Sinners, come; no longer wander;
Turn you from your evil way;
Precious time no longer squander:
Come, come away.

[VERSE 2]
Christ for you his life has offered,
What can you excusing say,
If you slight the pardon proffered?
Come, come away.

[VERSE 3]
Hold not back in hesitation,
There is danger in delay,
Haste, secure your soul’s salvation,
Come, come away.

[VERSE 4]
You may feel regret and sorrow,
If you fail to come to-day,
God may grant you no to-morrow,
Come, come away.

--- TITLE: How sweet the praise, how high the theme [item: PG46041-638]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c638
--- AUTHOR: B. Skene.

[VERSE 1]
How sweet the praise, how high the theme,
To sing of him who rules supreme,
Who dwells at God’s right hand on high,
Yet looks on us with tender eye.

[VERSE 2]
Th’ angelic host, in countless throngs,
Recount his glories in their songs,
And golden harps salute his ear;
Yet our weak praise he deigns to hear.

[VERSE 3]
The planets roll their orbits round;
Unnumbered worlds, in space profound,
Are ruled by him, by him controlled;
Yet he’s the Shepherd of our fold.

[VERSE 4]
Exalted high upon his throne,
The universe is all his own:
Untold the honors he doth wear;
Yet we are objects of his care.

--- TITLE: Praise the Lord! ye saints adore him [item: PG46041-673]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c673
--- AUTHOR: B. Skene.

[VERSE 1]
Praise the Lord! ye saints adore him,
All unite with one accord;
Bring your offerings, come before him—
O praise the Lord.

[VERSE 2]
Praise the Lord! who every blessing
On our heads hath richly poured;
Sing aloud, his love confessing—
O praise the Lord.

[VERSE 3]
Praise the Lord! who would not praise him?
He hath us to grace restored:
To the highest honors raise him—
O praise the Lord.

[VERSE 4]
Praise the Lord! your songs excelling
Worldly music’s richest chord;
Sing—your Saviour’s glory telling;
O praise the Lord.
