--- TITLE: Asleep in Jesus! Blesséd sleep [item: PG46041-1038]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1038
--- AUTHOR: Mrs. McKay.

[VERSE 1]
Asleep in Jesus! Blesséd sleep
From which none ever wakes to weep;
A calm and undisturbed repose,
Unbroken by the last of foes.

[VERSE 2]
Asleep in Jesus! O how sweet
To be for such a slumber meet!
With holy confidence to sing,
That death has lost its venomed sting.

[VERSE 3]
Asleep in Jesus! peaceful rest,
Whose waking is supremely blest:
No fear, no woe, shall dim the hour
That manifests the Saviour’s power.

[VERSE 4]
Asleep in Jesus! O for me
May such a blissful refuge be:
Securely shall my ashes lie,
And wait the summons from on high.

[VERSE 5]
Asleep in Jesus! time nor space
Affects this precious hiding-place:
On Indian plains, or Lapland snows,
Believers find the same repose.

[VERSE 6]
Asleep in Jesus! far from thee
Thy kindred and their graves may be:
But thine is still a blesséd sleep,
which none ever wake to weep.
