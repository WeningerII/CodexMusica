--- TITLE: Behold the Saviour of mankind [item: PG46041-175]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c175
--- AUTHOR: S. Wesley, sen.

[VERSE 1]
Behold the Saviour of mankind
Nailed to the shameful tree!
How vast the love that him inclined
To bleed and die for me!

[VERSE 2]
Hark! how he groans, while nature shakes,
And earth’s strong pillars bend!
The temple’s vail asunder breaks,
The solid marbles rend.

[VERSE 3]
’Tis finished! now the ransom’s paid,
“Receive my soul!” he cries:
See—how he bows his sacred head!
He bows his head and dies!

[VERSE 4]
But soon from death he’ll rise again,
And in full glory shine;
O Lamb of God! was ever pain—
Was ever love like thine?
