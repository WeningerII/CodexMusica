--- TITLE: There is a region lovelier far [item: PG46041-1125]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1125
--- AUTHOR: Tuck.

[VERSE 1]
There is a region lovelier far
Than sages tell or poets sing—
Brighter than summer’s beauties are,
And softer than the tints of spring.

[CHORUS]
I’m going home, I’m going home,
I’m going home to die no more,
To die no more, to die no more,
I’m going home to die no more.

[VERSE 2]
It is all holy and serene,
The land of glory and repose;
No cloud obscures the radiant scene;
There not a tear of sorrow flows.
