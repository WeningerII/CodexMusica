--- TITLE: Dear as thou wast, and justly dear [item: PG46041-1056]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1056
--- AUTHOR: Dale.

[VERSE 1]
Dear as thou wast, and justly dear
We would not weep for thee:
One thought shall check the starting tear,
It is that thou art free.

[VERSE 2]
And thus shall faith’s consoling power
The tears of love restrain;
O, who that saw thy parting hour,
Could wish thee here again!

[VERSE 3]
Gently the passing spirit fled,
Sustained by grace divine;
O, may such grace on us be shed,
And make our end like thine!

--- TITLE: When the spark of life is waning [item: PG46041-1091]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1091
--- AUTHOR: Dale.

[VERSE 1]
When the spark of life is waning,
Weep not for me;
When the languid eye is streaming,
Weep not for me;
When the feeble pulse is ceasing,
Start not at its swift decreasing,
’Tis the fettered soul’s releasing,
Weep not for me.

[VERSE 2]
When the pangs of death assail me,
Weep not for me;
Christ is mine, he can not fail me,
Weep not for me;
Yes, though sin and doubt endeavor,
From his love my soul to sever,
Jesus is my strength for ever;
Weep not for me.
