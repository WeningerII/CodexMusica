--- TITLE: Down the dark future, through long generations [item: PG46041-973]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c973
--- AUTHOR: Longfellow.

[VERSE 1]
Down the dark future, through long generations,
The sounds of war grow fainter, and then cease;
And like a bell with solemn, sweet vibrations,
I hear once more the voice of Christ say, “Peace!”

[VERSE 2]
Peace! and no longer, from its brazen portals,
The blast of war’s great organ shakes the skies;
But beautiful as songs of the immortals,
The holy melodies of love arise.

--- TITLE: Peace, peace on earth! the heart of man for ever [item: PG46041-974]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c974
--- AUTHOR: Longfellow.

[VERSE 1]
Peace, peace on earth! the heart of man for ever,
Through all these weary strifes, foretells the day;
Blesséd be God, the hope forsakes him never,
That war shall end, and swords be sheathed for aye.

[VERSE 2]
Peace, peace on earth! for men shall love each other;
Hosts shall go forth to bless, and not destroy;
For man shall see in every man a brother,
And peace on earth fulfill the angels’ joy.
