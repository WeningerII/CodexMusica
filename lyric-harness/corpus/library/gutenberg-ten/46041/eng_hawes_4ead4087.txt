--- TITLE: O thou, from whom all goodness flows [item: PG46041-862]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c862
--- AUTHOR: Hawes.

[VERSE 1]
O thou, from whom all goodness flows,
I lift my soul to thee;
In all my sorrows, conflicts, woes,
O Lord, remember me.

[VERSE 2]
If for thy sake, upon my name
Reproach and shame shall be,
I’ll hail reproach and welcome shame;
O Lord remember me!

[VERSE 3]
When worn with pain, disease, and grief,
This feeble body see;
Grant patience, rest and kind relief;
O Lord remember me!

[VERSE 4]
When, in the solemn hour of death,
I wait thy just decree,
Be this the prayer of my last breath—
O Lord, remember me!

[VERSE 5]
And when before thy throne I stand,
And lift my soul to thee,
Then with the saints at thy right hand,
O Lord, remember me!
