--- TITLE: O God! my heart is fully bent [item: PG46041-38]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c38
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady.

[VERSE 1]
O God! my heart is fully bent
To magnify thy name;
My tongue, with cheerful songs of praise,
Shall celebrate thy fame.

[VERSE 2]
Be thou, O God! exalted high
Above the starry frame;
And let the world, with one consent
Confess thy glorious name.

--- TITLE: No change of time shall ever shock [item: PG46041-65]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c65
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady.

[VERSE 1]
No change of time shall ever shock
My firm affection, Lord, to thee;
For thou hast always been my rock,
A fortress and defense to me.

[VERSE 2]
Thou my deliverer art, my God;
My trust is in thy mighty power;
Thou art my shield from foes abroad—
At home my safeguard and my tower.

[VERSE 3]
To thee I will address my prayer,
To whom all praise I justly owe;
So shall I by thy watchful care,
Be guarded from my treacherous foe.

--- TITLE: To bless thy chosen race [item: PG46041-737]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c737
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady.

[VERSE 1]
To bless thy chosen race,
In mercy, Lord, incline;
And cause the brightness of thy face
On all thy saints to shine;—

[VERSE 2]
That so thy wondrous way
May through the world be known:
While distant lands their homage pay,
And thy salvation own.

[VERSE 3]
Let all the nations join
To celebrate thy fame;
And all the world, Lord, combine,
To praise thy glorious name.

--- TITLE: Through all the changing scenes of life [item: PG46041-911]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c911
--- AUTHOR: Tate & Brady.

[VERSE 1]
Through all the changing scenes of life,
In trouble and in joy,
The praises of my God shall still
My heart and tongue employ.

[VERSE 2]
Of his deliverance I will boast,
Till all that are distressed,
From my example, comfort take,
And charm their griefs to rest.

[VERSE 3]
O, magnify the Lord with me,
With me exalt his name;
When in distress to him I called,
He to my rescue came.

[VERSE 4]
The hosts of God encamp around
The dwellings of the just;
Deliverance he affords to all,
Who on his succor trust.
