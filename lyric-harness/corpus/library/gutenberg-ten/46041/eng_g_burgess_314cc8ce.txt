--- TITLE: The harvest dawn is near [item: PG46041-1233]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1233
--- AUTHOR: G. Burgess.

[VERSE 1]
The harvest dawn is near,
The year delays not long;
And he who sows with many a tear,
Shall reap with many a song.

[VERSE 2]
Sad to his toil he goes,
His seed with weeping leaves;
But he shall come, at twilight’s close,
And bring his golden sheaves.

--- TITLE: The floods, O Lord, lift up their voice [item: PG46041-1290]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1290
--- AUTHOR: G. Burgess.

[VERSE 1]
The floods, O Lord, lift up their voice,
The mighty floods lift up their roar;
The floods in tumult loud rejoice,
And climb in foam the sounding shore.

[VERSE 2]
But mightier than the mighty sea,
The Lord of glory reigns on high;
Far o’er its waves we look to thee,
And see their fury break and die.

[VERSE 3]
Thy word is true, thy promise sure,
That ancient promise sealed in love;
Here be thy temple ever pure,
As thy pure mansions shine above.
