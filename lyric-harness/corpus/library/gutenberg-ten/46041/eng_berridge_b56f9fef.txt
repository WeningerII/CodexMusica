--- TITLE: Jesus, cast a look on me [item: PG46041-780]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c780
--- AUTHOR: Berridge.

[VERSE 1]
Jesus, cast a look on me!
Give me true simplicity:
Make me poor and keep me low,
Seeking only thee to know.

[VERSE 2]
All that feeds my busy pride,
Cast it evermore aside:
Bid my will to thine submit:
Lay me humbly at thy feet.

[VERSE 3]
Make me like a little child,
Simple, teachable, and mild;
Seeing only in thy light;
Walking only in thy might!

[VERSE 4]
Leaning on thy loving breast,
Where a weary soul may rest;
Feeling well the peace of God
Flowing from thy precious blood!

--- TITLE: Since Jesus freely did appear [item: PG46041-1297]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1297
--- AUTHOR: Berridge.

[VERSE 1]
Since Jesus freely did appear
To grace a marriage feast;
Lord, we ask thy presence here
To make a wedding guest.

[VERSE 2]
Upon the bridal pair look down,
Who now have plighted hands;
Their union with thy favor crown,
And bless the nuptial bands.

[VERSE 3]
With gifts of grace their hearts endow,
Of all rich dowries best;
Their substance bless, and peace bestow
To sweeten all the rest.

[VERSE 4]
In purest love their souls unite,
That they, with Christian care,
May make domestic burdens light,
By taking mutual share.
