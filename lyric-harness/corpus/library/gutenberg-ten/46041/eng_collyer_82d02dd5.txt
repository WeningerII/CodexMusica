--- TITLE: Morning breaks upon the tomb [item: PG46041-191]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c191
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Morning breaks upon the tomb,
Jesus scatters all its gloom;
Day of triumph through the skies—
See the glorious Saviour rise!

[VERSE 2]
Ye who are of death afraid,
Triumph in the scattered shade;
Drive your anxious cares away;
See the place where Jesus lay!

[VERSE 3]
Christian! dry your flowing tears,
Chase your unbelieving fears;
Look on his deserted grave;
Doubt no more his power to save.

--- TITLE: The angels that watched round the tomb [item: PG46041-193]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c193
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
The angels that watched round the tomb
Where low the Redeemer was laid,
When deep in mortality’s gloom
He hid for a season his head;

[VERSE 2]
That vailed their fair face while he slept,
And ceased their sweet harps to employ,
Have witnessed his rising, and swept
The chords with the triumphs of joy.

[VERSE 3]
You saints, who once languished below,
But long since have entered your rest,
I pant to be glorified too,
To lean on Immanuel’s breast.

[VERSE 4]
The grave in which Jesus was laid
Has buried my guilt and my fears;
And while I contemplate its shade,
The light of his presence appears.

[VERSE 5]
O sweet is the season of rest,
When life’s weary journey is done!
The blush that spreads over its west,
The last lingering ray of its sun!

[VERSE 6]
Though dreary the empire of night,
I soon shall emerge from its gloom,
And see immortality’s light
Arise on the shades of the tomb.

[VERSE 7]
Then welcome the last rending sighs,
When these aching heartstrings shall break,
When death shall extinguish these eyes,
And moisten with dew the pale cheek.

[VERSE 8]
No terror the prospect begets,
I am not mortality’s slave,
The sunbeam of life as it sets,
Paints a rainbow of peace on the grave.

--- TITLE: Haste, traveler, haste! the night comes on [item: PG46041-276]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c276
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Haste, traveler, haste! the night comes on,
And many a shining hour is gone;
The storm is gathering in the west,
And thou art far from home and rest:
Haste, traveler, haste!

[VERSE 2]
Awake, awake! pursue thy way
With steady course, while yet ’tis day;
While thou art sleeping on the ground,
Danger and darkness gather round;
Haste, traveler, haste!

[VERSE 3]
The rising tempest sweeps the sky;
The rains descend, the winds are high;
The waters swell, and death and fear
Beset thy path; no refuge near:
Haste, traveler, haste!

[VERSE 4]
Haste, while a shelter you may gain—
A covert from the wind and rain;
A hiding-place, a rest, a home—
A refuge from the wrath to come:
Haste, traveler, haste!

[VERSE 5]
Then linger not in all the plain;
Flee for thy life—the mountain gain;
Look not behind, make no delay;
O, speed thee, speed thee on thy way!
Haste, traveler, haste!

--- TITLE: Return, O wanderer, now return [item: PG46041-288]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c288
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Return, O wanderer, now return,
And seek thy Father’s face;
Those new desires which in thee burn
Were kindled by his grace.

[VERSE 2]
Return, O wanderer, now return!
He hears thy humble sigh!
He sees thy softened spirit mourn,
When no one else is nigh.

[VERSE 3]
Return, O wanderer, now return!
Thy Saviour bids thee live;
Go to his feet, and grateful learn
How freely he’ll forgive.

[VERSE 4]
Return, O wanderer, now return!
And wipe the falling tear;
Thy Father calls—no longer mourn,
’Tis love invites thee near.

--- TITLE: Jesus, thou Shepherd of the sheep [item: PG46041-482]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c482
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Jesus, thou Shepherd of the sheep,
Thy little flock in safety keep;
These lambs within thine arms now take,
Nor let them e’er thy fold forsake.

[VERSE 2]
Secure them from the scorching beam,
And lead them to the living stream;
In verdant pastures let them lie,
And watch them with a shepherd’s eye!

[VERSE 3]
O, teach them to discern thy voice,
And in its sacred sound rejoice!
From strangers may they ever flee,
And know no other guide but thee.

[VERSE 4]
Lord, bring thy sheep that wander yet,
And let their number be complete;
Then let the flock from earth remove,
And reach the heavenly fold above.

--- TITLE: Soft be the gently breathing notes [item: PG46041-514]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c514
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Soft be the gently breathing notes
That sing the Saviour’s dying love;
Soft as the evening zephyr floats,
Soft as the tuneful lyres above:
Soft as the morning dews descend,
While warbling birds exulting soar;
So soft to our almighty Friend
Be every sigh our bosoms pour.

[VERSE 2]
Pure as the sun’s enlivening ray,
That scatters life and joy abroad;
Pure as the lucid orb of day,
That wide proclaims its Maker, God;
Pure as the breath of vernal skies,
So pure let our contrition be;
And purely let our sorrows rise
To him who bled upon the tree.

--- TITLE: Father of the human race [item: PG46041-1299]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1299
--- AUTHOR: Collyer.

[VERSE 1]
Father of the human race,
Sanction with thy heavenly grace
What on earth hath now been done,
That these twain be truly one.

[VERSE 2]
One in sickness and in health,
One in poverty and wealth,
And as year rolls after year,
Each to other still more dear.

[VERSE 3]
One in purpose, one in heart,
Till the mortal stroke shall part;
One in cheerful piety,
One for ever, Lord, with thee.
