--- TITLE: Christ leads me through no darker rooms [item: PG46041-1003]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1003
--- AUTHOR: R. Baxter.

[VERSE 1]
Christ leads me through no darker rooms
Than he went through before:
He that into God’s kingdom comes
Must enter by this door.

[VERSE 2]
Come, Lord, when grace hath made me meet
Thy blesséd face to see;
For if thy work on earth be sweet,
What must thy glory be?

[VERSE 3]
Then I shall end my sad complaints,
And weary, sinful days,
And join with those triumphant saints
That sing Jehovah’s praise.
