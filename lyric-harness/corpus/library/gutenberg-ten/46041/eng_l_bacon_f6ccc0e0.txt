--- TITLE: Hail, tranquil hour of closing day [item: PG46041-1193]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1193
--- AUTHOR: L. Bacon.

[VERSE 1]
Hail, tranquil hour of closing day!
Begone, disturbing care;
And look, my soul, from earth away,
To him who heareth prayer.

[VERSE 2]
How sweet the tear of penitence,
Before his throne of grace,
While, to the contrite spirit’s sense,
He shows his smiling face.

[VERSE 3]
How sweet, through long remembered years,
His mercies to recall;
And, pressed with wants, and griefs, and fears,
To trust his love for all.

[VERSE 4]
How sweet to look, in thoughtful hope,
Beyond this fading sky,
And hear him call his children up
To his fair home on high.

[VERSE 5]
Calmly the day forsakes our heaven,
To dawn beyond the west;
So let my soul, in life’s last even,
Retire to glorious rest.
