--- TITLE: Lord, I am thine, entirely thine [item: PG46041-397]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c397
--- AUTHOR: Davies.

[VERSE 1]
Lord, I am thine, entirely thine,
Purchased alone by blood divine;
With full consent I yield to thee,
And own thy sovereign right to me.

[VERSE 2]
Grant me, in mercy, now a place
Among the children of thy grace;
A wretched sinner, lost to God,
But ransomed by Immanuel’s blood.

[VERSE 3]
Thee, my new Master, now I call,
And consecrate to thee my all:
Lord, let me live and die to thee;
Be thine through all eternity.
