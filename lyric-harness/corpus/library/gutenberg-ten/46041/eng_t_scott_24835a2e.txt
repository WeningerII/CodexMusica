--- TITLE: Haste, O sinner, to be wise [item: PG46041-306]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c306
--- AUTHOR: T. Scott.

[VERSE 1]
Haste, O sinner, to be wise,
Stay not for the morrow’s sun;
Wisdom warns thee from the skies,
All the paths of death to shun.

[VERSE 2]
Haste, and mercy now implore;
Stay not for the morrow’s sun;
Thy probation may be o’er
Ere this evening’s work is done.

[VERSE 3]
Haste, O sinner, now return;
Stay not for the morrow’s sun;
Lest thy lamp should cease to burn
Ere salvation’s work is done.

[VERSE 4]
Haste, while yet thou canst be blest;
Stay not for the morrow’s sun,
Death may thy poor soul arrest
Ere the morrow is begun.

--- TITLE: Thus Abraham, full of sacred awe [item: PG46041-1266]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1266
--- AUTHOR: T. Scott.

[VERSE 1]
Thus Abraham, full of sacred awe,
Before Jehovah stood,
And with a humble, fervent prayer,
For guilty Sodom sued.

[VERSE 2]
And could a single holy soul
So rich a boon obtain?
Great God! and shall a nation pray,
And plead with thee in vain?

[VERSE 3]
Still we are thine; we bear thy name;
Here yet is thine abode;
Long has thy presence blessed our land;
Forsake us not, O God!
