--- TITLE: Shepherds! hail the wondrous stranger [item: PG46041-133]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c133
--- AUTHOR: Psalmist.

[VERSE 1]
Shepherds! hail the wondrous stranger,
Now to Bethlehem speed your way;
Lo! in yonder humble manger,
Christ, the Lord, is born to-day.

[VERSE 2]
Bright the star of your salvation,
Pointing to his rude abode;
Rapturous news for every nation:
Now, behold the Son of God.

[VERSE 3]
Love eternal moved the Saviour,
Thus to lay his radiance by;
Blessings on the Lamb for ever;
Glory be to God on high.
