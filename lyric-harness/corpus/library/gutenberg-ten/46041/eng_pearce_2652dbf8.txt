--- TITLE: In the floods of tribulation [item: PG46041-1026]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1026
--- AUTHOR: Pearce.

[VERSE 1]
In the floods of tribulation,
While the billows o’er me roll,
Jesus whispers consolation,
And supports my sinking soul;
Sweet affliction!
Bringing Jesus to my soul.

[VERSE 2]
In the darkest dispensations
Doth my faithful Lord appear,
With his richest consolations,
To reanimate and cheer.
Sweet affliction!
Thus to bring my Saviour near.

[VERSE 3]
All I meet shall still befriend me
In my path to heavenly joy,
Where, though trials now attend me,
Trials never more annoy.
Sweet affliction!
Every promise gives me joy.

[VERSE 4]
Wearing there a weight of glory,
Still the path I’ll ne’er forget;
But, exulting, cry, It led me
To my blesséd Saviour’s seat.
Sweet affliction!
Which has brought me to his feet.
