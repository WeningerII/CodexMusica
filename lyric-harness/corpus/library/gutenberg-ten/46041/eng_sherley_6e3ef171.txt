--- TITLE: The glories of our birth and state [item: PG46041-1044]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1044
--- AUTHOR: Sherley.

[VERSE 1]
The glories of our birth and state
Are shadows, not substantial things;
There is no armor against fate;
Death lays his icy hands on kings.

[VERSE 2]
Princes and magistrates must fall,
And in the dust be equal made;
The high and mighty with the small,
Scepter and crown with scythe and spade.

[VERSE 3]
The laurel withers on our brow;
Then boast no more your mighty deeds;
Upon death’s purple altar now
See where the victor victim bleeds!
