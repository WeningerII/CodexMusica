--- TITLE: If human kindness meets return [item: PG46041-520]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c520
--- AUTHOR: R. W. Noel.

[VERSE 1]
If human kindness meets return,
And owns the grateful tie—
If tender thoughts within us burn
To feel a friend is nigh;

[VERSE 2]
O, shall not warmer accents tell
The gratitude we owe
To him who died our fears to quell,
And save from endless woe?

[VERSE 3]
While yet his anguished soul surveyed
Those pangs he would not flee,
What love his latest words displayed—
“Meet and remember me.”

[VERSE 4]
Remember thee! thy death, thy shame,
The griefs which thou didst bear!
O memory, leave no other name
But His recorded there.
