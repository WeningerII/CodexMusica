--- TITLE: Christian, the vision before thee is glorious [item: PG46041-1097]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1097
--- AUTHOR: A. S. Hayden.

[VERSE 1]
Christian, the vision before thee is glorious,
The earth shall allure thy tried spirit no more:
Thou wast in the day of thy trial victorious,
Secure now at last, thy temptations are o’er.

[VERSE 2]
Hard was the strife, but the strong one in battle,
Has been thy defender, and vanquished thy foes;
And heaven stood by thee to help thee in trouble,
And joyed when the sound of thy triumph arose.

[VERSE 3]
High was the anthem those raptures revealing,
Ten thousand celestials the chorus prolong;
But louder the strains of the ransomed are pealing,
And glory is swelling the conqueror’s song.

--- TITLE: The morning light returns [item: PG46041-1185]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1185
--- AUTHOR: A. S. Hayden.

[VERSE 1]
The morning light returns,
The sun begins to shine;
Now let our souls in haste arise,
To run the race divine.

[VERSE 2]
We praise the Father’s love,
Who kept us through the night;
O may his kindness be our song,
His pleasure our delight.

[VERSE 3]
While passing through this day,
Lord, we implore thy care,
To guide us on the heavenly way,
And guard from every snare.

[VERSE 4]
And when our life shall close,
O may it be in peace;
May we lie down in sweet repose,
And wake in endless bliss.

--- TITLE: While in the slippery paths of youth [item: PG46041-1214]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1214
--- AUTHOR: A. S. Hayden.

[VERSE 1]
While in the slippery paths of youth,
I run secure and free!
O let thy blesséd word of truth,
My guide and counsel be.

[VERSE 2]
If near the tempter’s wily snare
In heedlessness I tread;
O be thy kind protecting care,
To save me overspread.

[VERSE 3]
Thus o’er my life let mercy move,
And guide my feet the way
That leads me to thy throne above—
To everlasting day.
