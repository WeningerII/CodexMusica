--- TITLE: Awake, my tongue; thy tribute bring [item: PG46041-105]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c105
--- AUTHOR: Needham.

[VERSE 1]
Awake, my tongue; thy tribute bring
To him who gave thee power to sing;
Praise him who is all praise above,
The source of wisdom and of love.

[VERSE 2]
How vast his knowledge! how profound!
A depth where all our thoughts are drowned;
The stars he numbers, and their names
He gives to all those heavenly flames.

[VERSE 3]
Through each bright world above, behold
Ten thousand thousand charms unfold;
Earth, air, and mighty seas combine
To speak his wisdom all divine.

[VERSE 4]
But in redemption, O what grace!
Its wonders, O, what thought can trace!
Here, wisdom shines for ever bright;
Praise him, my soul, with sweet delight.

--- TITLE: O how divine, how sweet the joy [item: PG46041-358]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c358
--- AUTHOR: Needham.

[VERSE 1]
O how divine, how sweet the joy,
When but one sinner turns,
And, with a humble, broken heart,
His sins and errors mourns!

[VERSE 2]
Pleased with the news, the saints below,
In songs their tongues employ;
Beyond the skies the tidings go,
And heaven is filled with joy.

[VERSE 3]
Well pleased the Father sees, and hears
The conscious sinner’s moan;
Jesus receives him in his arms,
And claims him for his own.

[VERSE 4]
Nor angels can their joy contain,
But kindle with new fire;
“The sinner lost is found,” they sing,
And strike the sounding lyre.

--- TITLE: Rise, O my soul! pursue the path [item: PG46041-860]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c860
--- AUTHOR: Needham.

[VERSE 1]
Rise, O my soul! pursue the path
By ancient heroes trod;
Ambitious view those holy men
Who lived and walked with God.

[VERSE 2]
Though dead, they speak in reason’s ear;
And in example live;
Their faith, and hope, and mighty deeds,
Still fresh instruction give.

[VERSE 3]
’Twas through the Lamb’s most precious blood
They conquered every foe:
And to his power and matchless grace,
Their crowns and honor owe.

[VERSE 4]
Lord, may we ever keep in view
The patterns thou hast given,
And ne’er forsake the blessed road
Which led them safe to heaven.
