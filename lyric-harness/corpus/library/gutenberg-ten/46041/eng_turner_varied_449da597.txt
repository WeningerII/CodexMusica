--- TITLE: Beyond the starry skies [item: PG46041-259]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c259
--- AUTHOR: Turner, varied.

[VERSE 1]
Beyond the starry skies,
Far as th’ eternal hills,
Yon heaven of heavens, with living light,
Our great Redeemer fills.

[VERSE 2]
Around him angels fair,
In countless armies shine;
And ever, in exalted lays,
They offer songs divine.

[VERSE 3]
“Hail, Prince of life!” they cry,
“Whose unexampled love
Moved thee to quit those glorious realms
And royalties above.”

[VERSE 4]
And when he stooped to earth,
And suffered rude disdain,
They cast their honors at his feet,
And waited in his train.

[VERSE 5]
They saw him on the cross,
While darkness vailed the skies;
And when he burst the gates of death,
They saw the Conqueror rise.

[VERSE 6]
They thronged his chariot wheels,
And bore him to his throne;
Then swept their golden harps and sung—
“The glorious work is done.”
