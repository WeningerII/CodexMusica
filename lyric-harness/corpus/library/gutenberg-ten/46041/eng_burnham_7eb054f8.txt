--- TITLE: Jesus, thou art the sinner’s friend [item: PG46041-241]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c241
--- AUTHOR: Burnham.

[VERSE 1]
Jesus, thou art the sinner’s friend;
As such I look to thee;
Now, in the fullness of thy love,
O Lord, remember me!

[VERSE 2]
Remember thy pure word of grace,
Remember Calvary;
Remember all thy promises,
And then remember me.

[VERSE 3]
Thou mighty Advocate with God!
I yield myself to thee;
While thou art sitting on thy throne,
O Lord, remember me!

[VERSE 4]
I own I’m guilty—own I’m vile;
Yet thy salvation’s free;
Then, in thy all-abounding grace,
O Lord, remember me!

[VERSE 5]
Howe’er forsaken or distressed,
Howe’er oppressed I be,
Howe’er afflicted here on earth,
Do thou remember me!

[VERSE 6]
And when I close my eyes in death,
And creature helps all flee,
Then, O my great Redeemer, Lord,
I pray, remember me!
