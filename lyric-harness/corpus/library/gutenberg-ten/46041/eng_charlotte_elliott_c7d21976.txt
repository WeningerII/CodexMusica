--- TITLE: I can not always trace the way [item: PG46041-86]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c86
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
I can not always trace the way
Where thou, almighty One, dost move;
But I can always, always say,
That God is love.

[VERSE 2]
When fear her chilling mantle flings
O’er earth, my soul to heaven above,
As to her native home, upsprings;
For God is love.

[VERSE 3]
When mystery clouds my darkened path,
I’ll check my dread, my doubts reprove;
In this my soul sweet comfort hath,
That God is love.

[VERSE 4]
O may this truth my heart employ,
And every gloomy thought remove;
It fills my soul with boundless joy,
That God is love!

--- TITLE: Just as I am—without one plea [item: PG46041-343]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c343
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
Just as I am—without one plea,
But that thy blood was shed for me,
And that thou bidd’st me come to thee,
O Lamb of God, I come.

[VERSE 2]
Just as I am, and waiting not
To rid my soul of one dark blot—
To thee, whose blood can cleanse each spot,
O Lamb of God, I come.

[VERSE 3]
Just as I am, though tossed about
With many a conflict, many a doubt,
With fears within, and foes without—
O Lamb of God, I come.

[VERSE 4]
Just as I am, poor, wretched, blind;
Sight, riches, healing of the mind,
Yea, all I need, in thee to find,
O Lamb of God, I come.

[VERSE 5]
Just as I am, thou wilt receive,
Wilt welcome, pardon, cleanse, relieve,
Because thy promise I believe—
O Lamb of God, I come.

[VERSE 6]
Just as I am—thy love unknown,
Has broken every barrier down;
Now to be thine, yea, thine alone,
O Lamb of God, I come.

--- TITLE: My God! is any hour so sweet [item: PG46041-581]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c581
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
My God! is any hour so sweet,
From blush of morn to evening star,
As that which calls me to thy feet—
The hour of prayer?

[VERSE 2]
Blest is the tranquil hour of morn,
And blest that hour of solemn eve,
When, on the wings of prayer up-borne,
The world I leave.

[VERSE 3]
Then is my strength by thee renewed;
Then are my sins by thee forgiven;
Then dost thou cheer my solitude
With hopes of heaven.

[VERSE 4]
No words can tell what sweet relief
There for my every want I find;
What strength for warfare, balm for grief,
What peace of mind!

[VERSE 5]
Hushed is each doubt, gone every fear;
My spirit seems in heaven to stay;
And e’en the penitential tear
Is wiped away.

[VERSE 6]
Lord! till I reach that blissful shore,
No privilege so dear shall be
As thus my inmost soul to pour
In prayer to thee.

--- TITLE: Let me be with thee where thou art [item: PG46041-810]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c810
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
Let me be with thee where thou art,
My Saviour, my eternal Rest!
Then only will this longing heart
Be fully and for ever blest!

[VERSE 2]
Let me be with thee where thou art,
Where spotless saints thy name adore;
Then only will this sinful heart
Be evil and defiled no more!

[VERSE 3]
Let me be with thee where thou art,
Where none can die, where none remove;
There neither death nor life will part
Me from thy presence and thy love!

--- TITLE: My God, my Father, while I stray [item: PG46041-900]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c900
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
My God, my Father, while I stray
Far from my home, on life’s rough way,
O, teach me from my heart to say,
“Thy will be done!”

[VERSE 2]
What though in lonely grief I sigh
For friends beloved no longer nigh;
Submissive still would I reply,
“Thy will be done!”

[VERSE 3]
If thou shouldst call me to resign
What most I prize—it ne’er was mine;
I only yield thee what was thine:
“Thy will be done!”

[VERSE 4]
If but my fainting heart be blest
With thy sweet Spirit for its guest,
My God, to thee I leave the rest:
“Thy will be done!”

--- TITLE: Saviour! though my rebellious will [item: PG46041-998]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c998
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott.

[VERSE 1]
Saviour! though my rebellious will
Has been, by thy blest power, renewed;
Yet in its secret workings still
How much remains to be subdued!

[VERSE 2]
Oft I recall, with grief and shame,
How many years their course had run
Ere grace my murmuring heart o’ercame,
Ere I could say, “Thy will be done!”

[VERSE 3]
At length thy patient, wondrous love,
Unchanging, tender, pitying, strong,
Availed that stony heart to move,
Which had rebelled, alas! so long.

[VERSE 4]
Then was I taught by thee to say,
“Do with me what to thee seems best,
Give—take, whate’er thou wilt away,
Health, comfort, usefulness, or rest.

[VERSE 5]
“Be my whole life in suffering spent,
But let me be in suffering thine;
Still, O my Lord, I am content,
Thou now hast made thy pleasure mine.”
