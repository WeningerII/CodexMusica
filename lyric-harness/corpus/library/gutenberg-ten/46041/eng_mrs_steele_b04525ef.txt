--- TITLE: And is the gospel peace and love? [item: PG46041-143]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c143
--- AUTHOR: Mrs. Steele.

[VERSE 1]
And is the gospel peace and love?
Such let our conversation be:
The serpent blended with the dove—
Wisdom and meek simplicity.

[VERSE 2]
Whene’er the angry passions rise,
And tempt our thoughts or tongues to strife
On Jesus let us fix our eyes,
Bright pattern of the Christian life.

[VERSE 3]
O how benevolent and kind!
How mild! how ready to forgive!
Be his the temper of our mind,
And his the rules by which we live.

[VERSE 4]
To do his heavenly Father’s will
Was his employment and delight;
Humility, and love, and zeal,
Shone through his life divinely bright.

[VERSE 5]
Dispensing good where’er he came,
The labors of his life were love—
O! if we love the Saviour’s name,
Let his divine example move.

[VERSE 6]
But ah! how blind, how weak we are!
How frail, how apt to turn aside!
Lord, we depend upon thy care;
O may thy spirit be our guide!

[VERSE 7]
Thy fair example may we trace,
To teach us what we ought to be;
Make us, by thy transforming grace,
Lord Jesus, daily more like thee.

And did the holy and the just,
The Sovereign of the skies,
Stoop down to wretchedness and dust
That guilty man might rise?

[VERSE 2]
Yes, the Redeemer left his throne,
His radiant throne on high;
Surpassing mercy! love unknown!
To suffer, bleed, and die.

And suffered in our stead;

In thy most precious blood?
By this are sinners saved from hell,
And rebels brought to God.

Whence all our hopes and comforts flow:
Jesus, no other name but thine
Can save us from eternal woe.

[VERSE 2]
In vain would boasting reason find
Thy way to happiness and God;
Her weak directions leave the mind
Bewildered in a dubious road.

[VERSE 3]
No other name will heaven approve;
Thou art the true, the living way,
Ordained by everlasting love,
To the bright realms of endless day.

[VERSE 4]
Here let our constant feet abide,
Nor from the heavenly path depart;
O let thy Spirit, gracious Guide!
Direct our steps, and cheer our heart.

[VERSE 5]
Safe lead us through this world of night,
And bring us to the blissful plains—
The regions of unclouded light
Where perfect joy for ever reigns.

Here sweeter comforts cheer my heart,
Than all the round of nature gives.

One smile, one blissful smile of thine,
My dearest Lord, outweighs them all.

Thou art my life, my joy, my care;

Here safety dwells, and peace divine;
Still let me live beneath thine eye,
For life, eternal life, is thine.

My God, I bow before thy throne;

[VERSE 1]
Life is a span—a fleeting hour;
How soon the vapor flies!
Man is a tender, transient flower,
That, even in blooming, dies.

[VERSE 2]
The once-loved form, now cold and dead,
Each mournful thought employs;
And nature weeps her comforts fled,
And withered all her joys.

[VERSE 3]
Hope looks beyond the bounds of time,
When what we now deplore
Shall rise in full, immortal prime,
And bloom to fade no more.

[VERSE 4]
Cease then, fond nature, cease thy tears,
Religion points on high;
There everlasting spring appears,
And joys that can not die.

--- TITLE: When blooming youth is snatched away [item: PG46041-1058]

--- AUTHOR: Mrs. Steele.

[VERSE 1]
When blooming youth is snatched away
By death’s resistless hand,
Our hearts the mournful tribute pay,
Which pity must demand.

[VERSE 2]
While pity prompts the rising sigh,
O may this truth, impressed
With awful power, “I too must die,”
Sink deep in every breast.

[VERSE 3]
Let this vain world engage no more;
Behold the opening tomb:
It bids us seize the present hour:
To-morrow death may come.

[VERSE 4]
O let us fly—to Jesus fly,
Whose powerful arm can save;
Then shall our hopes ascend on high,
And triumph o’er the grave.

[VERSE 5]
Great God thy sovereign grace impart,
With cleansing, healing power;
This only can prepare the heart
For death’s approaching hour.

[VERSE 1]
O could our thoughts and wishes fly
Above these gloomy shades,
To those bright worlds beyond the sky,
Which sorrow ne’er invades!

[VERSE 2]
There joys, unseen by mortal eyes,
Or reason’s feeble ray,
In ever-blooming prospect rise,
Unconscious of decay.

[VERSE 3]

To guide our upward aim!
With one reviving touch of thine,
Our languid hearts inflame.

Our ardent wishes rise
To those bright scenes where pleasures spring,
Immortal to the skies.

--- TITLE: Lord of my life! O may thy praise [item: PG46041-1182]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1182
--- AUTHOR: Mrs. Steele.

[VERSE 1]
Lord of my life! O may thy praise
Employ my noblest powers,
Whose goodness lengthens out my days
And fills the circling hours.

[VERSE 2]
While many spent the night in sighs,
And restless pains and woes,
In gentle sleep I closed my eyes,
And undisturbed repose.

[VERSE 3]
O let the same Almighty care
My waking hours attend;
From every danger, every snare,
My heedless steps defend.

[VERSE 4]
Smile on my minutes as they roll,
And guide my future days;
And let thy goodness fill my soul
With gratitude and praise.
