--- TITLE: We lift our hearts to thee [item: PG46041-1314]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c1314
--- AUTHOR: J. Wesley.

[VERSE 1]
We lift our hearts to thee,
Thou Day-star from on high:
The sun itself is but thy shade,
Yet cheers both earth and sky.

[VERSE 2]
O, let thy rising beams
Dispel the shades of night;
And let the glories of thy love,
Come like the morning light!

[VERSE 3]
How beauteous nature now!
How dark and sad before!—
With joy we view the pleasing change,
And nature’s God adore.

[VERSE 4]
May we this life improve,
To mourn for errors past;
And live this short, revolving day,
As if it were our last.
