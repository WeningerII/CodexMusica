--- TITLE: Awake, and sing the song [item: PG46041-648]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c648
--- AUTHOR: Hammond.

[VERSE 1]
Awake, and sing the song
Of Moses and the Lamb!
Wake, every heart and every tongue,
To praise the Saviour’s name!

[VERSE 2]
Sing of his dying love!
Sing of his rising power!
Sing how he intercedes above
For those whose sins he bore!

[VERSE 3]
Sing on your heavenly way,
You ransomed sinners, sing;
Sing on, rejoicing every day
In Christ, the glorious King.

[VERSE 4]
Soon shall you hear him say,
“You blessed children, come,”
Soon will he call you hence away,
And take his pilgrims home.

--- TITLE: Lord, we come before thee now [item: PG46041-708]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c708
--- AUTHOR: Hammond.

[VERSE 1]
Lord, we come before thee now;
At thy feet we humbly bow:
O do not our suit disdain,
Shall we seek thee, Lord, in vain?

[VERSE 2]
Lord, on thee our souls depend,
In compassion now descend;
Fill our hearts with thy rich grace;
Tune our lips to sing thy praise.

[VERSE 3]
In thine own appointed way,
Now we seek thee; here we stay;
Lord, from hence we would not go,
Till a blessing thou bestow.

[VERSE 4]
Comfort those who weep and mourn;
Let the time of joy return;
Those that are cast down, lift up;
Make them strong in faith and hope.

[VERSE 5]
Grant that all may seek and find
Thee a God supremely kind;
Heal the sick; the captive free;
Let us all rejoice in thee.
