--- TITLE: Come to the house of prayer [item: PG46041-570]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c570
--- AUTHOR: E. Taylor.

[VERSE 1]
Come to the house of prayer,
O thou afflicted, come;
The God of peace shall meet thee there;
He makes that house his home.

[VERSE 2]
Come to the house of praise,
Ye who are happy now;
In sweet accord your voices raise,
In kindred homage bow.

[VERSE 3]
Ye agéd, hither come,
For you have felt his love;
Soon shall your trembling tongues be dumb,
Your lips forget to move.

[VERSE 4]
Ye young, before his throne
Come, bow; your voices raise;
Let not your hearts his praise disown
Who gives the power to praise.

[VERSE 5]
Thou, whose benignant eye
In mercy looks on all—
Who seest the tear of misery,
And hearest the mourner’s call—

[VERSE 6]
Up to thy dwelling-place
Bear our frail spirits on,
Till they outstrip time’s tardy pace,
And heaven on earth be won.
