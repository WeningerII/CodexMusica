--- TITLE: Purer yet and purer [item: PG46041-835]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c835
--- AUTHOR: Mason.

[VERSE 1]
Purer yet and purer
I would be in mind,
Dearer yet and dearer
Every duty find:

[VERSE 2]
Hoping still, and trusting
God without a fear
Patiently believing
He will make all clear:

[VERSE 3]
Calmer yet and calmer
Trial bear and pain,
Surer yet and surer
Peace at last to gain.

[VERSE 4]
Suffering still and doing,
To his will resigned,
And to God subduing
Heart, and will, and mind:

[VERSE 5]
Higher yet and higher,
Out of clouds and night,
Nearer yet and nearer
Rising to the light—

[VERSE 6]
Oft these earnest longings
Swell within my breast,
Yet their inner meaning
Ne’er can be expressed.
