--- TITLE: Welcome, O Saviour! to my heart [item: PG46041-359]
--- SOURCE: PG46041 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46041/pg46041-images.html#c359
--- AUTHOR: Bourne’s Coll.

[VERSE 1]
Welcome, O Saviour! to my heart;
Possess thy humble throne;
Bid every rival hence depart,
And claim me for thine own.

[VERSE 2]
The world and Satan I forsake—
To thee, I all resign;
My longing heart, O Jesus! take,
And fill with love divine.

[VERSE 3]
O! may I never turn aside,
Nor from thy bosom flee;
Let nothing here my heart divide—
I give it all to thee.
