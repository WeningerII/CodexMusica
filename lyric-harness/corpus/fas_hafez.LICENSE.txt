WHAT WAS REMOVED, AND WHY
-------------------------
`corpus/fas_hafez.json` is NOT the upstream file. It is the cleared PROJECTION
of it: each record keeps `id` and `poem` and nothing else. Three upstream
fields are dropped deliberately, per the `kavehbc/hafez` row in
`data/sources.tsv`:

  interpretation      495 modern Persian fal-divination prose entries, author
  alt_interpretation  unstated, date unstated. NOT cleared for provenance.
  mp3                 URLs to third-party recitation audio. Not ours to ship.

Only `poem[]` carries the 14th-century text. This is the old-text /
new-apparatus trap living inside a single JSON file, and the projection is
where it is cut.
