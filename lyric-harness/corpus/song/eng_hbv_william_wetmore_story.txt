--- TITLE: SNOWDROP
--- SOURCE: PG2620
[VERSE 1]
When, full of warm and eager love,
I clasp you in my fond embrace,
You gently push me back and say,
"Take care, my dear, you'll spoil my lace."
[VERSE 2]
You kiss me just as you would kiss
Some woman friend you chanced to see;
You call me "dearest." - All love's forms
Are yours, not its reality.
[VERSE 3]
Oh, Annie! cry, and storm, and rave!
Do anything with passion in it!
Hate me an hour, and then turn round
And love me truly, just one minute.

--- TITLE: The Violet
--- SOURCE: PG2621
[VERSE 1]
O faint, delicious, spring-time violet!
Thine odor, like a key,
Turns noiselessly in memory's wards to let
A thought of sorrow free.
[VERSE 2]
The breath of distant fields upon my brow
Blows through that open door
The sound of wind-borne bells, more sweet and low,
And sadder than of yore.
[VERSE 3]
It comes afar, from that beloved place,
And that beloved hour,
When life hung ripening in love's golden grace,
Like grapes above a bower.
[VERSE 4]
A spring goes singing through its reedy grass;
The lark sings o'er my head,
Drowned in the sky--O, pass, ye visions, pass!
I would that I were dead!--
[VERSE 5]
Why hast thou opened that forbidden door,
From which I ever flee?
O vanished Joy!  O Love, that art no more,
Let my vexed spirit be!
[VERSE 6]
O violet! thy odor through my brain
Hath searched, and stung to grief
This sunny day, as if a curse did stain
Thy velvet leaf.
