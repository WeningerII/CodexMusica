--- TITLE: THE MEETING PLACE
--- SOURCE: PG22515
[VERSE 1]
Where the faded flower shall freshen,
  Freshen never more to fade;
Where the shaded sky shall brighten,
  Brighten never more to shade:
Where the sun-blaze never scorches,
  Where the star-beams cease to chill;
Where no tempest stirs the echoes
  Of the wood, or wave, or hill:
Where the morn shall wake in gladness,
  And the noon the joy prolong,
Where the daylight dies in fragrance,
  'Mid the burst of holy song:
      Brother, we shall meet and rest
      'Mid the holy and the blest!
[VERSE 2]
Where no shadow shall bewilder,
  Where life's vain parade is o'er,
Where the sleep of sin is broken,
  And the dreamer dreams no more;
Where the bond is never sever'd,
  Partings, claspings, sob and moan,
Midnight waking, twilight weeping,
  Heavy noontide, all are done:
Where the child has found its mother,
  Where the mother finds the child,
Where dear families are gather'd
  That were scatter'd on the wild:
      Brother, we shall meet and rest
      'Mid the holy and the blest!
[VERSE 3]
Where the hidden wound is healèd,
  Where the blighted life re-blooms,
Where the smitten heart the freshness
  Of its buoyant youth resumes;
Where the love that here we lavish
  On the withering leaves of time,
Shall have fadeless flowers to fix on
  In an ever spring-bright clime:
Where we find the joy of loving,
  As we never loved before,
Loving on, unchill'd, unhinder'd,
  Loving once and evermore:
      Brother, we shall meet and rest
      'Mid the holy and the blest!
[VERSE 4]
Where a blasted world shall brighten
  Underneath a bluer sphere,
And a softer, gentler sunshine,
  Shed its healing splendour here;
Where earth's barren vales shall blossom,
  Putting on their robe of green,
And a purer, fairer Eden,
  Be where only wastes have been:
Where a king in kingly glory,
  Such as earth has never known,
Shall assume the righteous sceptre,
  Claim and wear the holy crown:
      Brother, we shall meet and rest
      'Mid the holy and the blest!

--- TITLE: TRUST NOT THESE SEAS AGAIN
--- SOURCE: PG22515
[VERSE 1]
Trust not these seas again,
  Though smooth and fair;
Trust not these waves again,
  Shipwreck is there.
[VERSE 2]
Trust not these stars again,
  Though bright and fair;
Trust not these skies again,
  Tempest is there.
[VERSE 3]
Trust not that breeze again,
  Gentle and fair;
Trust not these clouds again,
  Lightning is there.
[VERSE 4]
Trust not that isle again,
  Flower-crown'd and fair;
Trust not its rocks again,
  Earthquake is there.
[VERSE 5]
Trust not these flowers again,
  Fragrant and fair;
Trust not that rose again,
  Blighting is there.
[VERSE 6]
Trust not that earth again,
  Verdant and fair;
Trust not its fields again,
  Winter is there.
[VERSE 7]
Trust not these hopes again,
  Sunny and fair;
Trust not that smile again,
  Peril is there.
[VERSE 8]
Trust not this world again,
  Smiling and fair;
Trust not its sweets again,
  Wormwood is there;
[VERSE 9]
Trust not its love again,
  Sparkling and fair;
Trust not its joy again,
  Sorrow is there.

--- TITLE: Rejoice and be glad the Redeemer has come
--- FUNCTION: hymn
--- SOURCE: PG16455
--- AUTHOR: H. Bonar, 1874. — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 316, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
Rejoice and be glad the Redeemer has come!
Go look on his cradle, his cross and his tomb.
[REFRAIN]
Sound his praises, tell the story,
Of him who was slain,
Sound his praises, tell with gladness,
He liveth again.
[VERSE 2]
Rejoice and be glad: for the blood has been shed;
Redemption is finished, the price has been paid.
[VERSE 3]
Rejoice and be glad: for the Lamb that was slain,
O'er death is triumphant, and liveth again.
[VERSE 4]
Rejoice and be glad: for our King is on high;
He pleadeth for us on his throne in the sky.
[VERSE 5]
Rejoice and be glad: for he cometh again--
He cometh in glory, the Lamb that was slain.
