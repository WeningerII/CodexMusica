--- TITLE: One-and-Twenty
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
Long-expected one-and-twenty,
  Ling’ring year, at length is flown:
Pride and pleasure, pomp and plenty,
  Great *** ****, are now your own.
[VERSE 2]
Loosen’d from the minor’s tether,
  Free to mortgage or to sell,
Wild as wind, and light as feather,
  Bid the sons of thrift farewell.
[VERSE 3]
Call the Betsies, Kates, and Jennies,
  All the names that banish care;
Lavish of your grandsire’s guineas,
  Show the spirit of an heir.
[VERSE 4]
All that prey on vice and folly
  Joy to see their quarry fly:
There the gamester, light and jolly,
  There the lender, grave and sly.
[VERSE 5]
Wealth, my lad, was made to wander,
  Let it wander as it will;
Call the jockey, call the pander,
  Bid them come and take their fill.
[VERSE 6]
When the bonny blade carouses,
  Pockets full, and spirits high--
What are acres? What are houses?
  Only dirt, or wet or dry.
[VERSE 7]
Should the guardian friend or mother
  Tell the woes of wilful waste,
Scorn their counsel, scorn their pother;--
  You can hang or drown at last!

--- TITLE: On the Death of Mr. Robert Levet, a Practiser in Physic
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
Condemn’d to Hope’s delusive mine,
  As on we toil from day to day,
By sudden blasts or slow decline
  Our social comforts drop away.
[VERSE 2]
Well tried through many a varying year,
  See Levet to the grave descend,
Officious, innocent, sincere,
  Of every friendless name the friend.
[VERSE 3]
Yet still he fills affection’s eye,
  Obscurely wise and coarsely kind;
Nor, lettered Arrogance, deny
  Thy praise to merit unrefined.
[VERSE 4]
When fainting nature call’d for aid,
  And hov’ring death prepared the blow,
His vig’rous remedy display’d
  The power of art without the show.
[VERSE 5]
In Misery’s darkest cavern known,
  His useful care was ever nigh,
Where hopeless Anguish pour’d his groan,
  And lonely Want retired to die.
[VERSE 6]
No summons mock’d by chill delay,
  No petty gain disdain’d by pride;
The modest wants of every day
  The toil of every day supplied.
[VERSE 7]
His virtues walk’d their narrow round,
  Nor made a pause, nor left a void;
And sure th’ Eternal Master found
  The single talent well employ’d.
[VERSE 8]
The busy day, the peaceful night,
  Unfelt, uncounted, glided by;
His frame was firm--his powers were bright,
  Though now his eightieth year was nigh.
[VERSE 9]
Then with no fiery throbbing pain,
  No cold gradations of decay,
Death broke at once the vital chain,
  And freed his soul the nearest way.

--- TITLE: Epigram
--- SOURCE: PG2622
[VERSE 1]
If a man who turnips cries
Cry not when his father dies,
'Tis a proof that he would rather
Have a turnip than a father.
