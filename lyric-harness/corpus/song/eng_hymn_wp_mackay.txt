--- TITLE: We praise thee, O God! for the Son of thy love
--- AUTHOR: Wm. P. Mackay, 1866. — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 315, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
We praise thee, O God! for the Son of thy love,
For Jesus who died, and is now gone above.
[REFRAIN]
Hallelujah! thine the glory,
Hallelujah! Amen.
Hallelujah! thine the glory,
Revive us again.
[VERSE 2]
We praise thee, O God! for thy Spirit of light,
Who has shown us our Savior, and scattered our night.
[VERSE 3]
All glory and praise to the Lamb that was slain,
Who has borne all our sins, and has cleansed ev'ry stain.
[VERSE 4]
All glory and praise to the God of all grace,
Who has bought us, and sought us, and guided our ways.
[VERSE 5]
Revive us again; fill each heart with thy love;
May each soul be rekindled with fire from above.
