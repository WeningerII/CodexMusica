--- TITLE: Written in a Little Lady's Little Album
--- SOURCE: PG2619
[VERSE 1]
Hearts good and true
Have wishes few
In narrow circles bounded,
And hope that lives
On what God gives
Is Christian hope well founded.
[VERSE 2]
Small things are best;
Grief and unrest
To rank and wealth are given;
But little things
On little wings
Bear little souls to heaven.

--- TITLE: There's a wideness in God's mercy
--- FUNCTION: hymn
--- SOURCE: PG16455
--- AUTHOR: Frederick Faber, _ab._ — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 217, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
There's a wideness in God's mercy,
Like the wideness of the sea;
There's a kindness in his justice,
Which is more than liberty.
[REFRAIN]
He is calling, "Come to me;"
Lord, I'll gladly haste to thee.
[VERSE 2]
There's no place where earthly sorrows
Are more felt than up in heaven;
There's no place where earthly failings
Have such kindly judgment given.
[VERSE 3]
For the love of God is broader
Than the measure of man's mind,
And the heart of the Eternal
Is most wonderfully kind.
[VERSE 4]
But we make his love too narrow,
By false limits of our own;
And we magnify his strictness
With a zeal he will not own.
[VERSE 5]
Pining souls, come nearer Jesus;
Come, but come not doubting thus;
Come with faith that trusts more freely
His great tenderness for us.
[VERSE 6]
If our love were but more simple,
We should take him at his word;
And our lives would be all sunshine
In the sweetness of our Lord.
