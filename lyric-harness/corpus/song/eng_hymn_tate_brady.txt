--- TITLE: Oh, render thanks to God above
--- AUTHOR: Tate-Brady — the source's own signature under the last stanza, verbatim; a JOINT attribution and not a sole one
[VERSE 1]
Oh, render thanks to God above,
The fountain of eternal love;
Whose mercy firm, through ages past,
Hath stood, and shall forever last.
[VERSE 2]
Who can his mighty deeds express,
Not only vast--but numberless?
What mortal eloquence can raise
His tribute of immortal praise?
[VERSE 3]
Extend to me that favor, Lord,
Thou to thy chosen dost afford;
When thou return'st to set them free.
Let thy salvation visit me.
