--- TITLE: Stand up, and bless the Lord
[VERSE 1]
Stand up, and bless the Lord,
Ye people of his choice!
Stand up, and bless the Lord, your God,.
With heart, and soul, and voice.
[VERSE 2]
Though high above all praise,
Above all blessing high,
Who would not fear his holy name,
And laud and magnify?
[VERSE 3]
Oh, for the living flame
From his own altar brought,
To touch our lips, our minds inspire,
And wing to heaven our thought!
[VERSE 4]
God is our strength and song,
And his salvation ours;
Then be his love in Christ proclaimed,
With all our ransomed powers.

--- TITLE: Thank and praise Jehovah's name
[VERSE 1]
Thank and praise Jehovah's name;
For his mercies, firm and sure,
From eternity the same
To eternity endure.
[VERSE 2]
Let the ransomed thus rejoice,
Gathered out of every land;
As the people of his choice,
Plucked from the destroyer's hand.
[VERSE 3]
Praise him, ye who know his love;
Praise him from the depths beneath;
Praise him in the heights above;
Praise your Maker all that breathe.
[VERSE 4]
For his truth and mercy stand,
Past, and present, and to be,
Like the years of his right hand--
Like his own eternity.

--- TITLE: Oh, where shall rest be found
[VERSE 1]
Oh, where shall rest be found--
Rest for the weary soul?
'Twere vain the ocean depths to sound,
Or pierce to either pole.
[VERSE 2]
The world can never give
The bliss for which we sigh;
'Tis not the whole of life to live,
Nor all of death to die.
[VERSE 3]
Beyond this vale of tears
There is a life above,
Unmeasured by the flight of years;
And all that life is love.
[VERSE 4]
There is a death, whose pang
Outlasts the fleeting breath;
Oh, what eternal horrors hang
Around the second death!
[VERSE 5]
Lord God of truth and grace,
Teach us that death to shun,
Lest we be banished from thy face,
And evermore undone.

--- TITLE: Come, let us sing the song of songs
[VERSE 1]
Come, let us sing the song of songs,
With hearts and voices swell the strain;
The homage which to Christ belongs;--
"Worthy the Lamb, for he was slain!"
[VERSE 2]
Slain to redeem us by his blood,
To cleanse from every sinful stain;
And make us kings and priests to God:
"Worthy the Lamb, for he was slain!"
[VERSE 3]
To him who suffered on the tree,
Our souls, at his soul's price, to gain,
Blessing, and praise, and glory be!--
"Worthy the Lamb, for he was slain!"
[VERSE 4]
Come, Holy Spirit! from on high,
Our faith, our hope, our love sustain,
Living to sing, and dying cry,--
"Worthy the Lamb, for he was slain!"

--- TITLE: Lord! teach us how to pray aright
[VERSE 1]
Lord! teach us how to pray aright,
With reverence and with fear;
Though dust and ashes in thy sight,
We may, we must draw near.
[VERSE 2]
God of all grace, we come to thee,
With broken, contrite hearts,
Give, what thine eye delights to see,
Truth in the inward parts;
[VERSE 3]
Patience, to watch, and wait, and weep,
Though mercy long delay;
Courage, our fainting souls to keep,
And trust thee though thou slay.
[VERSE 4]
Give these, and then--thy will be done--
Thus strengthened with all might,
We by the Spirit and thy Son,
Shall pray, and pray aright.

--- TITLE: Prayer is the soul's sincere desire
[VERSE 1]
Prayer is the soul's sincere desire,
Uttered or unexpressed;
The motion of a hidden fire,
That trembles in the breast.
[VERSE 2]
Prayer is the burden of a sigh,
The falling of a tear,
The upward glancing of an eye,
When none but God is near.
[VERSE 3]
Prayer is the simplest form of speech,
That infant lips can try;
Prayer, the sublimest strains that reach
The Majesty on high.
[VERSE 4]
Prayer is the Christian's vital breath,
The Christian's native air:
His watchword at the gates of death;
He enters heaven with prayer.
[VERSE 5]
Oh, Thou, by whom we come to God,--
The Life, the Truth, the Way!
The path of prayer thyself hast trod;
Lord! teach us how to pray.

--- TITLE: Sow in the morn thy seed
[VERSE 1]
Sow in the morn thy seed,
At eve hold not thy hand;
To doubt and fear give thou no heed;
Broad-cast it o'er the land.
[VERSE 2]
And duly shall appear,
In verdure, beauty, strength,
The tender blade, the stalk, the ear,
And the full corn at length.
[VERSE 3]
Thou canst not toil in vain;
Cold, heat, and moist, and dry,
Shall foster and mature the grain,
For garners in the sky.
[VERSE 4]
Thence, when the glorious end,
The day of God, shall come,
The angel-reapers shall descend,
And heaven cry "Harvest-home!"

--- TITLE: God is my strong salvation
[VERSE 1]
God is my strong salvation;
What foe have I to fear?
In darkness and temptation,
My Light, my Help is near:
Though hosts encamp around me,
Firm to the fight I stand;
What terror can confound me,
With God at my right hand?
[VERSE 2]
Place on the Lord reliance;
My soul! with courage wait;
His truth be thine affiance,
When faint and desolate;
His might thy heart shall strengthen,
His love thy joy increase;
Mercy thy days shall lengthen;
The Lord will give thee peace.

--- TITLE: Daughter of Zion, from the dust
[VERSE 1]
Daughter of Zion, from the dust
Exalt thy fallen head;
Again in thy Redeemer trust--
He calls thee from the dead.
[VERSE 2]
Awake, awake, put on thy strength,
Thy beautiful array;
The day of freedom dawns at length--
The Lord's appointed day.
[VERSE 3]
Rebuild thy walls, thy bounds enlarge,
And send thy heralds forth;
Say to the South, Give up thy charge!
And, Keep not back, O North!
[VERSE 4]
They come, they come; thine exiled bands,
Where'er they rest or roam,
Have heard thy voice in distant lands,
And hasten to their home.

--- TITLE: Pour out thy Spirit from on high
[VERSE 1]
Pour out thy Spirit from on high;
Lord! thine assembled servants bless;
Graces and gifts to each supply.
And clothe thy priests with righteousness.
[VERSE 2]
Wisdom, and zeal, and faith impart,
Firmness with meekness from above,
To bear thy people on our heart,
And love the souls whom thou dost love;
[VERSE 3]
To watch and pray, and never faint;
By day and night, strict guard to keep;
To warn the sinner, cheer the saint,
Nourish thy lambs, and feed thy sheep.
[VERSE 4]
Then, when our work is finished here,
In humble hope, our charge resign;
When the chief Shepherd shall appear,
O God! may they and we be thine.

--- TITLE: Come in, beloved of the Lord
[VERSE 1]
Come in, beloved of the Lord,
Stranger nor foe art thou;
We welcome thee with warm accord,
Our friend, our brother, now.
[VERSE 2]
The hand of fellowship, the heart
Of love, we offer thee:
Leaving the world, thou dost but part
From lies and vanity.
[VERSE 3]
Come with us,--we will do thee good,
As God to us hath done;
Stand but in him, as those have stood
Whose faith the victory won.
[VERSE 4]
And when, by turns, we pass away,
And star by star grows dim,
May each, translated into day,
Be lost and found in him.

--- TITLE: "Forever with the Lord!"
[VERSE 1]
"Forever with the Lord!"
Amen, so let it be;
Life from the dead is in that word,
'Tis immortality.
Here in the body pent,
Absent from him I roam,
Yet nightly pitch my moving tent
A day's march nearer home;
Nearer home, nearer home,
A day's march nearer home.
[VERSE 2]
My Father's house on high,
Home of my soul, how near,
At times, to faith's aspiring eye
Thy golden gates appear.
Ah! then my spirit faints
To reach the land I love;
The bright inheritance of saints--
Jerusalem above;
Home above, home above,
Jerusalem above.
[VERSE 3]
Yet doubts still intervene,
And all my comfort flies;
Like Noah's dove, I flit between
Rough seas and stormy skies.
Anon the clouds depart,
The wind and waters cease,
While sweetly o'er my gladdened heart
Expands the bow of peace;
Bow of peace, bow of peace,
Expands the bow of peace.

--- TITLE: Our Heavenly Father, hear
[VERSE 1]
Our Heavenly Father, hear
The prayer we offer now:
Thy name be hallowed far and near,
To Thee all nations bow.
[VERSE 2]
Thy kingdom come; Thy will
On earth be done in love,
As saints and seraphim fulfil
Thy perfect law above.
[VERSE 3]
Our daily bread supply,
While by Thy word we live;
The guilt of our iniquity
Forgive as we forgive.
[VERSE 4]
From dark temptation's power
Our feeble hearts defend;
Deliver in the evil hour,
And guide us to the end.
[VERSE 5]
Thine, then, forever be
Glory and power divine;
The sceptre, throne, and majesty
Of heaven and earth are Thine.

--- TITLE: God of all grace, we come to Thee
[VERSE 1]
God of all grace, we come to Thee,
With humble, prayerful hearts;
Give what Thine eye delights to see,
Truth in the inward parts:
[VERSE 2]
Give deep humility; the sense
Of holy sorrow give:
A strong, desiring confidence,
To hear Thy voice and live:
[VERSE 3]
Patience, to watch, and wait, and weep,
Though mercy long delay;
Courage, our fainting souls to keep,
And trust Thee, though Thou slay.
[VERSE 4]
Give these,--and then Thy will be done!
Thus strengthened with all might,
We, in the spirit of Thy Son,
Shall pray, and pray aright.

--- TITLE: What shall we ask of God in prayer?
[VERSE 1]
What shall we ask of God in prayer?
Whatever good we want;
Whatever man may seek to share,
Or God in wisdom grant.
[VERSE 2]
Father of all our mercies,--Thou
In whom we move and live!
Hear us in heaven, Thy dwelling, now,
And answer, and forgive.
[VERSE 3]
When harassed by ten thousand foes,
Our helplessness we feel,
O, give the weary soul repose,
The wounded spirit heal!
[VERSE 4]
When dire temptations gather round,
And threaten or allure,
By storm or calm, in Thee be found
A refuge strong and sure.
[VERSE 5]
When age advances, may we grow
In faith, in hope, and love;
And walk in holiness below
To holiness above.

--- TITLE: Almighty God, in humble prayer
[VERSE 1]
Almighty God, in humble prayer
To Thee our souls we lift;
Do Thou our waiting minds prepare
For Thy most needful gift.
[VERSE 2]
We ask not golden streams of wealth
Along our path to flow;
We ask not undecaying health,
Nor length of years below:
[VERSE 3]
We ask not honors, which an hour
May bring and take away;
We ask not pleasure, pomp, or power,
Lest we should go astray:
[VERSE 4]
We ask for wisdom: Lord, impart
The knowledge how to live:
A wise and understanding heart
To all before Thee give.
[VERSE 5]
The young remember Thee in youth,
Before the evil days!
The old be guided by Thy truth
In wisdom's pleasant ways!

--- TITLE: Songs of praise the angels sang
[VERSE 1]
Songs of praise the angels sang,
Heaven with hallelujahs rang,
When, Creation's work begun,
Spake the Lord, and it was done.
[VERSE 2]
Songs of praise awoke the morn,
When the Prince of Peace was born;
Songs of praise arose, when he
Captive led captivity.
[VERSE 3]
Heaven and earth must pass away,
Songs of praise shall crown that day;
God will make new heavens and earth,
Songs of praise shall hail their birth.
[VERSE 4]
And shall man alone be dumb,
Till that glorious kingdom come?
No; his heart delights to raise
Psalms and hymns and songs of praise.
[VERSE 5]
Saints below, with heart and voice,
Still in songs of praise rejoice,
Learning here, by faith and love,
Songs of praise to sing above.

--- TITLE: God, in the high and holy place
[VERSE 1]
God, in the high and holy place,
Looks down upon the spheres;
Yet in His providence and grace
To every eye appears.
[VERSE 2]
He bows the heavens; the mountains stand,
A highway for our God:
He walks amidst the desert-land;
'Tis Eden where He trod.
[VERSE 3]
The forests in His strength rejoice;
Hark! on the evening breeze,
As once of old, the Lord God's voice
Is heard among the trees.
[VERSE 4]
In every stream His bounty flows,
Diffusing joy and wealth;
In every breeze His Spirit blows,--
The breath of life and health.
[VERSE 5]
His blessings fall in plenteous showers
Upon the lap of earth,
That teems with foliage, fruits, and flowers,
And rings with infant mirth.
[VERSE 6]
If God hath made this world so fair,
Where sin and death abound,
How beautiful beyond compare
Will Paradise be found!

--- TITLE: According to thy gracious word
[VERSE 1]
According to thy gracious word,
In meek humility,
This will I do, my dying Lord,
I will remember thee.
[VERSE 2]
Thy body, broken for my sake,
My bread from heaven shall be;
Thy testamental cup I take,
And thus remember thee.
[VERSE 3]
Gethsemane can I forget?
Or there thy conflict see,
Thine agony and bloody sweat,
And not remember thee?
[VERSE 4]
When to the cross I turn mine eyes,
And rest on Calvary,
O Lamb of God, my sacrifice!
I must remember thee;--
[VERSE 5]
Remember thee, and all thy pains,
And all thy love to me;
Yea, while a breath, a pulse, remains,
Will I remember thee.

--- TITLE: Go to dark Gethsemane
[VERSE 1]
Go to dark Gethsemane,
Ye that feel temptation's power,
Your Redeemer's conflict see,
Watch with him one bitter hour;
Turn not from his griefs away,
Learn of Jesus Christ to pray!
[VERSE 2]
Follow to the judgment-hall,
View the Lord of life arraigned;
O the wormwood and the gall!
O the griefs his soul sustained!
Shun not suffering, shame, or loss;
Learn of him to bear the cross!
[VERSE 3]
Calvary's mournful mountain climb;
There, admiring at his feet,
Mark that miracle of time,
Love's own sacrifice complete;
"It is finished," hear him cry;
Learn of Jesus Christ to die!

--- TITLE: God made all his creatures free
[VERSE 1]
God made all his creatures free;
Life itself is liberty;
God ordained no other bands
Than united hearts and hands.
[VERSE 2]
Sin the primal charter broke,--
Sin, itself earth's heaviest yoke;
Tyranny with sin began,
Man o'er brute, and man o'er man.
[VERSE 3]
But a better day shall be,
Life again be liberty,
And the wide world's only bands
Love-knit hearts and love-linked hands.
[VERSE 4]
So shall every slavery cease,
All God's children dwell in peace,
And the new-born earth record
Love, and Love alone, is Lord.

--- TITLE: Hasten, Lord, to my release
[VERSE 1]
Hasten, Lord, to my release;
Haste to help me, O my God!
Foes like armed bands increase;--
Turn them back the way they trod.
[VERSE 2]
Dark temptations round me press,
Evil thoughts my soul assail;
Doubts and fears, in my distress,
Rise, till flesh and spirit fail.
[VERSE 3]
Thou mine only helper art,
My redeemer from the grave;
Strength of my desiring heart,
Father! Helper! haste to save!

--- TITLE: Return, my soul, unto thy rest
[VERSE 1]
Return, my soul, unto thy rest,
From vain pursuits and maddening cares;
From lonely woes that wring thy breast,
The world's allurements, toils, and snares.
[VERSE 2]
Return unto thy rest, my soul,
From all the wanderings of thy thought;
From sickness unto death made whole;
Safe through a thousand perils brought.
[VERSE 3]
Then to thy rest, my soul, return,
From passions every hour at strife;
Sin's works, and ways, and wages, spurn,
Lay hold upon eternal life.
[VERSE 4]
God is thy rest; with heart inclined
To keep His word, that word believe;
Christ is thy rest; with lowly mind,
His light and easy yoke receive.

--- TITLE: As the hart, with eager looks
[VERSE 1]
As the hart, with eager looks,
Panteth for the water-brooks,
So my soul, athirst for Thee,
Pants the living God to see;
When, O, when, without a fear,
Lord, shall I to Thee draw near?
[VERSE 2]
Why art thou cast down, my soul?
God, thy God, shall make thee whole;
Why art thou disquieted?
God shall lift thy fallen head,
And His countenance benign
Be the saving health of thine.

--- TITLE: O, where shall rest be found
[VERSE 1]
O, where shall rest be found,
Rest for the weary soul?
'Twere vain the ocean depths to sound,
Or pierce to either pole:
[VERSE 2]
The world can never give
The rest for which we sigh;
'Tis not the whole of life to live,
Nor all of death to die.
[VERSE 3]
In Thee we end our quest;
Alone are found in Thee
The life of perfect love,--the rest
Of immortality.

--- TITLE: Heaven is a state of rest from sin
[VERSE 1]
Heaven is a state of rest from sin;
But all who hope to enter there
Must here that holy course begin,
Which shall their souls for rest prepare.
[VERSE 2]
Clean hearts, O God, in us create!
Right spirits, Lord, in us renew!
Commence we now that higher state,
Now do Thy will as angels do.
[VERSE 3]
In Jesus' footsteps may we tread,
Learn every lesson of his love;
And be from grace to glory led,
From heaven below to heaven above.

--- TITLE: The Lord is my Shepherd, no want shall I know
[VERSE 1]
The Lord is my Shepherd, no want shall I know;
I feed in green pastures, safe folded I rest;
He leadeth my soul where the still waters flow,
Restores me when wandering, redeems when opprest.
Through the valley and shadow of death though I stray,
Since Thou art my guardian, no evil I fear;
Thy rod shall defend me, Thy staff be my stay;
No harm can befall with my Comforter near.
[VERSE 2]
In the midst of affliction my table is spread;
With blessings unmeasured my cup runneth o'er;
With perfume and oil Thou anointest my head;
O, what shall I ask of Thy providence more?
[VERSE 3]
Let goodness and mercy, my bountiful God,
Still follow my steps till I meet Thee above;
I seek, by the path which my forefathers trod
Through the land of their sojourn, Thy kingdom of love.

--- TITLE: Within Thy courts have millions met
[VERSE 1]
Within Thy courts have millions met,
Millions this day before Thee bowed;
Their faces heavenward were set,
Their vows to Thee, O God! they vowed.
[VERSE 2]
Still as the light of morning broke
O'er island, continent, and deep,
Thy far-spread family awoke,
Sabbath all round the world to keep.
[VERSE 3]
From east to west the sun surveyed,
From north to south, adoring throngs;
And still where evening stretched her shade
The stars came forth to hear their songs.
[VERSE 4]
And not a prayer, a tear, a sigh,
Hath failed this day some suit to gain;
To hearts that sought Thee Thou wast nigh
Nor hath one sought Thy face in vain.
[VERSE 5]
The poor in spirit Thou hast fed,
The feeble soul hath strengthened been.
The mourner Thou hast comforted,
The pure in heart their God have seen.
[VERSE 6]
And Thou, soul-searching God! hast known
The hearts of all that bent the knee,
And all their prayers have reached Thy throne,
In soul and truth who worshipped Thee.

--- TITLE: Go to the grave in all thy glorious prime
[VERSE 1]
Go to the grave in all thy glorious prime,
In full activity of zeal and power:
A Christian cannot die before his time;
The Lord's appointment is the servant's hour.
[VERSE 2]
Go to the grave; at noon from labor cease;
Rest on thy sheaves, thy harvest work is done;
Come from the heat of battle, and in peace,
Soldier, go home; with thee the field is won.
[VERSE 3]
Go to the grave, for there thy Saviour lay,
In death's embraces, ere he rose on high;
And all the ransomed, by that narrow way,
Pass to eternal life beyond the sky.
[VERSE 4]
Go to the grave,--no, to thy home above;
Be thy pure spirit present with the Lord,
Where thou for faith and hope hast perfect love,
And open vision for the written word.

--- TITLE: Ages, ages have departed
[VERSE 1]
Ages, ages have departed,
Since the first dark vessel bore
Afric's children, broken-hearted,
To this far-off western shore;
She, like Rachel,
Weeping, for they were no more.
[VERSE 2]
Millions, millions have been slaughtered
In the fight and on the deep;
Millions, millions more have watered,
With such tears as captives weep,
Fields of labor
Where their wasted bodies sleep.
[VERSE 3]
Mercy, mercy, vainly pleading,
Rends her garments, smites her breast,
Till a voice from heaven proceeding
Gladden all the waiting west:
"Come, ye weary!
Come, and I will give you rest!"
[VERSE 4]
Tidings, tidings of salvation!
Brothers, rise with one accord,
Purge the plague-spot from our nation,
Till, unto their rights restored,
Slaves no longer,
All are freemen in the Lord!

--- TITLE: Climb we the mountain afar
[VERSE 1]
Climb we the mountain afar,
In the still hour of even;
Led by yon beautiful star,
First of the daughters of heaven:
Darkness yet covers the face of the deep;
Spirit of freedom! go forth in thy might,
Break the slave's bondage like infancy's sleep,
The moment when God shall say, Let there be light!
[VERSE 2]
Gaze we meanwhile for the day,
Praying in thought while we gaze;
Watch for the morning's first ray;
Prayer then be turned into praise!
Shout to the valleys, Behold ye the morn,
Long, long desired, but denied to our sight;
Lo, myriads of slaves into men are new-born;
The word was omnipotent, and there is light!

--- TITLE: Heralds of creation! cry
[VERSE 1]
Heralds of creation! cry,--
Praise the Lord, the Lord most high!
Heaven and earth! obey the call;
Praise the Lord, the Lord of all.
[VERSE 2]
For He spake, and forth from night
Sprang the universe to light;
He commanded,--nature heard,
And stood fast upon His word.
[VERSE 3]
Praise Him, all ye hosts above;
Spirits perfected in love!
Sun and moon! your voices raise;
Sing, ye stars! your Maker's praise.
[VERSE 4]
Earth! from all thy depths below,
Ocean's hallelujahs flow;
Lightning, vapor, wind, and storm,
Hail and snow! His will perform.
[VERSE 5]
Birds! on wings of rapture soar,
Warble at His temple's door;
Joyful sounds from herds and flocks,
Echo back, ye caves and rocks!
[VERSE 6]
High above all height His throne;
Excellent His name alone;
Him let all His works confess!
Him let all His children bless!

--- TITLE: All ye nations, praise the Lord
[VERSE 1]
All ye nations, praise the Lord;
All ye lands, your voices raise;
Heaven and earth, with loud accord,
Praise the Lord, forever praise.
[VERSE 2]
For His truth and mercy stand,
Past, and present, and to be,
Like the years of His right hand,
Like His own eternity.
[VERSE 3]
Praise Him, ye who know His love;
Praise Him, from the depths beneath;
Praise Him in the heights above;
Praise your Maker, all that breathe!

--- TITLE: One prayer I have,--all prayers in one
[VERSE 1]
One prayer I have,--all prayers in one,
When I am wholly Thine;
Thy will, my God, Thy will be done,
And let that will be mine.
[VERSE 2]
All-wise, almighty, and all-good,
In Thee I firmly trust;
Thy ways, unknown or understood,
Are merciful and just.
[VERSE 3]
May I remember that to Thee
Whate'er I have I owe;
And back in gratitude from me
May all Thy bounties flow.
[VERSE 4]
Thy gifts are only then enjoyed,
When used as talents lent;
Those talents only well employed,
When in Thy service spent.
[VERSE 5]
And though Thy wisdom takes away,
Shall I arraign Thy will?
No, let me bless Thy name, and say
"The Lord is gracious still."
[VERSE 6]
A pilgrim through the earth I roam,
Of nothing long possessed,
And all must fail when I go home,
For this is not my rest.

--- TITLE: Call the Lord thy sure salvation
[VERSE 1]
Call the Lord thy sure salvation,
Rest beneath the Almighty's shade;
In His secret habitation
Dwell, nor ever be dismayed!
[VERSE 2]
There no tumult can alarm thee,
Thou shalt dread no hidden snare,
Guile nor violence shall harm thee,
In eternal safeguard there.
[VERSE 3]
There, though winds and waves are swelling,
God, thy hope, shall bear through all;
Plague shall not come nigh thy dwelling,
Thee no evil shall befall.
[VERSE 4]
He shall charge His angel legions
Watch and ward o'er thee to keep,
Though thou walk through hostile regions,
Though in desert wilds thou sleep.
[VERSE 5]
Since, with pure and firm affection,
Thou on God hast set thy love,
With the wings of His protection
He shall shield thee from above.

--- TITLE: Out of the depths of woe
[VERSE 1]
Out of the depths of woe,
To Thee, O Lord, I cry;
Darkness surrounds Thee, but I know
That Thou art ever nigh.
[VERSE 2]
Like them whose longing eyes
Watch till the morning star,
Though late and seen through tempests, rise,
Heaven's portals to unbar,--
[VERSE 3]
Like them I watch and pray;
And though it tarry long,
Catch the first gleam of welcome day
Then burst into a song.
[VERSE 4]
Glory to God above!
The waters soon will cease;
For lo, the swift returning dove
Brings home the sign of peace.
[VERSE 5]

--- TITLE: In time of tribulation
[VERSE 1]
In time of tribulation,
Hear, Lord, our earnest cries;
With humble supplication
To Thee the spirit flies.
[VERSE 2]
Remembered songs of gladness,
Through night's lone silence brought,
Strike notes of deepest sadness,
And stir desponding thought.
[VERSE 3]
Hath God cast off forever?
Can time His truth impair?
His tender mercy never
Shall we presume to share?
[VERSE 4]
Hath He His loving-kindness
Shut up in bitter wrath?
No! it is human blindness,
That cannot see His path.
[VERSE 5]
We'll call to recollection
The years of Thy right hand,
And, strong in Thy protection,
Again through Faith we stand.
[VERSE 6]
Thy way is in great waters,
Thy footsteps are not known;
But let earth's sons and daughters
Confide in Thee alone!
[VERSE 7]
Through the wild sea Thou leddest
Thy chosen flock of yore;
Still on the wave thou treadest,
And Thy redeemed pass o'er.

--- TITLE: Yea, I will extol Thee
[VERSE 1]
Yea, I will extol Thee,
Lord of Life and light!
For Thine arm upheld me,
Turned my foes to flight.
[VERSE 2]
I implored Thy succor,
Thou wast swift to save,
To heal my wounded spirit,
And bring me from the grave.
[VERSE 3]
Grief may, like the pilgrim,
Through the night sojourn,
Yet shall joy, to-morrow,
With the sun return.
[VERSE 4]
Thou hast turned my mourning
Into minstrelsy;
Girded me with gladness,
Set from thraldom free.
[VERSE 5]
Thee my ransomed powers
Henceforth shall adore;
Thee, my great Deliverer,
Bless forevermore!

--- TITLE: The glorious universe around
[VERSE 1]
The glorious universe around,
The heavens with all their train,
Sun, moon and stars, are firmly bound
In one mysterious chain.
[VERSE 2]
The earth, the ocean, and the sky,
To form one world agree;
Where all that walk, or swim, or fly,
Compose one family.
[VERSE 3]
God in creation thus displays
His wisdom and His might;
While all His works with all His ways
Harmoniously unite.
[VERSE 4]
In one fraternal bond of love,
One fellowship of mind,
The saints below and saints above
Their bliss and glory find.
[VERSE 5]
Here, in their house of pilgrimage,
Thy statutes are their song;
There, through one bright, eternal age,
Thy praises they prolong.
[VERSE 6]
Lord, may our union form a part
Of that thrice happy whole;
Derive its pulse from Thee the heart,
Its life from Thee the soul.

--- TITLE: O Spirit of the living God
[VERSE 1]
O Spirit of the living God,
In all Thy plenitude of grace,
Where'er the foot of man hath trod,
Descend on our benighted race!
[VERSE 2]
Be darkness, at Thy coming, light;
Confusion, order, in Thy path;
Souls without strength inspire with might;
Bid mercy triumph over wrath.
[VERSE 3]
O spirit of the Lord! prepare
All the round earth her God to meet;
Breathe Thou abroad like morning air,
Till hearts of stone begin to beat.
[VERSE 4]
Baptize the nations; far and nigh
The triumphs of the cross record;
Thy name, O Father, glorify,
Till every people call Thee Lord.

--- TITLE: God comes, with succor speedy
[VERSE 1]
God comes, with succor speedy,
To those who suffer wrong;
To help the poor and needy,
And bid the weak be strong;
He comes to break oppression,
And set the captive free,
To take away transgression,
And rule in equity.
[VERSE 2]
He shall come down, as showers
Upon the thirsty earth;
And joy and hope, like flowers,
Spring in His path to birth.
Before Him, on the mountains,
Shall Peace, the herald, go,
And Righteousness, in fountains,
From hill to valley flow.
[VERSE 3]
To Him shall prayer unceasing,
And daily vows, ascend;
His kingdom still increasing,
A kingdom without end.
The tide of time shall never
His covenant remove;
His name shall stand forever;
His great, best name of Love.
