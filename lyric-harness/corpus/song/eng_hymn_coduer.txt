--- TITLE: Lord! I hear of showers of blessing
--- AUTHOR: Elizabeth Coduer, 1860. — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 269, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
Lord! I hear of showers of blessing,
Thou art scattering, broad and free;
Showers, the thirsty land refreshing;
Let their fullness fall on me.
[REFRAIN]
Even me, even me,
Let their fullness fall on me.
[VERSE 2]
Pass me not, oh, gracious Father!
Sinful, though my heart may be;
Thou might'st curse me, but the rather
Let thy mercy fall on me.
[VERSE 3]
Pass me not, oh, tender Savior!
Let me love and cling to thee;
I am longing for thy favor;
When thou comest, call for me.
[VERSE 4]
Pass me not, oh, mighty Spirit!
Thou canst make the blind to see;
Witnesser of Jesus' merit,
Speak the word of power to me.
[VERSE 5]
Have I long in sin been sleeping,
Long been slighting, grieving thee?
Has the world my heart been keeping?
Oh! forgive and rescue me.
[VERSE 6]
Love of God, so pure and changeless,--
Blood of God, so rich and free,--
Grace of God, so strong and boundless,--
Magnify them all in me.
