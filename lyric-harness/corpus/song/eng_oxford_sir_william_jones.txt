--- TITLE: Epigram
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
On parent knees, a naked new-born child,
Weeping thou sat’st while all around thee smiled:
So live, that sinking to thy life’s last sleep,
Calm thou may’st smile, whilst all around thee weep.
