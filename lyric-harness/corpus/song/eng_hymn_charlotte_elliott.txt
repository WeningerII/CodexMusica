--- TITLE: O holy Savior! Friend unseen
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott. — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 279, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
O holy Savior! Friend unseen,
Since on thine arm thou bid'st me lean,
Help me, throughout life's changing scene,
By faith to cling to thee!
[REFRAIN]
Help me cling to thee, Savior,
Help me cling to thee!
Help me, throughout life's changing scene,
By faith to cling to thee!
[VERSE 2]
Without a murmur I dismiss
My former dreams of earthly bliss;
My joy, my recompense be this,
Each hour to cling to thee!
[VERSE 3]
Though faith and hope are often tried,
I ask not, need not, aught beside;
So safe, so calm, so satisfied,
The soul that clings to thee!

--- TITLE: O thou, the contrite sinner's Friend
--- AUTHOR: Charlotte Elliott. — the source's own signature under the last stanza, hymn 354, verbatim
[VERSE 1]
O thou, the contrite sinner's Friend,
Who loving, lov'st them to the end,
On this alone my hopes depend
That thou wilt plead for me.
[REFRAIN]
O Savior, plead for me,
O Savior, plead for me,
On this alone my hopes depend
That thou wilt plead for me.
[VERSE 2]
When weary in the Christian race,
Far off appears my resting place,
And, fainting, I mistrust thy grace,
Then, Savior, plead for me.
[VERSE 3]
When I have erred and gone astray,
Afar from thine and wisdom's way,
And see no glimmering, guiding ray,
Still, Savior, plead for me.
[VERSE 4]
When Satan, by my sins made bold,
Strives from thy cross to loose my hold,
Then with thy pitying arms enfold,
And plead, oh, plead for me!
[VERSE 5]
And when my dying hour draws near,
Darkened with anguish, guilt and fear,
Then to my fainting sight appear,
Pleading in heaven for me.
