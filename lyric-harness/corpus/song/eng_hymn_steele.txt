--- TITLE: Awake, my soul, awake my tongue
[VERSE 1]
Awake, my soul, awake my tongue,
My God demands the grateful song;
Let all my inmost powers record
The wondrous mercy of the Lord.
[VERSE 2]
Divinely free his mercy flows,
Forgives my sins, allays my woes,
And bids approaching death remove,
And crowns me with indulgent love.
[VERSE 3]
His mercy, with unchanging rays,
Forever shines, while time decays;
And children's children shall record
The truth and goodness of the Lord.
[VERSE 4]
While all his works his praise proclaim
And men and angels bless his name,
Oh, let my heart, my life, my tongue
Attend, and join the blissful song!

--- TITLE: Father of mercies, in thy word
[VERSE 1]
Father of mercies, in thy word
What endless glory shines!
Forever be thy name adored
For these celestial lines.
[VERSE 2]
Here may the wretched sons of want
Exhaustless riches find--
Riches above what earth can grant,
And lasting as the mind.
[VERSE 3]
Here the fair tree of knowledge grows,
And yields a free repast;
Sublimer sweets than nature knows
Invite the longing taste.
[VERSE 4]
Here the Redeemer's welcome voice
Spreads heavenly peace around;
And life and everlasting joys
Attend the blissful sound.
[VERSE 5]
Oh, may these heavenly pages be
My ever dear delight;
And still new beauties may I see
And still increasing light.

--- TITLE: How helpless guilty nature lies
[VERSE 1]
How helpless guilty nature lies,
Unconscious of its load!
The heart, unchanged, can never rise
To happiness and God.
[VERSE 2]
Can aught, beneath a power divine,
The stubborn will subdue?
'Tis thine, almighty Spirit! thine,
To form the heart anew.
[VERSE 3]
'Tis thine, the passions to recall,
And upward bid them rise;
To make the scales of error fall,
From reason's darkened eyes.
[VERSE 4]
Oh! change these wretched hearts of ours,
And give them life divine;
Then shall our passions and our powers,
Almighty Lord! be thine.

--- TITLE: Ye wretched, hungry, starving poor
[VERSE 1]
Ye wretched, hungry, starving poor!
Behold a royal feast,
Where mercy spreads her bounteous store,
For every humble guest.
[VERSE 2]
See, Jesus stands, with open arms;
He calls,--he bids you come;
Guilt holds you back, and fear alarms;
But, see! there yet is room.
[VERSE 3]
Room, in the Savior's bleeding heart;
There love and pity meet;
Nor will he bid the soul depart,
That trembles at his feet
[VERSE 4]
Oh! come, and with his children taste
The blessings of his love:
While hope attends the sweet repast
Of nobler joys above.
[VERSE 5]
There, with united heart and voice,
Before th' eternal throne,
Ten thousand thousand souls rejoice,
In ecstasies unknown.
[VERSE 6]
And yet ten thousand thousand more
Are welcome still to come;
Ye longing souls! the grace adore,
Approach, there yet is room.

--- TITLE: Father! whate'er of earthly bliss
[VERSE 1]
Father! whate'er of earthly bliss
Thy sovereign hand denies,
Accepted at thy throne of grace,
Let this petition rise:--
[VERSE 2]
"Give me a calm, a thankful heart,
From every murmur free;
The blessings of thy grace impart,
And let me live to thee.
[VERSE 3]
"Let the sweet hope that thou art mine
My path of life attend;
Thy presence through my journey shine,
And bless its happy end."

--- TITLE: My soul no more shall strive in vain
[VERSE 1]
My soul no more shall strive in vain,
Slave to the world, and slave to sin!
A nobler toil I will sustain,
A nobler satisfaction win.
[VERSE 2]
I will resolve, with all my heart,
With all my powers, to serve the Lord;
Nor from His precepts e'er depart,
Whose service is a rich reward.
[VERSE 3]
O, be His service all my joy!
Around let my example shine,
Till others love the blest employ,
And join in labors so divine.
[VERSE 4]
O, may I never faint nor tire,
Nor, wandering, leave His sacred ways;
Great God! accept my soul's desire,
And give me strength to live Thy praise.


--- TITLE: There is a God--all nature speaks
--- SOURCE: PG16455
[VERSE 1]
There is a God--all nature speaks,
  Through earth, and air, and sea, and skies;
See, from the clouds his glory breaks,
  When earliest beams of morning rise.
[VERSE 2]
The rising sun, serenely bright,
  Throughout the world's extended frame,
Inscribes in characters of light
  His mighty Maker's glorious name.
[VERSE 3]
Ye curious minds, who roam abroad,
  And trace creation's wonders o'er,
Confess the footsteps of your God--
  Bow down before him and adore.

--- TITLE: Awake, awake, the sacred song
--- SOURCE: PG16455
[VERSE 1]
Awake, awake, the sacred song,
  To our incarnate Lord;
Let every heart and every tongue
  Adore th' eternal Word.
[VERSE 2]
That awful Word, that sovereign Power,
  By whom the worlds were made;
Oh, happy morn--illustrious hour--
  Was once in flesh arrayed.
[VERSE 3]
To dwell with misery here below,
  The Savior left the skies,
And sunk to wretchedness and woe,
  That worthless man might rise.
[VERSE 4]
Adoring angels tuned their songs,
  To hail the joyful day;
With rapture, then, let human tongues
  Their grateful worship pay.

--- TITLE: So fades the lovely, blooming flower--
--- SOURCE: PG16455
[VERSE 1]
So fades the lovely, blooming flower--
Frail smiling solace of an hour!
So soon our transient comforts fly,
And pleasure only blooms to die.
[VERSE 2]
Is there no kind, no lenient art,
To heal the anguish of the heart?
Spirit of grace! be ever nigh,
Thy comforts are not made to die.
[VERSE 3]
Bid gentle patience smile on pain,
Till dying hope shall live again;
Hope wipes the tear from sorrow's eye
And faith points upward to the sky.
