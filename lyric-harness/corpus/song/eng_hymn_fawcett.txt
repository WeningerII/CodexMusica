--- TITLE: Thy presence, gracious God! afford
[VERSE 1]
Thy presence, gracious God! afford:
Prepare us to receive thy word;
Now let thy voice engage our ear,
And faith be mixed with what we hear.
[VERSE 2]
Distracting thoughts and cares remove,
And fix our hearts and hopes above;
With food divine may we be fed
And satisfied with living bread.
[VERSE 3]
To us thy sacred word apply,
With sovereign power and energy;
And may we, in thy faith and fear,
Reduce to practice what we hear.
[VERSE 4]
Father, in us thy Son reveal;
Teach us to know and do thy will;
Thy saving power and love display.
And guide us to the realms of day.

--- TITLE: How precious is the book divine
[VERSE 1]
How precious is the book divine,
By inspiration given!
Bright as a lamp its doctrines shine,
To guide our souls to heaven.
[VERSE 2]
Its light, descending from above,
Our gloomy world to cheer,
Displays a Savior's boundless love,
And brings his glories near.
[VERSE 3]
It sweetly cheers our drooping hearts,
In this dark vale of tears;
Life, light, and joy it still imparts,
And quells our rising fears.
[VERSE 4]
This lamp, through all the tedious night
Of life, shall guide our way,
Till we behold the clearer light
Of an eternal day.

--- TITLE: Thy way, O God! is in the sea
[VERSE 1]
Thy way, O God! is in the sea,
Thy paths I cannot trace;
Nor comprehend the mystery
Of thine unbounded grace.
[VERSE 2]
'Tis but in part I know thy will;
I bless thee for the sight;
When will thy love the rest reveal,
In glory's clearer light?
[VERSE 3]
Here the dark veils of flesh and sense
My captive soul surround;
Mysterious deeps of providence
My wondering thoughts confound.
[VERSE 4]
As through a glass I dimly see
The wonders of thy love;
How little do I know of thee,
Or of the joys above!
[VERSE 5]
With rapture I shall soon survey
Thy providence and grace;
And spend an everlasting day
In wonder, love, and praise.

--- TITLE: Our sins on Christ were laid
[VERSE 1]
Our sins on Christ were laid;
He bore the mighty load;
Our ransom price he fully paid
In groans, and tears, and blood.
[VERSE 2]
To save a world he dies;
Sinners, behold the Lamb!
To him lift up your longing eyes;
Seek mercy in his name.
[VERSE 3]
Pardon and peace abound;
He will your sins forgive;
Salvation in his name is found,--
He bids the sinner live.
[VERSE 4]
Jesus, we look to thee;--
Where else can sinners go?
Thy boundless love shall set us free
From wretchedness and woe.

--- TITLE: Sinners, this solemn truth regard
[VERSE 1]
Sinners, this solemn truth regard,
Hear, all ye sons of men;
For Christ, the Savior, hath declared,
"Ye must be born again."
[VERSE 2]
Whate'er might be your birth or blood,
The sinner's boast is vain;
Thus saith the glorious Son of God,
"Ye must be born again."
[VERSE 3]
That which is born of flesh is flesh,
And flesh it will remain:
Then marvel not that Jesus saith,
"Ye must be born again."
[VERSE 4]
Spirit of life, thy grace impart,
And breathe on sinners slain:
Bear witness, Lord, in ev'ry heart,
That we are born again.

--- TITLE: Blest be the tie that binds
[VERSE 1]
Blest be the tie that binds
Our hearts in Christian love!
The fellowship of kindred minds
Is like to that above.
[VERSE 2]
Before our Father's throne,
We pour our ardent prayers;
Our fears, our hopes, our aims are one,
Our comforts and our cares.
[VERSE 3]
We share our mutual woes;
Our mutual burdens bear;
And often for each other flows
The sympathizing tear.
[VERSE 4]
When we asunder part,
It gives us inward pain;
But we shall still be joined in heart,
And hope to meet again.
[VERSE 5]
This glorious hope revives
Our courage by the way;
While each in expectation lives,
And longs to see the day.
[VERSE 6]
From sorrow, toil, and pain,
And sin we shall be free;
And perfect love and friendship reign
Through all eternity.
