--- TITLE: On the Death of a particular Friend
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
As those we love decay, we die in part,
String after string is sever’d from the heart;
Till loosen’d life, at last but breathing clay,
Without one pang is glad to fall away.
[VERSE 2]
Unhappy he who latest feels the blow!
Whose eyes have wept o’er every friend laid low,
Dragg’d ling’ring on from partial death to death,
Till, dying, all he can resign is--breath.

--- TITLE: In the Train
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
As we rush, as we rush in the Train,
  The trees and the houses go wheeling back,
But the starry heavens above the plain
  Come flying on our track.
[VERSE 2]
All the beautiful stars of the sky,
  The silver doves of the forest of Night,
Over the dull earth swarm and fly,
  Companions of our flight.
[VERSE 3]
We will rush ever on without fear;
  Let the goal be far, the flight be fleet!
For we carry the Heavens with us, dear,
  While the Earth slips from our feet!

--- TITLE: Sunday up the River
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
My love o’er the water bends dreaming;
  It glideth and glideth away:
She sees there her own beauty, gleaming
  Through shadow and ripple and spray.
[VERSE 2]
O tell her, thou murmuring river,
  As past her your light wavelets roll,
How steadfast that image for ever
  Shines pure in pure depths of my soul.

--- TITLE: Gifts
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
Give a man a horse he can ride,
  Give a man a boat he can sail;
And his rank and wealth, his strength and health,
  On sea nor shore shall fail.
[VERSE 2]
Give a man a pipe he can smoke,
  Give a man a book he can read:
And his home is bright with a calm delight,
  Though the room be poor indeed.
[VERSE 3]
Give a man a girl he can love,
  As I, O my love, love thee;
And his heart is great with the pulse of Fate,
  At home, on land, on sea.

--- TITLE: The Vine
--- SOURCE: PG66619
[VERSE 1]
The wine of Love is music,
  And the feast of Love is song:
And when Love sits down to the banquet,
    Love sits long:
[VERSE 2]
Sits long and arises drunken,
  But not with the feast and the wine;
He reeleth with his own heart,
    That great, rich Vine.
