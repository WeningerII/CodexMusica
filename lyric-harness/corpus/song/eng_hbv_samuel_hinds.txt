--- TITLE: "Baby Sleeps"
--- SOURCE: PG2619
[VERSE 1]
The baby wept;
The mother took it from the nurse's arms,
And hushed its fears, and soothed its vain alarms,
And baby slept.
[VERSE 2]
Again it weeps,
And God doth take it from the mother's arms,
From present griefs, and future unknown harms,
And baby sleeps.
