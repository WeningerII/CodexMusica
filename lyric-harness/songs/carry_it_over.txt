Freight, grey water, low in the hold
Weigh it out and cross the strait
Down in the engine, counting strokes
The oaks on the far side go dark
Past the bell, one low arc
Spark on the black swell, quell it
Carry it. Don't look down.
A name on every crate
Half of it sighed, borne into rust
Tide is cussed, iron, salt, mourn
Nobody told us this was war
Door of the hold. The light goes thin
Rust on the chain. The chain holds.
Count it again from the bell
Low arc, and the light comes back
Everything here has a weight
Carry it. Don't look down.
Salt in the seam and the hold
Nobody drowns on a schedule
Carry it. Don't look down.
Over. Over. The far bank.
After the crossing, we count
Dust on the deck where we stood
And we go back for the rest
The bell again, and we go
