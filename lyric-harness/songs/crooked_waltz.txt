[INTRO1]
Come to the fair, by the farmer's stall, and stir
Come to the square, the charmer, the carpenter

[CHORUS1]
The night is a ring toss, try your lucky bait
Guy lines are singing tonight, and the scales, we tell
The tents of the bonsai man tilt in the wind
Sky wheels are turning, stilt men striding tall
The bright booths lean to the west, doors gliding wide
Pylons at the stiles swing, and the bells count toll
And we all fall in line, dressed up in white

[POSTCHORUS1]
We sat where the heater sighs, on the edge
We set out late; the crowd drifts thin
We suit up in spades, a liter down, the etch
We watch the meter man quit; the dogs fetch

[VERSE1]
A crooked waltz counts to eleven, bows

[CODA1]
Homeward we carry the last of the trade
Home is the fair at half-price, an empty tray
Home down the lane, whistling the crooked tune
