The road had gone cold
and the curtains drawn
and the hinges yawn
to the light in the field
Frost, and the floor swept
and the room was warm
I could not stay dry
and the hour was late
and you came home, arm
Rain, and the long night
the last place you stepped
You are home for good
Snow, and it is time
your pillow, and mine
Stay till it is known
Stay. This is no loan
You are home for good

your pillow, and mine
