Nothing announced itself
You set out two cups
No vow, no ring, no witness
just water and the gas ring lit
just steam, and the same chipped rim
You never called it anything
You only kept the second out
and I drank mine and never asked
Then one week you were gone
and I stood at the counter
and set the second down
my hand ahead of me
already knowing what it knew
The cupboard told me what I was
before I had a word for it
I had been answering for years
in a language I could not hear
You came back Thursday, rain and all
You set out two cups
and I had already poured
The kettle ticked and cooled
It's cold. You knew before I did
Nobody said a word of it
Nobody has. Nobody will.
That is the way I know it holds
