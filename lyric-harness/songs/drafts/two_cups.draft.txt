Nothing announced itself
You set out two cups
No debt, no ring, no witness
Steam on the glass, the gas ring lit
Sweat, dream, and the kettle spit
You never called it anything
You only kept the second set
and I drank mine and never knew
Then one week you were gone
I stood in the kitchen alone
My hand knew why, before the rest
High on the shelf, the second cup
Dry, and I already knew
The board above the sink was bare
before I stored it anywhere
I had been counting to ten
It was my language, never shown
You came back Thursday, rain and all
You set out two cups
and I had already poured
The kettle ticked and cooled
It's cold against the metal
Nobody said it, nobody quit
There was no sign, my whole life
You are it, my one sure thing
