Late this winter, one year on, back in harbour
That grey light, windy, and the lamp on the pier
Boats back, salt drying on the skin, the catch to weigh
Day after day the window, and nothing farther
The sea goes flat and silver and the gulls fall still
By dusk the lamps touch water, and they point us home
Nothing in the shops we'd buy, and nothing to send
I won't say that any part of this is easy
Bye, I say, to the ferry as it clears the wall
And still the water carries the boats homeward
So I leave the porch light on and the door unlocked
