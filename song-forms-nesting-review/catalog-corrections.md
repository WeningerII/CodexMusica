# Sixteen catalog corrections

The previous review at ee47d0c labeled these entries contested. Fifteen diagrams now give the supported rule, variant or bounded setting. One retains a specific unresolved point. Source locators and scoped evidence are in comparison/claim-source-ledger.tsv. Historical inputs retain their original wording.

| Entry | Judgment | Corrected reading |
|---|---|---|
| Binary | supported | Two reprises form A and B. Each reprise is commonly repeated, giving AABB; repeats are optional. In rounded binary, opening material returns within B. |
| Head arrangement | supported | The players devise and remember the arrangement together. A performance may use head, solos and returning head; that sequence describes one way of playing the arrangement. |
| Long-measure ballad | supported | The illustrated narrative quatrain has four stresses in every line. AABB is one rhyme arrangement. Dividing a regular fourteener into four and three stresses yields a different ballad-meter profile. |
| Canon | supported | One voice leads and another enters later in imitation. A strict canon preserves the leader’s material according to its rule; a free canon permits adjustments. A round cycles, while other canons may have a written close. |

| Sonnet sequence | supported | A series of sonnets develops a subject or progression. Some sequences use verbal linkage: in a crown, each closing line opens the next sonnet and the cycle returns to its beginning. |
| Rondeau prime | supported | The documented twelve-position setting divides into a septet and a quintain, abbaabR / abbaR. The returning opening fragment occupies positions seven and twelve. Historical naming can depend on the particular refrain treatment. |
