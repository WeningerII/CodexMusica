# Table B review dispositions

The review covers the 89 folk, participatory and sacred song entries, their diagrams and their outgoing links. Each supported link carries its own evidence. An empty link reference list identifies a judgment based on the catalog. An entry’s attested references support particular observations in its note or diagram. Other retained source links are contextual.

| T1 and P13, long-measure ballad | The diagram shows the inventory’s four-stress AABB quatrain as an example. Its fourteener alternative divides into four and three beats, so containment of a Fourteener was rejected. The links to common metre and ballad stanza explain that alternative. The link to long metre is conditional on an iambic reading. [Toronto glossary](https://rpo.library.utoronto.ca/glossary/common-measure), [hymn-metre handbook](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/54966/pg54966-images.html). |
