## Open distinctions

- Generic Sonnet is absent as a target for Duplex and for the explicitly named sonnets in Caudate sonnet, Crown of sonnets, Heroic crown and Sonnet sequence. Importing the name of a specific sonnet would confuse the general frame with a subtype.
- The catalog has no general Envoi or Refrain form target. Where a particular envoi has a known stanza type, the link names that unit.
- The two titles Rondeau prime and Rondine share one construction. The exact scope of interchangeability across their other recorded readings remains open.
- A bayt’s English presentation depends on the verse tradition, edition and translation. The conditional couplet question for Qasida remains open.
- A glosa may use a more specifically patterned ten-line unit, but the general description alone supplies neither the Dizain pattern nor the Décima espinela pattern.
- The 5–7 range for the general Canzone row comes from the input. Evidence for its stricter subtypes is independent of that range.
- Existing reciprocal parent-to-child variant records are normalized by the package-wide T4 pass. The additions here run from the specialized form to its parent.
