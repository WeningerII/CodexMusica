## Survey reconciliation

The [crosswalk](comparison/structure-crosswalk.md) and [row-level data](comparison/structure-crosswalk.json) retain all 623 occurrences.

| Findings | Decision |
|---|---|
| P17–19 | The Rondeau family already covers multiple refrain lengths. A 13-line layout is a variant. Compound AABA can be a hierarchical reading of Verse–chorus–bridge. Ballata and Medieval carol with burden cover the Virelai row’s text-level refrain recurrence; use of an English text as a sung virelai remains unverified. Neither comparison demands a surviving score to establish recurrence in the text. |
| P20–21 | Recurring instrumental ritornello material around English vocal episodes is directly attested. Sequence qualifies through an official English text used in the liturgy. An illustrative translation alone does not establish use in the tradition. [Ritornello guide](https://pressbooks.cuny.edu/understandingmusicbmcc/?p=716) · [Liturgical text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/041628.cfm) |
| P22 | Head–solos–head is diagrammed already. Arranging by memory is a separate concept. A hook-first intro establishes an opening gesture, which is chorus-first only when the hook is itself the chorus. |
| P26–27 | Glossary comparisons have their own denominator. The survey’s three supported gaps cannot count glossary genres, occasions or compositions. The glossary reviewer separately reassessed the affected headwords against comparable existing entries and removed obsolete alias rationales. |
| P28–29 | Distinguish the context of tag, shout, lining out and ritornello. Subtractive form in survey row 58 is removal of textural layers. Countdown song supplies the distinct textual decrement. The comparison for additive form similarly points to Cumulative song only in its lyric sense. |

Counts are 15 present, 23 alias/variant, 535 other-layer, 47 unclear and three supported gaps. Rows 19, 31 and 305 identify recurring ritornello material, terminally climactic form and English liturgical Sequence respectively. The wider team reconciled these decisions with its meta-form, song-gap and poetry comparisons.
