## Validation

- **Historical manual record, without bundled reconstruction code or inputs.** Reconstructed all three source matrices with their row defaults, applied the six-row v2 delta and then v3, and joined the fixed snapshot’s D/E/S/A/R/X/I/N vector by original ID. All **1,624 comparisons** matched the package for M001–M203 (name, group, attestation kind and status, representation, declared check, recognition and the eight-field vector). Later source mentions and catalog links do not backfill frozen statuses.
- Verified 87 IDs with associations, 116 without and 105 unique ID/title pairs. Each points to the exact source title and an actual catalog line. Every record has a nonempty scope, unit, strength, type and evidence locator. The normalized pairs round-trip to the ID table; six other catalog Welsh forms remain explicitly catalog-only.
- **Historical manual record, without the original input vectors or executable script.** Used the unmodified pinned Python comparator in isolation for **12 test cases and three additional assertions**. Outcomes cover successful and failed positional matches, preserved secondary stress, a partial reading, four- and five-foot initial inversion, extra and missing syllables, unsupported symbols/policy and CLI-entry stream selection. These are synthetic checks of this interface. No production verdict or whole-poem recognition was inferred.
