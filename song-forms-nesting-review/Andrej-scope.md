# Scope of the Song Forms review

Historical review at ee47d0c. See README.md for current figures and partial-entries.md and catalog-corrections.md for subsequent dispositions.

**Criterion.** A diagram is supported when it represents the stated organizing rule or explicitly bounded construction, partial when the note can name a material missing branch, position, rule or unresolved identification, and contested only when two traceable accounts make incompatible claims about the same feature at the same scope. Reserve unresolved for an entry whose construction cannot be determined. Evidence provenance is a separate field. A difference of language, an allowed variant, or an unspecified optional detail does not by itself make an entry contested.

The review read the inventory text, all six model files, all six audit files and each form’s complete tree and entry note. The overlay contains one record per exact original title with only `status` and `note`. Group C is stored as C1 and C2. The status belongs to the entry, independently of the verdicts on its individual relationship edges.

Every partial note identifies what is outside the diagram or what the material leaves undecided. Examples include the undrawn CDCDCD sestets in both the Petrarchan and Sicilian sonnets, the absent four-stanza Bop branch and the undrawn six-stanza Pearl grouping. The four previously catalog-only “sources differ” cases were separated from evidence disputes. Mummers’ play verse shows only the hero-combat plot, Square-dance singing call one layout among possible calls, Irish syllabic quatrains a non-exhaustive bundle, and Pearl stanza a typical five-stanza grouping. None of those four now implies that two external sources were verified. All contested notes state which feature is incompatible; merely accepting multiple working variants does not count as a dispute.

Entry notes no longer credit an external authority under catalog-only evidence for Acrostic, Epic, Aisling, Rhopalic verse, Metrical charm, Reverso, Field holler and Tumbling verse. Simple verse-chorus now states the shared harmony directly. The relationship ledger carries separate evidence treatment.

## The 47 survey decisions

All 47 former `attestation-unclear` rows have an individual decision and a term-specific reason in [the survey table](comparison/structure-crosswalk.json). The previous status and method remain in each record. Nineteen named traditions or musical designs are retained as comparison candidates, without asserting an English-text occurrence or adding a catalog form. Twenty-five occurrences describe other layers. One is an alias of another survey candidate. Two are deliberately excluded because the intended referent is unidentified (Jiuhua and Rebate). The eleven identical layer-screen reasons are gone. Structural modifiers, such as an ordinal second drop and a key-changed chorus, each receive their own explanation.

The 623 decisions now comprise 15 present, 24 alias/variant, 560 other-layer, 19 candidate gaps, 2 unidentified and 3 gaps already supported for English use. There are 237 individually assessed rows and 386 layer screens. Survey row 76 concerns the praise process. The separately documented English Oríkì translation supports reading in English and does not establish a sung performance in English.

## Evidence for the 16 contested entries

The inventory gives one side for all rows except the two independent accounts of the Monk’s Tale. The source column identifies the actual limit of the second account. The canzone lengths themselves agree with their inspected sources; the attribution of one historical example and the full end-word positions remain separately qualified.
