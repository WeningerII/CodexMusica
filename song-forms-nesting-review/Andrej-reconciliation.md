### Other comments

- **Build, counts, cache and encoding.** Active scripts resolve their local imports in isolated mode, use UTF-8 and suppress bytecode on child rebuilds. verify-offline.py checks exact headline passages, recomputes the figures independently, deletes and reproduces generated files in a temporary copy, and checks the complete file set. Andrej-build.md documents the command and its boundaries.
- **Unshipped checks.** The prior 1,624-field and 12-plus-three meter results are explicitly historical manual records. The new cross-version checks have separate replay instructions and pinned source identities. See Andrej-meter.md.
- **Edge URLs.** Every referenced edge’s citations are included in its form’s review bibliography by construction. Each relationship still derives its own evidence status separately.
- **Search.** Exact headings, alternate names and discovery terms take precedence over incidental text matches. All 534 exact terms kept their candidate sets. Andrej-search.md records the before/after results for all six queries, including genuine ambiguity.
- **Meter units.** The normalized meter table separates the measured unit, its position and the strength of the claim for every pair. The raw research names, witness names, source descriptions and editor names stay in review-only data. They do not enter browser search or generation instructions.
- **Framing and baseline.** The Simple verse-chorus entry uses a structural description. The preserved original explorer and its unsanitized catalog are clearly marked as provenance only. The current explorer publishes reviewed fields.
- **Archived decisions and broader classes.** The source label on a rejected, ambiguous or redirected row describes its decision basis. Only supported facts are navigable. Wider class memberships inferred solely through a narrower component are distinguished from directly shown components and independent facts. See the graph dispositions and unit crosswalk.
- **Unresolved survey.** Each previously unresolved row receives an individual disposition with a basis and a boundary. A candidate or a term with an unknown identity stays open where the evidence is insufficient. The survey dispositions carry no authority to add forms or alter the production planner.
- **Refuted and outside scope.** Catalog-only observations, legitimate names in reference URLs, and the previously bounded haibun, qasida and head-arrangement cases remain permissible in the audit. The reviewer's earlier tests and sample of sources concern the incoming revision. The two proposed repository fixes to the named scheme and stale structure-canon entries belong to their repository owners. This review changes only the portable review package.
